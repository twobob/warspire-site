Play in browser How it plays Wiki

Three moves.
Every round.

No twitch reflexes, no action bar. You win in the thirty seconds before the gates open.

1

Deploy

Buy from a shop every player shares. Three of a kind fuse into a stronger unit, so every purchase is also a denial.

2

Conquer

Place your warband on the board and let go. Alliances trigger, abilities fire, and the round is settled without another tap.

3

Ascend

Spend gold on levels, loot and talents. Outlast seven rivals and the top of the spire is yours.

Pick your
commander

Your commander stands on the board with your warband, with two builds apiece and talents chosen as the match unfolds.

    The warband

    Every unit in the shop, from gutter scavengers to the legends at the top of the spire.

    Bind alliances

    Each unit carries two or three allegiances. Field enough of one and the whole warband changes shape.

    Arenas

    The spire is more than one biome. Matches can be fought across different battlegrounds, each with its own light, stone and weather.
