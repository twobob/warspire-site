Spire Acolyte is a tier 1 (Common) caster unit. It costs 1 gold in the shop and carries the Spireborn, Arcanist and Triad alliances.

Ability

Frost Sigil

Brands the target with a freezing sigil, dealing magical damage and rooting it in place.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.4 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target.
  • Deals 100 / 150 / 200 magical damage to the target (values per star).
  • Applies Rooted (units) (cannot move) to the target for 2 / 2.5 / 3 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Frost Sigil ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Spireborn race

The last citizens of the spire draw on its fading wards. All allies generate mana every second.

Arcanist class

Scholars of the broken wards. All enemies lose magic resistance, so they take more magical damage.

Units neededEffect
3All enemies: -35% magic resistance.
Enemies summoned later in the fight get this too.
6All enemies: -70% magic resistance.

Triad race

Three elemental siblings who fight as one. With three Triad units, a spell charges its caster, and a charged Triad slams the ground around its target, dealing magical damage and stunning (at most once every 6 seconds each).

Units neededEffect
3When a Triad unit casts an ability (at most once every 6 seconds), it deals 130 / 195 / 260 magical damage to the enemy its ability was cast on (its current target if there is none) and all enemies within 1 cell of it and applies Slammed (cannot move, attack or cast) to the same targets for 1.5 / 2.25 / 3 seconds (values per star).

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
