Glossary

Every term used in the game and this wiki.

112 terms used in Warspire and on this wiki, in alphabetical order. Each entry ends with the page that covers it in full.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ

A

Ability
The special action of a unit or commander. A unit casts its ability when its mana is full; commander abilities run on a cooldown instead. See Mana and casting.
Ace
A tier 5 unit with an extra effect that switches on while its named alliance is active. 8 units are aces, and an ace whose alliance you have active is 15% more likely to be picked when the shop rolls its tier. See Aces.
Alliance
A group that units belong to. Field enough different units of an alliance and it grants a bonus at each alliance step. There are 33 alliances. See How alliances count.
Alliance step
One threshold of an alliance, such as 2, 4 or 6 different units. Only the highest step you have reached applies; steps do not add together. See How alliances count.
Armour
Reduces physical damage. 10 armour removes 37.7% of each physical hit, and negative armour makes hits stronger. See Damage, armour and resistance.
Assassin (role)
A unit role carried by 9 units, such as Oath Cutter, Hive Skulker and Riftlance Duelist. All are melee. One-star health is 550 to 825 at tier 1 and 1,500 at tier 5. See Unit stat table.
Attack damage
The damage of a unit's basic attack, rolled between its lowest and highest value on every hit and dealt as physical damage. See Stat reference.
Attack point
The wind-up of an attack: the time between starting the swing and the hit landing or the projectile leaving. Attack speed shortens it. See Combat.
Attack range
How many cells away a unit can attack. Diagonal cells count as one step, and a range of 1 is melee. See Combat.
Attack rate
The seconds between one attack and the next before any attack speed bonus. A lower number is a faster unit. See Stat reference.
Attack speed
A percentage bonus that divides the time between attacks (the attack rate), so +100 attack speed attacks twice as often. See Stat reference.
Auto deploy
A setting, on by default, that moves bench units onto the board when the fight starts, strongest first, until you reach your unit cap. See Board and bench.

B

Bench
The 8 slots that hold units you own but are not fielding. Bench units do not fight and do not count toward an alliance. See Board and bench.
Board
Your half of the battlefield: 8 cells wide and 4 deep, 32 cells in all. Units placed here fight. See Board and bench.
Bot
A computer player. Bots fill the empty seats so that every match has 8 players, and each follows one of 4 drafting styles. See Bots.
Bruiser (role)
A unit role carried by 13 units, such as Gutter Scavenger, Thornback Boar and Saw Rigger. All are melee. One-star health is 1,100 to 1,150 at tier 1 and 1,500 at tier 5. See Unit stat table.
Buff
A status effect that helps the unit holding it, such as extra attack speed or armour. See How status effects work.
Build
A commander's loadout. Each commander has 2 builds; a build decides its second ability and the talents it is offered. See How commanders work.

C

Cast point
The delay between a unit starting to cast and the ability taking effect. Mana is spent when the cast point completes, and a stun or silence before then cancels the cast. See Mana and casting.
Caster (role)
A unit role carried by 16 units, such as Spire Acolyte, Pit Imp and Rift Mote. All are ranged. One-star health is 700 to 900 at tier 1 and 1,350 to 1,400 at tier 5. See Unit stat table.
Cell
One square of the board. Ranges and areas are measured in cells. See Board and bench.
Class (alliance kind)
One of the 2 kinds of alliance. 17 alliances are of this kind, such as Warden, Bulwark and Cutthroat. Both kinds count in the same way. See Alliances.
Combat
The phase in which boards fight on their own. A fight lasts at most 45 seconds (the time limit). See Combat.
Combine
3 copies of the same unit at the same star merge into one unit of the next star. It happens by itself as soon as you own them. See Star upgrades.
Commander
A hero that joins your board. In the preparation of round 10 you choose one of 4; it cannot be sold or benched and does not count toward your unit cap. See How commanders work.
Cooldown
The wait between two casts of a commander ability. Commander abilities need no mana, and their cooldowns keep running while the commander is stunned. See How commanders work.
Copy
One purchase of a unit. A one-star unit is 1 copy, a two-star unit holds 3 and a three-star unit holds 9. Copies come out of the pool. See The shared pool.
Critical strike
An attack that deals multiplied damage. Items and effects grant the chance and the multiplier. See Stat reference.

D

Damage over time
A debuff that deals its damage in small steps, twice a second, while it lasts, such as poison or burning. See How status effects work.
Damage reduction
A percentage taken off physical and magical damage after armour or magic resistance. Pure damage ignores it. See Damage, armour and resistance.
Debuff
A status effect that harms the unit holding it: a disable, damage over time or a lowered stat. A dispel from an ally removes it. See How status effects work.
Defensive (item kind)
29 items are defensive, such as Riveted Mail, Heartwood Charm and Warding Cowl. They keep a unit alive. See Item table.
Disable
A debuff that stops a unit doing something: stun, silence, root and disarm. See How status effects work.
Disarm
A disable. A disarmed unit cannot make basic attacks, but still moves and casts. Status effects that carry it include Disarmed (units), Last Light and Hexed. See How status effects work.
Dispel
An effect that strips timed status effects. Cast on an ally it removes every debuff, each disable included; cast on an enemy it removes every buff. See How status effects work.
Draw
A fight in which both sides are wiped out together. Nobody takes damage, nobody earns victory gold and a streak stays as it was. See Player damage and elimination.

E

Elimination
A player whose health reaches zero is out of the match and all their units return to the pool. Players knocked out in the same round are ranked by health, lowest last. See Player damage and elimination.
Evasion
The chance for a unit to dodge an attack completely. Several sources of evasion combine with diminishing returns. See Stat reference.
Experience
Fills the bar toward your next level. You gain 1 each round, and can buy 5 for 5 gold. See Levels and experience.

F

Free reroll
Losing a round gives you one reroll at no cost. It is kept until you use it, and you never hold more than one. See The shop.

G

Ghost
A copy of another living player's board. When an odd number of players remain, one of them fights a ghost in that matchup; the result counts for that player only, and the player who was copied is not affected. See Matchups.
Global item
An item that works for your whole warband as soon as you take it. It is never equipped on a unit and does not use inventory space. 20 items are global, such as Oathbound Insignia, Recruiter's Ledger and Venomist Sigil. See Loot and items.
Gold
The currency for units, rerolls and experience. You start with 5 and can hold no more than the gold cap. See Gold and income.
Gold cap
You can hold at most 100 gold. Income above the cap is lost. See Gold and income.

H

Heal over time
A buff that restores health in small steps, twice a second, while it lasts. See How status effects work.
Health
For a unit, the damage it can take before it dies. For a player, the 100 points you start with and lose when you are beaten; at zero comes elimination. See Player damage and elimination.

I

Interest
Extra income for saved gold: 1 gold for every 10 you hold when the fight starts, up to 3 a round. See Gold and income.
Inventory
Where an unequipped item waits. It holds 12 items. See Loot and items.
Item
Equipment won in a loot round. A unit carries one item; there are 109 items in 5 tiers. A global item is not carried by anyone. See Loot and items.

L

Level
Your player level, from 1 to 10, raised with experience. It sets your unit cap and your shop odds. See Levels and experience.
Lifesteal
Heals the attacker for a percentage of the damage its attacks deal. See Stat reference.
Lobby
The waiting room before a match. The match starts when all 8 seats hold players or the wait runs out, and a bot takes each empty seat. See Matchmaking and lobbies.
Lock
A switch on the shop that keeps its units through the end of the round. It stays on until you turn it off. See The shop.
Loot round
A round whose preparation offers a choice of 3 items, with 1 reroll of the offer. Loot rounds are rounds 1, 4, then round 8 and every 4 rounds after it. See Loot and items.
Lose streak
Rounds lost in a row. 3 or 4 in a row pays 1 gold and 5 or more pays 2. Only a loss in which you took damage lengthens it. See Gold and income.

M

Magic resistance
Reduces magical damage by its percentage: 25 magic resistance removes a quarter of each magical hit. See Damage, armour and resistance.
Magical damage
One of the 3 damage types. It is reduced by the target's magic resistance. See Damage, armour and resistance.
Mana
The bar that fuels an ability. A unit gains mana from each attack that lands (1 for every 7.5 of the attack's damage before armour, at most 10 a hit) and from every hit it takes (1 for every 10 damage after armour or magic resistance, at most 10 a hit), and casts when the bar is full. An attack that misses or is dodged gives nothing, and neither does the burn of overtime. See Mana and casting.
Mana regeneration
Mana gained every second from an item or effect, on top of the mana from attacking and being hit. See Mana and casting.
Matchup
The pairing of two players for a round. Pairs are drawn at random, and an opponent you met in the last 2 rounds is much less likely to come up again. With an odd number of players one of them meets a ghost. See Matchups.
Melee
A unit with an attack range of 1. It must stand next to its target. See Combat.
Move speed
How fast a unit walks from cell to cell. See Stat reference.

N

Neutral wave
A round fought against neutral creatures in place of another player. The current rules have neutral waves switched off, so every round is against a player or a ghost. See Rounds and phases.

O

Offensive (item kind)
32 items are offensive, such as Hunter's Edge, Ironwood Greatblade and Quickstep Gauntlets. They raise the damage a unit deals. See Item table.
Overtime
From 35 seconds into a fight a new step begins every second. The bonus to all damage grows by 15% each step, up to 150%; the cut to all healing grows by 10% each step, up to 80%; and at each step every unit takes a burn of pure damage, a share of its maximum health that grows by 0.5% each step, up to 5%. The damage bonus strengthens the burn too. The fight still ends at the time limit. See Time limit and overtime.

P

Physical damage
One of the 3 damage types. It is reduced by the target's armour. Basic attacks deal physical damage. See Damage, armour and resistance.
Placeable
An object that an item plants beside its wearer at the start of combat. Placeables never move or attack, do not keep a side alive and deal no damage to the losing player. There are 5: Spiked Palisade, Straw Decoy, Sunfruit Tree, Mending Totem and Restless Gravestone. See Summons and placeables.
Placement
Your final rank in the match, first to last, set by the order of elimination. See After the match.
Pool
The shared stock of unit copies that every shop takes its units from: 30 of each tier 1 unit, 20 of each tier 2 unit, 18 of each tier 3 unit, 12 of each tier 4 unit and 10 of each tier 5 unit. See The shared pool.
Power level
A unit's tier plus 2 for each star above the first. It decides how much damage a surviving unit deals to the losing player. See Player damage and elimination.
Preparation
The phase for buying, placing and equipping. It lasts 30 seconds, except 15 seconds on rounds 1, 2 and 3, plus 10 seconds in a loot round. Combat follows it. See Rounds and phases.
Projectile
The shot of a ranged attack. It takes time to fly, and misses if its target dies or becomes untargetable first. See Combat.
Pure damage
One of the 3 damage types. Nothing reduces it: not armour, magic resistance or damage reduction. See Damage, armour and resistance.

R

Race (alliance kind)
One of the 2 kinds of alliance. 16 alliances are of this kind, such as Feral, Abyssal and Ashen. Both kinds count in the same way. See Alliances.
Ranged
A unit with an attack range of 2 or more. Its attacks are a projectile. See Combat.
Ranged (role)
A unit role carried by 11 units, such as Grave Archer, Gargoyle Gunner and Mire Skirmisher. All are ranged. One-star health is 600 to 950 at tier 1 and 1,275 to 1,500 at tier 5. The role is the label for ranged units that fight mainly with their attacks; all casters and some supports also attack from range. Commanders with this role: Pip Quickfingers and Fizzwick Emberhorn. See Unit stat table.
Ready
The button that says you have finished preparing. Combat starts early once every connected player is ready. See Rounds and phases.
Reroll
Replaces all 5 shop units with a fresh roll for 2 gold. A lost round can earn a free reroll. See The shop.
Revive
An effect that brings a unit back in place, once per fight, with part of its health and its timed debuffs removed. See How status effects work.
Role
A label on each unit that says how it tends to fight. Roles are: assassin, bruiser, caster, placeable, ranged, summon, support and tank. See Units.
Root
A disable. A rooted unit cannot move, but still attacks and casts at whatever is in reach. Status effects that carry it include Rooted (units). See How status effects work.
Round
One preparation followed by one fight. A match that reaches round 60 ends there: the players still alive are ranked by health, and the one with the most wins. See Rounds and phases.
Round gold
The base income of every round: 7 gold, except 5 gold on rounds 1 and 2. See Gold and income.

S

Sell
Selling a unit returns the price of one copy, plus 2 gold if it is two-star or 4 gold if it is three-star, and puts every copy it holds back in the pool. See Star upgrades.
Shield
A layer of extra health from an ability or item. It absorbs damage, after armour and magic resistance, before the unit's own health. Examples are Ember Vow and Resonance Mantle. See How status effects work.
Shop
The 5 units offered to you for gold. It rolls anew after every round and whenever you reroll, by your shop odds. See The shop.
Shop odds
The chance of each tier for every shop slot, set by your level. Higher tiers open up as you level: tier 3 first appears at level 3, tier 4 first appears at level 6 and tier 5 first appears at level 8. See The shop.
Silence
A disable. A silenced unit cannot cast, and a cast it had started is cancelled, but it still moves and attacks. Status effects that carry it include Silenced (units), Pact Silence and Static Shock. See How status effects work.
Stack
Some status effects build up: each new application adds a stack, up to a limit, and every stack adds its bonus again. See How status effects work.
Star
A unit's upgrade rank, from 1 to 3, raised when copies combine. Higher stars have more health and damage and stronger abilities. See Star upgrades.
Status effect
A named buff or debuff on a unit, usually for a limited time. The wiki lists 100; see also status effect class. See Status effects.
Status effect class
Every named status effect is either a buff or a debuff. Of those listed, 51 are buffs and 49 are debuffs; 15 of the debuffs are disables. The class decides what a dispel removes: cast on an ally it removes every timed debuff, disables included, and cast on an enemy it removes timed buffs. An effect that carries a disable is a debuff even when it also protects its holder, such as Last Light and Swept Aloft. See How status effects work.
Streak
A run of wins or of losses in a row: a win streak or a lose streak. Both pay extra gold each round. See Gold and income.
Stun
A disable. A stunned unit cannot move, attack or cast, and an attack or cast it had started is cancelled. Status effects that carry it include Stunned (general), Slammed and Swept Aloft. See How status effects work.
Summon
A unit created during a fight by an ability, alliance, item or commander. Summons cannot be bought, do not count toward alliances and deal no damage to the losing player. 18 units are summons, such as Rift Echo, Greater Rift Echo and Thorn Hound. See Summons and placeables.
Support (item kind)
28 items are support, such as Cinder Core, Wellspring Brooch and Stalwart Pennant. They help with mana, healing and allies. See Item table.
Support (role)
A unit role carried by 5 units, such as Bog Hexer, Thornwood Seer and Oathkeeper Thane. 2 are melee and 3 ranged. One-star health is 800 to 975 at tier 1 and 1,300 at tier 4. Commanders with this role: Vey the Pactbinder. See Unit stat table.
Survive a killing blow
The first killing blow the unit takes in a fight leaves it at 1 health instead. It works once per fight, and a hit on a unit already at 1 health still kills it. Status effects that carry it include Grave-Cloth. See How status effects work.
Survivor
A unit still alive when a fight ends. The winner's survivors decide how much damage the loser takes, each by its power level. See Player damage and elimination.

T

Talent
A permanent upgrade for your commander. At player levels 4, 6, 8 and 10 you choose one of the talents your build offers. See Talents.
Tank (role)
A unit role carried by 11 units, such as Drowned Pikeman, Ashen Pilgrim and Chainwarden. All are melee. One-star health is 900 to 1,000 at tier 1 and 1,400 to 3,000 at tier 5. Commanders with this role: Brugg Ironjaw. See Unit stat table.
Target
The enemy a unit is attacking. Units pick the closest enemy they can reach, prefer the one with less health when distances are equal, and keep a target while it can still be reached. See Triggers and targeting.
Tick
The step of the combat clock: 0.1 seconds. See Combat.
Tier
The rarity of a unit or item, from 1 to 5. A unit costs 1 gold at tier 1, 2 gold at tier 2, 3 gold at tier 3, 4 gold at tier 4 and 5 gold at tier 5. See Units.
Time limit
A fight that reaches 45 seconds ends at once. Both players count as having lost and each takes damage from the other's survivors. Overtime sets in before then. See Time limit and overtime.
Trigger
A rule that fires an effect when something happens, such as landing an attack, casting or dying. See Triggers and targeting.

U

Unit
A fighter bought from the shop. There are 65 shop units: 14 at tier 1, 14 at tier 2, 14 at tier 3, 13 at tier 4 and 10 at tier 5. See Units.
Unit cap
The number of units you may field: your level, and never fewer than 3. Units over the cap when the fight starts go back to the bench, starting with the ones you touched most recently. See Board and bench.
Untargetable
An untargetable unit cannot be chosen as a target by enemies, and shots already in flight miss it. Status effects that carry it include Skittering, Last Light and Swept Aloft. See How status effects work.

V

Victory gold
1 gold for winning a round. See Gold and income.

W

Warband
All the units you field, with your commander and any summons. See Board and bench.
Win streak
Rounds won in a row. 3 or 4 in a row pays 1 gold, 5 or 6 pays 2, 7 pays 3 and 8 pays 4; after the payment for win 8 the streak starts again. See Gold and income.
