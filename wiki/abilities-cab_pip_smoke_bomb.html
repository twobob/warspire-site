Smoke Bomb is an extra ability of Pip Quickfingers. No build starts with it: it is added by the talent Smoke Bomb.

Description

Pip drops a smoke bomb: adjacent enemies are stunned for 1 second and Pip cannot be targeted for 1 second.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 8 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 8.2 seconds apart. The first cast starts 4 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
0.2 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies within 1 cell of the commander.
  • Applies Stunned (general) (cannot move, attack or cast) to the targets for 1 second.
  • Applies Smoke Cloud (cannot be targeted by enemies) to the commander for 1 second.

By player level

Nothing about this ability changes with player level: it is the same from the day the commander arrives.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 8.2 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 4.2 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 5 casts at most.
  • The commander does not wait for anything to be in reach. The cast goes off as soon as it is ready and affects whatever is in its area at that moment, which can be nothing. The part that affects the commander still happens, and the cooldown is spent either way.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Applying it again

  • Stunned (general): it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
  • Smoke Cloud: it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
