Static Bloom

The ability of Rift Mote: deals magical damage. It targets all enemies within 2 cells of itself.

In game: Releases a crackling bloom of rift energy that damages all enemies within 2 cells.

Static Bloom is the ability of Rift Mote.

What it does

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies within 2 cells of itself.
  • Deals 120 / 180 / 250 magical damage to the targets (values per star).

How the cast works

  • Rift Mote needs 100 mana to cast Static Bloom. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It affects every enemy within 2 cells of the caster. A diagonal step counts as one cell. Enemies that cannot be targeted are never picked. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
