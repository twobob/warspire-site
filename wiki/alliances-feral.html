Alliance

Feral

A race alliance of 6 units with steps at 2, 4 and 6.

Feral
Kind
Race
Units
6
Steps
2 / 4 / 6

Feral beasts of the ash wastes grow stronger with every blow. Affected units gain attack damage each time they land an attack, for the rest of the combat.

Feral is a race alliance carried by 6 units. It has 3 steps, reached with 2, 4 and 6 different Feral units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2When a Feral unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 2 units) (+5 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
4When a Feral unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 4 units) (+8 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
6When an ally lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 6 units) (+13 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

There are exactly 6 different Feral units, and the top step needs 6, so the top step needs every one of them on your board at the same time.

Status effects

The steps of this alliance apply these status effects. Each has its own page, and How status effects work explains how they stack and expire.

  • Feral Fury: each step applies its own version, with 2 units, with 4 units and with 6 units.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
