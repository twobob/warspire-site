Bearclaw Wilder is a tier 4 (Epic) ranged unit. It costs 4 gold in the shop and carries the Feral, Thornkin and Conjurer alliances.

Ability

Call the Great Bear

Summons a great spire bear to fight beside it.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
  • Summons a Spire Bear at its own star level in the nearest free cell.

Full details are on the Call the Great Bear ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Feral race

Feral beasts of the ash wastes grow stronger with every blow. Affected units gain attack damage each time they land an attack, for the rest of the combat.

Units neededEffect
2When a Feral unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 2 units) (+5 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
4When a Feral unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 4 units) (+8 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
6When an ally lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 6 units) (+13 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Thornkin race

Druids of the overgrown lower terraces. At the start of combat a random Thornkin calls a beast to fight beside it; higher levels call a stronger beast.

Units neededEffect
2One random Thornkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat, the chosen unit summons a Thorn Hound in the nearest free cell.
4One random Thornkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat, the chosen unit summons a Carrion Wing in the nearest free cell.
6One random Thornkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat, the chosen unit summons an Elder Thornwyrm in the nearest free cell.

Conjurer class

Callers of things from beyond the spire. Every summoned ally deals 20% more damage (40% with four Conjurers). Conjurers share the bonus.

Units neededEffect
2Conjurer units and the summoned units that carry the Conjurer mark: +20% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
4Conjurer units and the summoned units that carry the Conjurer mark: +40% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
