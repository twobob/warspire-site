Items

Every item by tier and kind.

Warspire has 109 items across 5 tiers. Items are not bought. They are won on item rounds: you are shown 3 items and keep one, with 1 reroll of the offer. The inventory holds 12 unequipped items. Loot and items has the full rules.

Tiers

TierNameItemsOffered on
Tier 1Common18Rounds 1 and 4
Tier 2Uncommon29Rounds 8 and 12
Tier 3Rare25Rounds 16 and 20
Tier 4Epic19Rounds 24 and 28
Tier 5Legendary18Round 32 and every 4 rounds after that

The round decides the tier of an offer. If every item of that tier is one you already own the limit of, the offer falls back to the nearest lower tier, then to a higher one.

Kinds

  • Offensive (32): Offensive items make their holder hit harder or faster.
  • Defensive (29): Defensive items keep their holder, or the units around it, alive.
  • Support (28): Support items feed mana, help allies or change how the holder fights.
  • Global (20): Global items are held by you, not by a unit, and work for the whole warband.

An offensive, defensive or support item is equipped on one unit, one item per unit. A global item is never equipped: it works for you as soon as you pick it.

5 items place a structure on the board at the start of combat: Palisade Stakes, Straw Decoy, Sunfruit Sapling, Mending Totem, Restless Gravestone. See Summons and placeables.
