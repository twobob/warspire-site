Rivet Burst

The ability of Gutter Scavenger: deals magical damage and stuns. It targets all enemies within 1 cell of itself.

In game: Detonates a pouch of rivets, dealing magical damage to adjacent enemies and briefly stunning them.

Rivet Burst is the ability of Gutter Scavenger.

How the cast works

  • Gutter Scavenger needs 100 mana to cast Rivet Burst. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It affects every enemy within 1 cell of the caster. A diagonal step counts as one cell. Enemies that cannot be targeted are never picked. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
