Greater Rift Echo is a summoned unit. It is never sold in the shop: it appears during combat and is gone when the fight ends. See Summons and placeables. It carries the Conjurer alliance.

Ability

Greater Rift Echo has no ability and no mana bar. It only attacks.

Seconds per attack is the time from the start of one attack to the start of the next, before any attack speed bonus. Average damage per second is the middle of the damage range divided by the seconds per attack, before armour, bonuses and critical strikes.

Its health and damage are the same at every star level.

Where it comes from

Greater Rift Echo is never offered in the shop. It is created during combat by an alliance. It appears in the free cell nearest to whatever created it. See Summons and placeables for how summoned and placed units behave.

  • Alliance Riftborn
    • At the 4 unit step, each Riftborn unit leaves 1 behind at its own star level when it dies.
    • Only the highest step you have reached is in force: a higher step replaces a lower one.

Alliances

Greater Rift Echo is a summoned unit. Summoned units never count towards an alliance step, but a summon that carries an alliance receives the bonuses that alliance gives to its members. See How alliances count.

Conjurer class

Callers of things from beyond the spire. Every summoned ally deals 20% more damage (40% with four Conjurers). Conjurers share the bonus.
