Sharp Darts is a talent of Pip Quickfingers, offered at player level 8 to the Pickpocket and Brawler builds.

Description

Pip deals 25% more damage.

What it changes

The commander: +25% attack damage.

  • This raises attack damage only. Damage from abilities and damage over time are not increased.
  • The bonus is put on at the start of each fight and lasts the whole fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

The choice

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Sharp Darts means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
