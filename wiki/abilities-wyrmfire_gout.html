Wyrmfire Gout

The ability of Cinder Wyrmrider: deals magical damage and weakens enemies. It targets every enemy in a line from it through its current target, 3 cells long.

In game: Spews wyrmfire in a line, dealing magical damage and weakening the damage of everything it burns.

Wyrmfire Gout is the ability of Cinder Wyrmrider.

How the cast works

  • Cinder Wyrmrider needs 50 mana to cast Wyrmfire Gout. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The cast also waits until the enemy the unit is attacking (or the closest enemy, if it has no target) is within the cast range. Until then the unit keeps moving and attacking with its mana full.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It draws a line from the caster through the enemy it is attacking, 3 cells long, stopping at the edge of the board, and hits every enemy on that line. Enemies that cannot be targeted are never picked. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
