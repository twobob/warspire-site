Crowd Brawler is the passive of Brugg Ironjaw. The commander has it in every build, from the moment the commander joins your board.

Description

Brugg loves a crowd: he gains 5 armour for every unit next to him, friend or foe, and regenerates health.

Standing bonus

A standing bonus on the commander alone. It is in place for the whole fight.

  • +5 armour for each other adjacent allied unit (up to 4)
  • +5 armour for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 4)
  • +20 (levels 1 to 5), then 30 (levels 6 to 8), then 40 (levels 9 and 10) health regeneration per second

By player level

These values grow with your player level, and the game uses your level at the moment the fight starts. Levels that share every value are one row. Everything else about the ability is the same at every level.

When it fires

  • This is a passive. It is never cast, costs nothing and has no cooldown.
