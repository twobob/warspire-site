Gutter Scavenger is a tier 1 (Common) bruiser unit. It costs 1 gold in the shop and carries the Scrapkin and Artificer alliances.

Ability

Rivet Burst

Detonates a pouch of rivets, dealing magical damage to adjacent enemies and briefly stunning them.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies within 1 cell of itself.
  • Deals 100 / 175 / 250 magical damage to the targets (values per star).
  • Applies Stunned (general) (cannot move, attack or cast) to the targets for 0.5 / 0.75 / 1 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Rivet Burst ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Scrapkin race

Scavengers who patch their armour from the ruins. Affected units gain +7 armour and regenerate 8 health per second. Both bonuses double while you have fewer units alive than your opponent.

Units neededEffect
2One random Scrapkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
The chosen unit: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second.
In addition, the chosen unit: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second while outnumbered (fewer living units than the enemy side).
4Scrapkin units: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second.
In addition, Scrapkin units: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second while outnumbered (fewer living units than the enemy side).
6All allies: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second.
In addition, all allies: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second while outnumbered (fewer living units than the enemy side).
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Artificer class

Their contraptions are rigged to blow. When an Artificer dies it explodes, dealing magical damage equal to a share of its maximum health to all enemies within 1 cell.

Units neededEffect
2When an Artificer unit dies, it deals magical damage to all enemies within 1 cell of itself equal to 10% of its own maximum health.
4When an Artificer unit dies, it deals magical damage to all enemies within 1 cell of itself equal to 20% of its own maximum health.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
