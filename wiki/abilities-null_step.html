Null Step

The ability of Hollow Strider: leaps behind its target, deals magical damage and weakens enemies. It targets the farthest enemy.

In game: Steps out of reality behind the farthest enemy, dealing magical damage around it and shredding armour.

Null Step is the ability of Hollow Strider.

What it does

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.25 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
The farthest enemy.
  • Leaps behind the target.
  • Deals 200 / 300 / 400 magical damage to all enemies within 1 cell of itself (values per star).
  • Applies Unmade (-10 / -15 / -20 armour) to the same targets for 5 seconds (values per star).

How the cast works

  • Hollow Strider needs 100 mana to cast Null Step. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It picks the enemy farthest from the caster. Enemies that cannot be targeted are never picked. See Triggers and targeting.
  • The leap moves the unit to the free cell nearest to the one directly behind its target, and that enemy becomes the unit's attack target.
  • The damage and Unmade reach every enemy within 1 cell of the caster, measured from where the caster stands after the leap. The enemy it leapt at is hit only if the caster landed within 1 cell of it.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
