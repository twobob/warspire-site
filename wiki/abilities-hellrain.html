Hellrain

The ability of Abyssal Archon: deals magical damage and weakens enemies. It targets its current target and all enemies within 2 cells of it.

In game: Rains hellfire on the target, dealing magical damage to it and all enemies within 2 cells, then again as the embers fall.

Hellrain is the ability of Abyssal Archon.

  • Applies Embers (50 / 75 / 100 magical damage taken per second, -40% movement speed) to the targets for 4 seconds (values per star).

How the cast works

  • Abyssal Archon needs 100 mana to cast Hellrain. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It affects every enemy within 2 cells of the enemy the unit is attacking (the closest enemy if it has no target). That enemy is hit too. A diagonal step counts as one cell. Enemies that cannot be targeted are never picked. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
