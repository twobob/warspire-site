Smoke and Mirrors is the passive of Pip Quickfingers. The commander has it in every build, from the moment the commander joins your board.

Description

The first time Pip drops below half health he vanishes in a puff of smoke: he sheds his debuffs, cannot be targeted for 1.5 seconds and stuns adjacent enemies for 1.5 seconds.

Triggers

  • When the commander is below 50% health after taking damage (once per combat), it removes all timed debuffs from the commander; gains Smoke Cloud (cannot be targeted by enemies) for 1.5 seconds; and applies Stunned (general) (cannot move, attack or cast) to all enemies within 1 cell of the commander for 1.5 seconds.

By player level

Nothing about this ability changes with player level: it is the same from the day the commander arrives.

When it fires

  • This is a passive. It is never cast, costs nothing and has no cooldown.
  • Its triggered effects fire on their own when their condition is met.
