Gargoyle Gunner is a tier 1 (Common) ranged unit. It costs 1 gold in the shop and carries the Stoneborn, Stalker and Ironsight alliances.

Ability

Grit Blast

Fires a spray of stone grit at the target and enemies next to it, dealing physical damage and slowing their attacks.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.4 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target and all enemies within 1 cell of it.
  • Deals 150 / 250 / 350 physical damage to the targets (values per star).
  • Applies Grit-Blinded (-10 / -15 / -20% attack speed) to the targets for 2 / 3 / 4 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Grit Blast ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Stoneborn race

Gargoyles and golems carved from the spire itself, hardened by every battle. Each Stoneborn unit permanently gains maximum health for every enemy it has killed this game, more at higher stars.

Units neededEffect
2Stoneborn units: +10 / 20 / 30 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +600 / 1,200 / 1,800 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).
4Stoneborn units: +40 / 80 / 120 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +2,400 / 4,800 / 7,200 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).

Stalker class

Hunters of the ruins. Each landed attack has a chance to fire a quick volley of extra attacks at the same target.

Units neededEffect
3When a Stalker unit lands an attack (30% chance), it launches 2 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).
6When a Stalker unit lands an attack (38% chance), it launches 3 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).

Ironsight race

Gunsmith clans who never waste a shot on a healthy target. Every attack an Ironsight lands also strikes the most wounded enemy for bonus physical damage.

Units neededEffect
1When an Ironsight unit lands an attack, it deals 35 / 70 / 140 physical damage to the enemy with the lowest share of health left (values per star).
