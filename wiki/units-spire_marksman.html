Spire Marksman is a tier 3 (Rare) ranged unit. It costs 3 gold in the shop and carries the Scrapkin, Stalker and Ironsight alliances.

Ability

Long Shot

Takes careful aim at the farthest enemy and fires a devastating shot.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
2 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
The farthest enemy.
  • Deals 700 / 1,400 / 2,200 magical damage to the target (values per star).

Full details are on the Long Shot ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Scrapkin race

Scavengers who patch their armour from the ruins. Affected units gain +7 armour and regenerate 8 health per second. Both bonuses double while you have fewer units alive than your opponent.

Units neededEffect
2One random Scrapkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
The chosen unit: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second.
In addition, the chosen unit: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second while outnumbered (fewer living units than the enemy side).
4Scrapkin units: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second.
In addition, Scrapkin units: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second while outnumbered (fewer living units than the enemy side).
6All allies: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second.
In addition, all allies: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second while outnumbered (fewer living units than the enemy side).
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Stalker class

Hunters of the ruins. Each landed attack has a chance to fire a quick volley of extra attacks at the same target.

Units neededEffect
3When a Stalker unit lands an attack (30% chance), it launches 2 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).
6When a Stalker unit lands an attack (38% chance), it launches 3 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).

Ironsight race

Gunsmith clans who never waste a shot on a healthy target. Every attack an Ironsight lands also strikes the most wounded enemy for bonus physical damage.

Units neededEffect
1When an Ironsight unit lands an attack, it deals 35 / 70 / 140 physical damage to the enemy with the lowest share of health left (values per star).

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
