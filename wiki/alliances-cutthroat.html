Cutthroat

A class alliance of 8 units with steps at 3 and 6.

Cutthroat Badge

Knife-work in the dark. Cutthroats gain a chance to critically strike, and a critical strike wounds the target, reducing its healing received for 3 seconds.

Cutthroat is a class alliance carried by 8 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 3 and 6 different Cutthroat units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
3Cutthroat units: +15% critical strike chance, critical strikes deal 325% damage instead of the usual 200%.
When a Cutthroat unit lands a critical strike, it applies Wounded (Cutthroat, 3 units) (-75% healing received) to the unit it hit for 3 seconds.
6Cutthroat units: +25% critical strike chance, critical strikes deal 400% damage instead of the usual 200%.
When a Cutthroat unit lands a critical strike, it applies Wounded (Cutthroat, 6 units) (-100% healing received) to the unit it hit for 3 seconds.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

There are 8 different Cutthroat units and the top step needs 6, so you can reach it with 2 units to spare. Cutthroat Badge counts as 1 extra unit, so every step that needs more than one unit needs 1 unit fewer on your board. At least one real Cutthroat unit must be there for the item to count.

Aces

This unit is an ace tied to Cutthroat. An ace's extra effect is switched on while the ace is on your board and Cutthroat has at least one step active. See Aces. While Cutthroat is active, each copy of this ace left in the pool counts 15% heavier when the shop rolls a tier 5 unit, so the ace turns up a little more often.

Status effects

The steps of this alliance apply these status effects. Each has its own page, and How status effects work explains how they stack and expire.

  • Wounded: each step applies its own version, with 3 units and with 6 units.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.
  • An item that counts as an extra Cutthroat unit only counts while at least one Cutthroat unit is on your board.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
