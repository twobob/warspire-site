Chainwarden is a tier 2 (Uncommon) tank unit. It costs 2 gold in the shop and carries the Wight, Warden and Watcher alliances.

Ability

Grave Chain

Lashes the farthest enemy with a rusted chain, dealing physical damage and stunning it.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
The farthest enemy.
  • Deals 150 / 225 / 300 physical damage to the target (values per star).
  • Applies Stunned (general) (cannot move, attack or cast) to the target for 1 / 1.5 / 2 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Grave Chain ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.
