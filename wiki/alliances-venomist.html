Venomist

A class alliance of 4 units with a step at 3.

Venomist Sigil

Every attack from a Venomist adds a stack of venom to the target. Each stack reduces attack speed by 7.

Venomist is a class alliance carried by 4 units. It has one step, reached with 3 different Venomist units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

3When a Venomist unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Venom (-7% attack speed, stacks up to 99 times) to the unit it hit for the rest of combat.

There are 4 different Venomist units and the top step needs 3, so you can reach it with 1 unit to spare. Venomist Sigil counts as 1 extra unit, so every step that needs more than one unit needs 1 unit fewer on your board. At least one real Venomist unit must be there for the item to count.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.
  • An item that counts as an extra Venomist unit only counts while at least one Venomist unit is on your board.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
