Night Sentinel is a tier 2 (Uncommon) ranged unit. It costs 2 gold in the shop and carries the Spireborn, Stalker and Shade alliances.

Ability

Ricochet Glaive

Hurls a glaive that strikes the target and every enemy within 2 cells of it.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target and all enemies within 2 cells of it.
  • Deals 150 / 225 / 325 physical damage to the targets (values per star).

Full details are on the Ricochet Glaive ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Spireborn race

The last citizens of the spire draw on its fading wards. All allies generate mana every second.

Units neededEffect
2All allies: +4 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
4All allies: +8 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
6All allies: +15 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Stalker class

Hunters of the ruins. Each landed attack has a chance to fire a quick volley of extra attacks at the same target.

Units neededEffect
3When a Stalker unit lands an attack (30% chance), it launches 2 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).
6When a Stalker unit lands an attack (38% chance), it launches 3 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).

Shade class

Shadow-walkers who are never quite where you strike. Shades gain evasion, and gain attack damage each time they evade an attack.

Units neededEffect
2Shade units: +20% evasion.
When a Shade unit evades an attack, it applies a stack of Riposte (Shade, 2 units) (+10 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
4Shade units: +40% evasion.
When a Shade unit evades an attack, it applies a stack of Riposte (Shade, 4 units) (+20 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
