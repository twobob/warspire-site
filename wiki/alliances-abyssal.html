Abyssal

A race alliance of 5 units with steps at 1 and 2.

Demons of the pit beneath the spire share one hunger. A lone Abyssal deals 50% more damage. With two or more, each gains 100% more damage, but casting a spell silences the other Abyssals for 4 seconds.

Abyssal is a race alliance carried by 5 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 1 and 2 different Abyssal units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

There are 5 different Abyssal units and the top step needs 2, so you can reach it with 3 units to spare. Abyssal Emblem counts as 1 extra unit, so every step that needs more than one unit needs 1 unit fewer on your board. At least one real Abyssal unit must be there for the item to count.

Aces

This unit is an ace tied to Abyssal. An ace's extra effect is switched on while the ace is on your board and Abyssal has at least one step active. See Aces. While Abyssal is active, each copy of this ace left in the pool counts 15% heavier when the shop rolls a tier 5 unit, so the ace turns up a little more often.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.
  • An item that counts as an extra Abyssal unit only counts while at least one Abyssal unit is on your board.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
