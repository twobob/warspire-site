  • Attacks per second is 1 divided by the unit's attack rate, the seconds between attacks. Attack speed bonuses raise it.
  • Damage per second is the average attack damage times attacks per second, before armour, critical strikes, abilities and items.
  • Range is in cells; 1 is melee. Speed is movement speed: a unit crosses one cell in 128 divided by its speed, in seconds.
  • Mana is the size of the mana bar. A unit with 0 mana has no ability to cast.
  • A two-star unit has 2 times its one-star health and attack damage; a three-star unit has 4 times, or 4.8 times for tier 3 units.
