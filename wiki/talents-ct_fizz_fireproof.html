Fireproof Apron is a talent of Fizzwick Emberhorn, offered at player level 4 to the Bombardier and Firestarter builds.

Description

Fizzwick gains 30% magic resistance and 200 maximum health.

What it changes

The commander: +30% magic resistance, +200 maximum health.

  • The bonus is put on at the start of each fight and lasts the whole fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

The choice

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Fireproof Apron means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
