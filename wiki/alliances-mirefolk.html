Mirefolk

A race alliance of 5 units with steps at 2 and 4.

Bog-dwellers whose war drums quicken every heart. Mirefolk gain attack speed and other allies gain half as much.

Mirefolk is a race alliance carried by 5 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 2 and 4 different Mirefolk units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2Mirefolk units: +20% attack speed.
At the start of combat, each Mirefolk unit applies War Drums (Mirefolk, 2 units) (+20% attack speed) to all allies (itself included) for the rest of combat. This does not stack: however many units apply it, each target has it once.
4Mirefolk units: +30% attack speed.
At the start of combat, each Mirefolk unit applies War Drums (Mirefolk, 4 units) (+40% attack speed) to all allies (itself included) for the rest of combat. This does not stack: however many units apply it, each target has it once.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

Aces

This unit is an ace tied to Mirefolk. An ace's extra effect is switched on while the ace is on your board and Mirefolk has at least one step active. See Aces. While Mirefolk is active, each copy of this ace left in the pool counts 15% heavier when the shop rolls a tier 5 unit, so the ace turns up a little more often.

Status effects

The steps of this alliance apply these status effects. Each has its own page, and How status effects work explains how they stack and expire.

  • War Drums: each step applies its own version, with 2 units and with 4 units.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
