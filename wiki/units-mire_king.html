Mire King is a tier 5 (Legendary) ranged unit. It costs 5 gold in the shop and carries the Mirefolk and Warden alliances. It is an ace.

Ability

Mire Frenzy

Works itself into a frenzy, gaining a large amount of attack speed for 8 seconds.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
None: the effect comes at once.
Targets
Itself.
  • Applies Frenzy (+100 / 150 / 200% attack speed) to itself for 8 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Mire Frenzy ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

The shared pool holds 10 copies of Mire King for all players together, as it does for every tier 5 unit. See The shared pool and The shop.

Ace effect

Mire King is the ace of the Mirefolk alliance. Its ace effect is on while it stands on the board and at least one step of that alliance is active. See Aces.

In the game's own words: Ace: the Mire King gains +100 attack speed.

In play, the effect applies to Mire King itself:

  • +100% attack speed

When your shop rolls a tier 5 unit, each unit of that tier is weighted by the copies of it left in the pool. While that alliance is active for you, the weight of Mire King in that draw is 15% higher. See The shop.

Alliances

Mirefolk race

Bog-dwellers whose war drums quicken every heart. Mirefolk gain attack speed and other allies gain half as much.

Units neededEffect
2Mirefolk units: +20% attack speed.
At the start of combat, each Mirefolk unit applies War Drums (Mirefolk, 2 units) (+20% attack speed) to all allies (itself included) for the rest of combat. This does not stack: however many units apply it, each target has it once.
4Mirefolk units: +30% attack speed.
At the start of combat, each Mirefolk unit applies War Drums (Mirefolk, 4 units) (+40% attack speed) to all allies (itself included) for the rest of combat. This does not stack: however many units apply it, each target has it once.
