Longshot Scope

Uncommon offensive item. The holder: +35 attack damage, +8 attack range in cells.

That is the wording on the item's card. The sections below are worked out from the item itself and say what happens in play.

As an uncommon item (tier 2) it is offered on rounds 8 and 12. See Loot and items for how offers work.

What it does

The holder: +35 attack damage, +8 attack range in cells.
Only a ranged unit (attack range 2 or more) can hold it.

Who can carry it

Melee and ranged are judged by the unit's own attack range at one star: 1 cell or less is melee, anything longer is ranged. Range gained from items or effects does not change that. 30 units from the shop can hold it and 35 cannot. A unit holds one item at a time.

Equipping it from your inventory sends the unit's current item back to the inventory. Dragging it from one unit to another swaps the two items; the swap is refused if either item would end up on a unit that cannot hold it. See Loot and items.

Among the commanders, Vey the Pactbinder, Pip Quickfingers and Fizzwick Emberhorn can hold it; Brugg Ironjaw cannot.

An item does nothing while its holder sits on the bench. Selling the holder returns the item to your inventory.

There is no limit on copies, though an offer shows items you do not own yet before it repeats one.
