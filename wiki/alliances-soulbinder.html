Soulbinder

A class alliance of 7 units with steps at 2, 4 and 6.

Casting a spell binds the Soulbinder to the most wounded ally. For a short time both heal for part of the damage they deal.

Soulbinder is a class alliance carried by 7 units. It has 3 steps, reached with 2, 4 and 6 different Soulbinder units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 2 units) (+50% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 2 seconds.
4When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 4 units) (+60% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 2.5 seconds.
6When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 6 units) (+70% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 3 seconds.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

There are 7 different Soulbinder units and the top step needs 6, so you can reach it with 1 unit to spare.

Aces

This unit is an ace tied to Soulbinder. An ace's extra effect is switched on while the ace is on your board and Soulbinder has at least one step active. See Aces. While Soulbinder is active, each copy of this ace left in the pool counts 15% heavier when the shop rolls a tier 5 unit, so the ace turns up a little more often.

AceExtra effect
Soul TyrantWhen this unit casts an ability, it applies Tyrant’s Link (+200% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 6 seconds.

Status effects

The steps of this alliance apply these status effects. Each has its own page, and How status effects work explains how they stack and expire.

  • Soul Link: each step applies its own version, with 2 units, with 4 units and with 6 units.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
