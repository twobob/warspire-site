Thornkin

A race alliance of 6 units with steps at 2, 4 and 6.

Druids of the overgrown lower terraces. At the start of combat a random Thornkin calls a beast to fight beside it; higher levels call a stronger beast.

Thornkin is a race alliance carried by 6 units. It has 3 steps, reached with 2, 4 and 6 different Thornkin units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2One random Thornkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat, the chosen unit summons a Thorn Hound in the nearest free cell.
4One random Thornkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat, the chosen unit summons a Carrion Wing in the nearest free cell.
6One random Thornkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat, the chosen unit summons an Elder Thornwyrm in the nearest free cell.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.
  • A step that affects a random unit picks it afresh at the start of every combat, from the Thornkin units on your board.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
