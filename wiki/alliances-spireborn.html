Spireborn

A race alliance of 8 units with steps at 2, 4 and 6.

Spireborn
Kind
Race
Units
8
Steps
2 / 4 / 6

The last citizens of the spire draw on its fading wards. All allies generate mana every second.

Spireborn is a race alliance carried by 8 units. It has 3 steps, reached with 2, 4 and 6 different Spireborn units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2All allies: +4 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
4All allies: +8 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
6All allies: +15 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
