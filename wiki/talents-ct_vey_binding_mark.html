Binding Mark is a talent of Vey the Pactbinder, offered at player level 10 to the Thrall Binder build.

Description

Thrall Mark lasts 9 seconds, removes 15 armour and makes the target take 30% more damage.

What it changes

Thrall Mark becomes Binding Mark.

  • The change takes effect from the next fight, and the upgraded form keeps the original's place in the casting order.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Binding Mark means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
