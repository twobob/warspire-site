Pit Marshal is a tier 4 (Epic) tank unit. It costs 4 gold in the shop and carries the Abyssal, Ravager and Wyrmblood alliances.

Ability

Dread Sentence

Pronounces dread on the target: it is silenced and takes pure damage every second for 10 seconds.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target.
  • Applies Sentenced (60 / 90 / 120 pure damage taken per second, cannot cast abilities) to the target for 10 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Dread Sentence ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Abyssal race

Demons of the pit beneath the spire share one hunger. A lone Abyssal deals 50% more damage. With two or more, each gains 100% more damage, but casting a spell silences the other Abyssals for 4 seconds.

Units neededEffect
1Abyssal units: +50% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
2Abyssal units: +100% attack damage.
When an Abyssal unit casts an ability, it applies Pact Silence (cannot cast abilities) to every other allied Abyssal unit for 4 seconds.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

Ravager class

Brutes who break their foes’ will. A Ravager’s attack maims the target, dealing physical damage and reducing the target’s damage for 4 seconds.

Units neededEffect
2When a Ravager unit lands an attack (at most once every 4 seconds), it deals 80 physical damage to the unit it hit and applies Maimed (Ravager, 2 units) (-30% attack damage) to the same target for 4 seconds.
4When a Ravager unit lands an attack (at most once every 4 seconds), it deals 120 physical damage to the unit it hit and applies Maimed (Ravager, 4 units) (-50% attack damage) to the same target for 4 seconds.

Wyrmblood race

Heirs of the old wyrms. With two Wyrmblood, each of their spells also breathes fire on the target and every enemy within 1 cell, dealing magical damage and scorching them.

Units neededEffect
2When a Wyrmblood unit casts an ability, it deals 150 / 250 / 400 magical damage to the enemy its ability was cast on (its current target if there is none) and all enemies within 1 cell of it and applies Scorched (alliances) (30 / 50 / 80 magical damage taken per second) to the same targets for 4 seconds (values per star).

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
