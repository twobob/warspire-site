Swarmkin

A race alliance of 5 units with steps at 2 and 4.

Chitinous things from the spire's foundations. At the start of combat and every 10 seconds, a random Swarmkin releases untargetable skitterlings that harry the enemy for 10 seconds; their bites weaken the target's damage.

Swarmkin is a race alliance carried by 5 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 2 and 4 different Swarmkin units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2One random Swarmkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat and then every 10 seconds, the chosen unit summons 3 Skitterling units at star 1 on the nearest free cells; each lasts 10 seconds. The repeats stop when the chosen unit dies.
4One random Swarmkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat and then every 10 seconds, the chosen unit summons 3 Skitterling units at star 2 on the nearest free cells; each lasts 10 seconds. The repeats stop when the chosen unit dies.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.
  • A step that affects a random unit picks it afresh at the start of every combat, from the Swarmkin units on your board.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
