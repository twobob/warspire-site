Drowned Behemoth is a tier 4 (Epic) tank unit. It costs 4 gold in the shop and carries the Drowned, Warden and Watcher alliances.

Ability

Crushing Tide

Calls up a crushing tide around itself, damaging and stunning all enemies within 2 cells.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies within 2 cells of itself.
  • Deals 150 / 225 / 350 magical damage to the targets (values per star).
  • Applies Stunned (general) (cannot move, attack or cast) to the targets for 2 / 2.25 / 3 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Crushing Tide ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.
