Restless Gravestone

Restless Gravestone is a placeable planted on the board by the item Restless Gravestone. It is not sold in the shop.

Restless Gravestone is a placeable: an object that stands in a cell of the board. The unit holding Restless Gravestone plants 1 at the start of combat, in the free cell nearest to it. It has 3,000 health, never moves and never attacks. See Summons and placeables.

Ability

Restless Gravestone has no ability to cast and no mana bar. What it does is listed under its unit effect.

Unit effect

Restless Gravestone carries an effect of its own, apart from any ability. It is always on. See Triggers and targeting for how such effects fire.

  • When an ally of this unit dies (50% chance; at most once every 4 seconds), it summons a Grave Shambler at star 1 in the nearest free cell.

Restless Gravestone is a placeable. It never moves and never attacks. It acts only through its unit effect, listed above.

Where it comes from

Restless Gravestone is never offered in the shop. It is planted on the board by an item. See Summons and placeables for how summoned and placed units behave.

  • Item Restless Gravestone
    • The unit holding the item plants 1 at the start of combat, in the free cell nearest to it.
A placeable does not keep your side in the fight: when your last fighting unit falls the round is lost and Restless Gravestone collapses. If you win, it adds nothing to the damage dealt to the loser.

Alliances

Restless Gravestone carries no alliance. It neither counts towards an alliance step nor gains a members' bonus.
