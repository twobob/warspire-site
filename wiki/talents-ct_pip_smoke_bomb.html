Smoke Bomb

Smoke Bomb is a talent of Pip Quickfingers, offered at player level 8 to the Pickpocket and Brawler builds.

Description

Pip also casts Smoke Bomb.

What it changes

The commander also casts Smoke Bomb.

  • The new ability runs on its own cooldown from the next fight on, and is cast after the build's own abilities when several are ready together.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

Ability

AbilityTimingDescription
Added by the talentSmoke BombCooldown 8 secondsPip drops a smoke bomb: adjacent enemies are stunned for 1 second and Pip cannot be targeted for 1 second.

The choice

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Smoke Bomb means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
