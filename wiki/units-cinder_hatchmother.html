Cinder Hatchmother is a tier 3 (Rare) bruiser unit. It costs 3 gold in the shop and carries the Ashen, Soulbinder and Venomist alliances.

Ability

Spawn Mites

Hatches a clutch of cinder mites that swarm the nearest enemies.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.2 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
  • Summons 3 / 4 / 6 Cinder Mite units at its own star level on the nearest free cells (values per star).

Full details are on the Spawn Mites ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Ashen race

The burnt martyrs of the spire. When an Ashen unit first falls, allies within 2 cells are healed for 250 and gain 10% bonus damage. With six Ashen, each one rises again once per combat with 40% of its health.

Units neededEffect
3When an Ashen unit dies (once per combat), it heals all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.
6Ashen units: one revival per combat at 40% of maximum health.
When an Ashen unit revives (once per combat), it heals itself and all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.

Soulbinder class

Casting a spell binds the Soulbinder to the most wounded ally. For a short time both heal for part of the damage they deal.

Units neededEffect
2When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 2 units) (+50% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 2 seconds.
4When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 4 units) (+60% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 2.5 seconds.
6When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 6 units) (+70% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 3 seconds.

Venomist class

Every attack from a Venomist adds a stack of venom to the target. Each stack reduces attack speed by 7.

Units neededEffect
3When a Venomist unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Venom (-7% attack speed, stacks up to 99 times) to the unit it hit for the rest of combat.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
