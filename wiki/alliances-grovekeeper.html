Grovekeeper

A class alliance of 5 units with steps at 2 and 4.

Keepers of the hanging gardens who share the grove's strength. At the start of combat, one Grovekeeper (two with four) grows into a stronger form with double health and damage.

Grovekeeper is a class alliance carried by 5 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 2 and 4 different Grovekeeper units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.
  • A step that affects a random unit picks it afresh at the start of every combat, from the Grovekeeper units on your board.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
