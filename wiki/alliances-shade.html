Shade

A class alliance of 5 units with steps at 2 and 4.

Shadow-walkers who are never quite where you strike. Shades gain evasion, and gain attack damage each time they evade an attack.

Shade is a class alliance carried by 5 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 2 and 4 different Shade units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2Shade units: +20% evasion.
When a Shade unit evades an attack, it applies a stack of Riposte (Shade, 2 units) (+10 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
4Shade units: +40% evasion.
When a Shade unit evades an attack, it applies a stack of Riposte (Shade, 4 units) (+20 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

There are 5 different Shade units and the top step needs 4, so you can reach it with 1 unit to spare. Shade Pennon counts as 1 extra unit, so every step that needs more than one unit needs 1 unit fewer on your board. At least one real Shade unit must be there for the item to count.

Aces

This unit is an ace tied to Shade. An ace's extra effect is switched on while the ace is on your board and Shade has at least one step active. See Aces. While Shade is active, each copy of this ace left in the pool counts 15% heavier when the shop rolls a tier 5 unit, so the ace turns up a little more often.

AceExtra effect
DuskmotherWhen this unit takes damage from an attack or an ability (15% chance), it applies Dusk Veil (+100% evasion, +35% attack speed) to itself for 5 seconds.

Status effects

The steps of this alliance apply these status effects. Each has its own page, and How status effects work explains how they stack and expire.

  • Riposte: each step applies its own version, with 2 units and with 4 units.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.
  • An item that counts as an extra Shade unit only counts while at least one Shade unit is on your board.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
