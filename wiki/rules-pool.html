The shared pool

How many copies of each unit exist and how they come back.

Every unit in the shop comes out of one pool that all 8 players share. The pool holds a fixed number of copies of each unit, set by the unit's tier. At the start of a match it holds 1,208 copies of 65 units. When a unit's copies are all owned by players, nobody is offered that unit until some come back.

Units that are not sold, such as summons and commanders, are not part of the pool.

How copies leave and come back

  • A copy leaves the pool when a player buys it. Being shown in a shop does not take a copy out, and neither does locking a shop. If two players are offered the last copy, the first to buy gets it and the other offer can no longer be bought.
  • Combining does not return anything: the copies that made a higher star stay out of the pool, inside that unit.
  • Selling a unit returns its copies at once: a 1 star returns 1, a 2 star returns 3, a 3 star returns 9. That is every copy that went into it.
  • When a player is knocked out, every unit on their board and bench goes back to the pool in the same way, before the next shops are rolled. See Player damage and elimination.
  • Rerolling returns nothing, because the units in a shop were never taken out.
  • Summoned units and commanders never touch the pool.

Other players and your odds

The chance that a shop slot rolls a given tier comes only from your level (see the tier odds). Inside the tier, each unit's chance is its share of the copies left in that tier. So every copy another player holds of the unit you want lowers your chance of seeing it, and every copy they hold of a different unit of the same tier raises it slightly. Watching the other players shows what they are building, and so which units are thin.

Stars against the pool

3 copies combine into the next star, so a 2 star is 3 copies, a 3 star is 9 copies (see Star upgrades). Set against the pool, that decides how many players can finish the same unit.

At tier 4 and tier 5 only one player in the match can own a given unit at 3 star, and the other players can stop even that: a tier 4 unit has 3 spare copies, so nobody can finish it once the other players hold 4 copies of it between them; a tier 5 unit has 1 spare copy, so nobody can finish it once the other players hold 2 copies of it between them. It can be finished again once enough of those copies are sold or their owner is knocked out.
