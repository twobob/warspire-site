Hive Skulker is a tier 1 (Common) assassin unit. It costs 1 gold in the shop and carries the Swarmkin and Cutthroat alliances.

Ability

Skitter Lunge

Scuttles to the farthest enemy and bites it, dealing physical damage and leaving a festering wound.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.2 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
The farthest enemy.
  • Leaps behind the target.
  • Deals 125 / 200 / 300 physical damage to the target (values per star).
  • Applies Festering Bite (20 / 35 / 50 magical damage taken per second) to the target for 4 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Skitter Lunge ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Swarmkin race

Chitinous things from the spire's foundations. At the start of combat and every 10 seconds, a random Swarmkin releases untargetable skitterlings that harry the enemy for 10 seconds; their bites weaken the target's damage.

Units neededEffect
2One random Swarmkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat and then every 10 seconds, the chosen unit summons 3 Skitterling units at star 1 on the nearest free cells; each lasts 10 seconds. The repeats stop when the chosen unit dies.
4One random Swarmkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat and then every 10 seconds, the chosen unit summons 3 Skitterling units at star 2 on the nearest free cells; each lasts 10 seconds. The repeats stop when the chosen unit dies.

Cutthroat class

Knife-work in the dark. Cutthroats gain a chance to critically strike, and a critical strike wounds the target, reducing its healing received for 3 seconds.

Units neededEffect
3Cutthroat units: +15% critical strike chance, critical strikes deal 325% damage instead of the usual 200%.
When a Cutthroat unit lands a critical strike, it applies Wounded (Cutthroat, 3 units) (-75% healing received) to the unit it hit for 3 seconds.
6Cutthroat units: +25% critical strike chance, critical strikes deal 400% damage instead of the usual 200%.
When a Cutthroat unit lands a critical strike, it applies Wounded (Cutthroat, 6 units) (-100% healing received) to the unit it hit for 3 seconds.
