Alliances

Every alliance and what it grants at each step.

Warspire has 33 alliances: 16 Race alliances and 17 Class alliances. Every unit you can buy carries one or more of them. Field enough different units of an alliance and it reaches a step, which grants an effect for the whole fight. How alliances count explains how units are counted, and the Alliance matrix shows which units carry which alliances.

Table

Steps are the numbers of different units needed. Units is how many different units you can buy that carry the alliance. The last column is what the highest step grants.

AllianceKindStepsUnitsTop step effect
AbyssalRace1 / 25Abyssal units: +100% attack damage.
When an Abyssal unit casts an ability, it applies Pact Silence (cannot cast abilities) to every other allied Abyssal unit for 4 seconds.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
ArcanistClass3 / 68All enemies: -70% magic resistance.
Enemies summoned later in the fight get this too.
ArtificerClass2 / 45When an Artificer unit dies, it deals magical damage to all enemies within 1 cell of itself equal to 20% of its own maximum health.
AshenRace3 / 66Ashen units: one revival per combat at 40% of maximum health.
When an Ashen unit revives (once per combat), it heals itself and all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.
BulwarkClass2 / 4 / 67Bulwark units: 25% less physical and magical damage taken.
In addition, Bulwark units: 25% less physical and magical damage taken while there is at least one other adjacent allied Bulwark unit. The reductions multiply instead of adding up: with both, 43.75% less damage in all.
ConjurerClass2 / 45Conjurer units and the summoned units that carry the Conjurer mark: +40% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
CutthroatClass3 / 68Cutthroat units: +25% critical strike chance, critical strikes deal 400% damage instead of the usual 200%.
When a Cutthroat unit lands a critical strike, it applies Wounded (Cutthroat, 6 units) (-100% healing received) to the unit it hit for 3 seconds.
DrownedRace2 / 45All allies: +55% magic resistance.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
DuelistClass3 / 68Duelist units: +25% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
FeralRace2 / 4 / 66When an ally lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 6 units) (+13 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
GrovekeeperClass2 / 452 random Grovekeeper units are picked at the start of combat.
The chosen units: +100% maximum health, +100% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
IronsightRace13When an Ironsight unit lands an attack, it deals 35 / 70 / 140 physical damage to the enemy with the lowest share of health left (values per star).
MenderClass2 / 45All allies: +45% healing received.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
MirefolkRace2 / 45Mirefolk units: +30% attack speed.
At the start of combat, each Mirefolk unit applies War Drums (Mirefolk, 4 units) (+40% attack speed) to all allies (itself included) for the rest of combat. This does not stack: however many units apply it, each target has it once.
OathboundRace23When an Oathbound unit dies, it applies a stack of Oath of Vengeance (+125 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to every other allied Oathbound unit for the rest of combat.
ParagonClass11Paragon units: +15% attack damage, +40% maximum health.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
RavagerClass2 / 44When a Ravager unit lands an attack (at most once every 4 seconds), it deals 120 physical damage to the unit it hit and applies Maimed (Ravager, 4 units) (-50% attack damage) to the same target for 4 seconds.
RiftbornRace2 / 45When a Riftborn unit dies, it summons a Greater Rift Echo in the nearest free cell.
ScrapkinRace2 / 4 / 66All allies: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second.
In addition, all allies: +7 armour, +8 health regeneration per second while outnumbered (fewer living units than the enemy side).
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
ShadeClass2 / 45Shade units: +40% evasion.
When a Shade unit evades an attack, it applies a stack of Riposte (Shade, 4 units) (+20 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
SoulbinderClass2 / 4 / 67When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 6 units) (+70% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 3 seconds.
SpirebornRace2 / 4 / 68All allies: +15 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
StalkerClass3 / 68When a Stalker unit lands an attack (38% chance), it launches 3 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).
StonebornRace2 / 45Stoneborn units: +40 / 80 / 120 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +2,400 / 4,800 / 7,200 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).
SunderedClass34When an ally lands an attack (40% chance; at most once every 0.5 seconds), it deals pure damage to the unit it hit equal to 4% of the target's maximum health.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
SwarmkinRace2 / 45One random Swarmkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat and then every 10 seconds, the chosen unit summons 3 Skitterling units at star 2 on the nearest free cells; each lasts 10 seconds. The repeats stop when the chosen unit dies.
ThornkinRace2 / 4 / 66One random Thornkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat, the chosen unit summons an Elder Thornwyrm in the nearest free cell.
TriadRace34When a Triad unit casts an ability (at most once every 6 seconds), it deals 130 / 195 / 260 magical damage to the enemy its ability was cast on (its current target if there is none) and all enemies within 1 cell of it and applies Slammed (cannot move, attack or cast) to the same targets for 1.5 / 2.25 / 3 seconds (values per star).
VenomistClass34When a Venomist unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Venom (-7% attack speed, stacks up to 99 times) to the unit it hit for the rest of combat.
WardenClass3 / 67Warden units: +25 armour.
WatcherClass2 / 45All allies: +25% attack damage.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
WightRace2 / 4 / 66All enemies: -15 armour.
Enemies summoned later in the fight get this too.
WyrmbloodRace23When a Wyrmblood unit casts an ability, it deals 150 / 250 / 400 magical damage to the enemy its ability was cast on (its current target if there is none) and all enemies within 1 cell of it and applies Scorched (alliances) (30 / 50 / 80 magical damage taken per second) to the same targets for 4 seconds (values per star).
