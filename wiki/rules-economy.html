Gold and income

Round gold, interest, streaks and the gold cap.

Gold buys units, rerolls of the shop and experience. You start a match with 5 gold and can never hold more than 100 gold. Income is paid once per round, as soon as that round's fights have ended and before the next preparation phase begins.

Order of payment

Income is paid in five steps, always in this order. The order matters for two reasons: interest is worked out before streak gold and round gold arrive, and each step is checked against the gold cap on its own.

  1. Victory gold: 1 gold if you won your fight.
  2. Interest: 1 gold for every 10 gold held, up to 3 gold.
  3. Streak gold: a bonus for a run of wins or a run of losses.
  4. Round gold: the fixed payment for the round just fought.
  5. Gold from items: the bonus of any global item that pays gold each round.

After the gold is paid you gain 1 experience, then matching copies combine, then the shop refreshes unless you locked it. A player who has just been eliminated is paid nothing, and nothing is paid after the final round of a match.

Round gold

Every living player is paid round gold after every round, win or lose. The amount depends only on the number of the round that was just fought.

Interest

Interest is 1 gold for every full 10 gold, to a maximum of 3 gold a round. The maximum is reached at 30 gold; holding more than that earns nothing extra.

The gold that is counted is the gold you held at the moment the fight began, plus the victory gold you were just paid for that fight. Anything you buy, sell or reroll while the fight is running does not change it, so spending during a fight never costs interest for that round. Streak gold, round gold and item gold are paid after interest and are not counted until the next round.

Because victory gold is counted, a winner needs slightly less. Start a fight you go on to win with 9 gold and you are paid interest as if you held 10 gold: 1 gold instead of 0 gold.

Victory gold

Winning a fight pays 1 gold. A fight against a ghost counts in full: you are paid victory gold for beating a ghost, and the player the ghost was copied from is not affected at all. A draw pays no victory gold, and neither does a fight that runs out of time, because both sides are treated as having lost (see Time limit and overtime). A round in which you have no opponent pays no victory gold.

Win and lose streaks

Streak gold is paid every round from the streak you hold after that round's fight. If you have a win streak you are paid from the win table; otherwise you are paid from the lose table. You never hold both at once.

What changes a streak

  • A win adds one to your win streak and sets your lose streak to zero.
  • A loss sets your win streak to zero. It adds one to your lose streak only if you took damage; a loss that costs no health leaves the lose streak as it was.
  • A fight that runs out of time is a loss for both players, each by the rule above.
  • A draw changes neither streak. Whatever streak you had is kept and is paid again.
  • A round with no opponent changes neither streak, and your streak gold is paid again.
  • A fight against a ghost counts exactly like a fight against a player, for the player who fought it.

Only a win ends a lose streak. A draw, a round with no opponent or a loss that costs no health leaves it as it was, and its streak gold is paid again.

A win streak does not grow for ever. When it reaches 8 wins you are paid the bonus for 8 wins (4 gold) and the streak then returns to zero, so the next win counts as the first of a new streak. A full run of 8 wins pays 13 gold in streak gold in total. A lose streak has no such limit.

The gold cap

You can hold at most 100 gold. Every payment, whether a step of income or the proceeds of a sale, is cut short at the cap and the part that does not fit is lost. Nothing is stored for later. Because income is paid step by step, a player near the cap simply receives less from the later steps.

Interest stops growing at 30 gold, so gold held above that amount earns nothing and only brings you closer to the cap.

Items that change gold

Some global items change your income or your prices for as long as you own them.

  • Tithe Purse (Tier 3): +1 gold at the end of every round, paid last
  • Merchant's Compass (Tier 4): +2 gold at the end of every round, paid last
  • Recruiter's Ledger (Tier 2): each reroll costs 1 gold less

These effects add together if you hold more than one such item.

No alliance, unit ability or commander talent pays gold or changes a price at present. Items cannot be sold for gold.

Rerolls

A reroll of the shop costs 2 gold. Recruiter's Ledger lowers that to 1 gold.

  • Losing a fight gives you one free reroll at the start of the next round. A fight that runs out of time counts as a loss for this, a loss to a ghost counts, and so does a loss that cost no health. A draw gives nothing.
  • While you hold a free reroll the reroll button reads FREE instead of a price, and your next reroll uses it up instead of gold.
  • You hold one free reroll at most. Losing again while you still have one does not give a second.
  • An unused free reroll is kept from round to round until you spend it.

Buying experience

Buying experience costs 5 gold and gives 5 experience. It can be bought any number of times in a round, during preparation or during a fight, until you reach level 10, at which point it can no longer be bought. Levels and experience lists what each level costs.

Selling units

Selling a unit pays its tier's cost, plus a bonus for its stars: +2 at 2 stars, +4 at 3 stars. A one star unit therefore sells for exactly what it cost. A combined unit sells for much less than the 3 copies that went into each step, so the table shows the sale price beside the gold the unit cost to make.

  • The gold from a sale is subject to the gold cap like any other payment.
  • The copies go back to the shared pool and any item the unit held returns to your inventory.
  • Units on the bench can be sold at any time. Units on the board cannot be sold while your own fight is running.
  • Your commander cannot be sold.
  • If your board is over the unit cap when a fight begins and the bench has no room, the cheapest units on your board are sold for you at these prices, one for each unit over the cap (the most recently moved first when prices tie).
