Mending Totem

Mending Totem is a placeable planted on the board by the item Mending Totem. It is not sold in the shop.

Mending Totem is a placeable: an object that stands in a cell of the board. The unit holding Mending Totem plants 1 at the start of combat, in the free cell nearest to it. It has 500 health, never moves and never attacks. See Summons and placeables.

Ability

Mending Totem has no ability to cast and no mana bar. What it does is listed under its unit effect.

Unit effect

Mending Totem carries an effect of its own, apart from any ability. It is always on. See Triggers and targeting for how such effects fire.

  • Every second, this unit heals itself and all allies within 1 cell for 50 health.

Mending Totem is a placeable. It never moves and never attacks. It acts only through its unit effect, listed above.

Where it comes from

Mending Totem is never offered in the shop. It is planted on the board by an item. See Summons and placeables for how summoned and placed units behave.

  • Item Mending Totem
    • The unit holding the item plants 1 at the start of combat, in the free cell nearest to it.
A placeable does not keep your side in the fight: when your last fighting unit falls the round is lost and Mending Totem collapses. If you win, it adds nothing to the damage dealt to the loser.

Alliances

Mending Totem carries no alliance. It neither counts towards an alliance step nor gains a members' bonus.
