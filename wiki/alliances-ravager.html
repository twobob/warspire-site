Ravager

A class alliance of 4 units with steps at 2 and 4.

Brutes who break their foes’ will. A Ravager’s attack maims the target, dealing physical damage and reducing the target’s damage for 4 seconds.

Ravager is a class alliance carried by 4 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 2 and 4 different Ravager units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2When a Ravager unit lands an attack (at most once every 4 seconds), it deals 80 physical damage to the unit it hit and applies Maimed (Ravager, 2 units) (-30% attack damage) to the same target for 4 seconds.
4When a Ravager unit lands an attack (at most once every 4 seconds), it deals 120 physical damage to the unit it hit and applies Maimed (Ravager, 4 units) (-50% attack damage) to the same target for 4 seconds.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

There are exactly 4 different Ravager units, and the top step needs 4, so the top step needs every one of them on your board at the same time. Ravager Seal counts as 1 extra unit, so every step that needs more than one unit needs 1 unit fewer on your board. At least one real Ravager unit must be there for the item to count.

Status effects

The steps of this alliance apply these status effects. Each has its own page, and How status effects work explains how they stack and expire.

  • Maimed: each step applies its own version, with 2 units and with 4 units.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.
  • An item that counts as an extra Ravager unit only counts while at least one Ravager unit is on your board.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
