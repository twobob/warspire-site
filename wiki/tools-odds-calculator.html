Shop odds calculator

How likely is the shop to show the unit you are looking for? Choose your level and the unit's tier, then say how many copies have already left the pool. A two-star unit holds 3 copies and a three-star unit 9, wherever it sits.

With Gilded Invitation your shop rolls as if you were 1 level higher: choose that level here.

How the shop rolls

  • The shop has 5 slots and fills them one after another.
  • For each slot it first rolls a tier, using the odds of your level in the table below.
  • It then picks a unit of that tier. Every unit is weighted by the copies of it left in the shared pool, less any copies already placed in this same shop, so a unit that other players are collecting comes up less often.
  • A tier 5 ace whose alliance you have active counts 15% more than its copies.
  • If the rolled tier has no copies left, the slot takes the nearest lower tier that has some, and only then a higher one.
  • A unit you already own at 3 stars is never offered to you.
  • When you reroll by hand, the units of the shop being replaced are skipped as long as other units of the rolled tier have copies.

The full rules are on The shop and The shared pool.

What the calculator leaves out

  • Only the tier you pick is tracked. Other tiers are assumed to have copies left, so a slot never falls into your tier from an empty one.
  • The copies of other units that are gone are treated as one total. The result is exact as long as no single unit of the tier has run out, because the fall in weight is the same whichever unit lost the copies.
  • Other units of the tier are not treated as aces of your active alliances, and none of them is counted as owned at the highest star.
  • The rule that a hand reroll skips the previous shop's units is left out. It can only raise the chance a little, when unwanted units of the same tier were in the shop you replaced.
  • The gold figures count every shop as a paid reroll at 2 gold, assume you buy each copy the moment it shows, and assume no other player takes a copy meanwhile. The free shop at the start of each round and a free reroll after a loss make the real cost lower.
