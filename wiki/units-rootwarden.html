Rootwarden is a tier 4 (Epic) tank unit. It costs 4 gold in the shop and carries the Thornkin, Mender and Grovekeeper alliances.

Ability

Leeching Roots

Binds the target and enemies next to it in leeching roots that hold them still and drain their health.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target and all enemies within 1 cell of it.
  • Applies Rooted (units) (cannot move) to the targets for 3 seconds.
  • Applies Leeched (100 / 150 / 220 magical damage taken per second) to the targets for 5 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Leeching Roots ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.
