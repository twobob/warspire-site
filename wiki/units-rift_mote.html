Rift Mote is a tier 1 (Common) caster unit. It costs 1 gold in the shop and carries the Riftborn, Arcanist and Triad alliances.

Ability

Static Bloom

Releases a crackling bloom of rift energy that damages all enemies within 2 cells.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies within 2 cells of itself.
  • Deals 120 / 180 / 250 magical damage to the targets (values per star).

Full details are on the Static Bloom ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Riftborn race

Things that crawled out of the cracks in the spire. When a Riftborn unit dies it leaves behind a Rift Echo that fights on. Echoes are not Riftborn.

Units neededEffect
2When a Riftborn unit dies, it summons a Rift Echo in the nearest free cell.
4When a Riftborn unit dies, it summons a Greater Rift Echo in the nearest free cell.

Arcanist class

Scholars of the broken wards. All enemies lose magic resistance, so they take more magical damage.

Units neededEffect
3All enemies: -35% magic resistance.
Enemies summoned later in the fight get this too.
6All enemies: -70% magic resistance.
Enemies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Triad race

Three elemental siblings who fight as one. With three Triad units, a spell charges its caster, and a charged Triad slams the ground around its target, dealing magical damage and stunning (at most once every 6 seconds each).

Units neededEffect
3When a Triad unit casts an ability (at most once every 6 seconds), it deals 130 / 195 / 260 magical damage to the enemy its ability was cast on (its current target if there is none) and all enemies within 1 cell of it and applies Slammed (cannot move, attack or cast) to the same targets for 1.5 / 2.25 / 3 seconds (values per star).

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
