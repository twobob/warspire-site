Grand Heist is the upgraded form of Pickpocket, an ability of Pip Quickfingers. It takes the place of the original once the talent Grand Heist is chosen.

Description

At the start of combat Pip robs two random enemies, which deal 25% less damage and attack 25 slower, while Pip gains 40% damage and 40 attack speed. Kills drop even more loot.

In play. The percentage change to damage applies to attack damage only. Damage from abilities and damage over time are unchanged.

Triggers

  • At the start of combat, the commander applies Robbed Blind (-25% attack damage, -25% attack speed) to 2 random enemies for the rest of combat.
  • At the start of combat, the commander gains Big Score (+40% attack damage, +40% attack speed) for the rest of combat.
  • When the commander kills an enemy, it gains a stack of Rich Spoils (+25 attack damage, +15% attack speed, stacks up to 10 times) for the rest of combat.

By player level

Nothing about this ability changes with player level: it is the same from the day the commander arrives.

When it fires

  • This is a passive. It is never cast, costs nothing and has no cooldown.
  • Its triggered effects fire on their own when their condition is met.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

What the upgrade changes

This is what Pickpocket becomes.

Pickpocket: At the start of combat Pip robs a random enemy, which deals 20% less damage and attacks 20 slower, while Pip gains the same. Every enemy Pip kills drops more loot: +15 attack damage and +10 attack speed.

Everything that differs between the two forms. Anything not listed is the same in both.
