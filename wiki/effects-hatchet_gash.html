How status effects work

What it does

A unit with this effect: 10 to 30% more physical and magical damage taken. The values depend on the star level of the unit that applies it. The table gives each one.

Values by the star level of the unit that applies it
Star 1Star 2Star 3
10% more physical and magical damage taken20% more physical and magical damage taken30% more physical and magical damage taken

This counts as a debuff, because it harms the unit that carries it.

Stacking and duration

  • It does not stack. A unit carries one copy at a time. Applying it again renews it: the longer of the time left and the new duration is kept. The values of the newest application replace the old ones, even when they are weaker.

See How status effects work for how effects stack, renew and expire, and Stat reference for what each stat means.
