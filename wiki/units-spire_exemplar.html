Spire Exemplar is a tier 5 (Legendary) bruiser unit. It costs 5 gold in the shop and carries the Paragon, Spireborn and Duelist alliances.

Ability

Exemplar's Challenge

Strikes the current target with a decisive blow, then fights on with greater fury for 6 seconds.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target.
  • Deals 400 / 700 / 1,500 physical damage to the target (values per star).
  • Applies Exemplar's Fury (+20 / 30 / 50% attack damage, +20 / 30 / 50% attack speed) to itself for 6 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Exemplar's Challenge ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Paragon class

A living legend of the spire. The Paragon gains 15% bonus damage and 40% bonus maximum health.

Units neededEffect
1Paragon units: +15% attack damage, +40% maximum health.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

Spireborn race

The last citizens of the spire draw on its fading wards. All allies generate mana every second.

Duelist class

Blade-masters who thrive in the press. Each Duelist deals bonus damage for every enemy standing next to it.

Units neededEffect
3Duelist units: +10% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).

6Duelist units: +25% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
