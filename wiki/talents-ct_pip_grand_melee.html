Grand Melee is a talent of Pip Quickfingers, offered at player level 10 to the Brawler build.

Description

Free-for-All deals 50% more damage and stuns for 2.5 seconds.

What it changes

Free-for-All becomes Grand Melee.

  • The change takes effect from the next fight, and the upgraded form keeps the original's place in the casting order.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Grand Melee means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
