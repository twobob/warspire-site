Ember Duelist is a tier 3 (Rare) assassin unit. It costs 3 gold in the shop and carries the Ashen, Duelist and Cutthroat alliances.

Ability

Flicker Strikes

Flickers between enemies within 2 cells, striking each for physical damage.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.25 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies within 2 cells of itself.
  • Deals 150 / 225 / 325 physical damage to the targets (values per star).

Full details are on the Flicker Strikes ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Ashen race

The burnt martyrs of the spire. When an Ashen unit first falls, allies within 2 cells are healed for 250 and gain 10% bonus damage. With six Ashen, each one rises again once per combat with 40% of its health.

Units neededEffect
3When an Ashen unit dies (once per combat), it heals all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.
6Ashen units: one revival per combat at 40% of maximum health.
When an Ashen unit revives (once per combat), it heals itself and all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.

Duelist class

Blade-masters who thrive in the press. Each Duelist deals bonus damage for every enemy standing next to it.

Units neededEffect
3Duelist units: +10% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
6Duelist units: +25% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

Cutthroat class

Knife-work in the dark. Cutthroats gain a chance to critically strike, and a critical strike wounds the target, reducing its healing received for 3 seconds.

Units neededEffect
3Cutthroat units: +15% critical strike chance, critical strikes deal 325% damage instead of the usual 200%.
When a Cutthroat unit lands a critical strike, it applies Wounded (Cutthroat, 3 units) (-75% healing received) to the unit it hit for 3 seconds.
6Cutthroat units: +25% critical strike chance, critical strikes deal 400% damage instead of the usual 200%.
When a Cutthroat unit lands a critical strike, it applies Wounded (Cutthroat, 6 units) (-100% healing received) to the unit it hit for 3 seconds.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
