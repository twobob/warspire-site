Grand Melee is the upgraded form of Free-for-All, an ability of Pip Quickfingers. It takes the place of the original once the talent Grand Melee is chosen.

Description

Pip ropes every enemy into a wild brawl: each takes 50% more physical damage, three stacks of toxin and a 2.5 second stun.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 10 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 10.1 seconds apart. The first cast starts 10 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
0.1 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies.
  • Deals 75 (levels 1 to 5), then 112.5 / 150 / 187.5 / 225 / 262.5 (levels 6 to 10) physical damage to the targets.
  • Applies 3 stacks of Pip's Toxin to the targets for the rest of combat: 10 (levels 1 to 6), then 14 (levels 7 and 8), then 18 (levels 9 and 10) physical damage taken per second; stacks up to 10 times.
  • Applies Stunned (general) (cannot move, attack or cast) to the targets for 2.5 seconds.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 10.1 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 10.1 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 4 casts at most.
  • It has no cast range: any enemy on the board can be picked, however far away.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Applying it again

  • Pip's Toxin: each application adds a stack, up to 10, and every stack counts in full.
  • Stunned (general): it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
