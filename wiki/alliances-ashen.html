Ashen

A race alliance of 6 units with steps at 3 and 6.

Ashen

The burnt martyrs of the spire. When an Ashen unit first falls, allies within 2 cells are healed for 250 and gain 10% bonus damage. With six Ashen, each one rises again once per combat with 40% of its health.

Ashen is a race alliance carried by 6 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 3 and 6 different Ashen units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
3When an Ashen unit dies (once per combat), it heals all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.
6Ashen units: one revival per combat at 40% of maximum health.
When an Ashen unit revives (once per combat), it heals itself and all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
