Ironsight

A race alliance of 3 units, active with a single unit.

Gunsmith clans who never waste a shot on a healthy target. Every attack an Ironsight lands also strikes the most wounded enemy for bonus physical damage.

Ironsight is a race alliance carried by 3 units. It has one step, active as soon as one Ironsight unit is on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Units neededWhat it grants
1When an Ironsight unit lands an attack, it deals 35 / 70 / 140 physical damage to the enemy with the lowest share of health left (values per star).

Any one of the 3 different Ironsight units is enough to reach the only step. Ironsight Monocle makes no difference to this alliance: the item only counts once an Ironsight unit is on your board, and one unit already reaches the only step.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
