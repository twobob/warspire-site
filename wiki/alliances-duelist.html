Duelist

A class alliance of 8 units with steps at 3 and 6.

Blade-masters who thrive in the press. Each Duelist deals bonus damage for every enemy standing next to it.

Duelist is a class alliance carried by 8 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 3 and 6 different Duelist units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
3Duelist units: +10% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
6Duelist units: +25% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

There are 8 different Duelist units and the top step needs 6, so you can reach it with 2 units to spare. Duelist Token counts as 1 extra unit, so every step that needs more than one unit needs 1 unit fewer on your board. At least one real Duelist unit must be there for the item to count.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.
  • An item that counts as an extra Duelist unit only counts while at least one Duelist unit is on your board.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
