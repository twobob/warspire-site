Straw Decoy

Straw Decoy is a placeable planted on the board by the item Straw Decoy. It is not sold in the shop.

Straw Decoy is a placeable: an object that stands in a cell of the board. The unit holding Straw Decoy plants 1 at the start of combat, in the free cell nearest to it. It has 1,200 health, never moves and never attacks. See Summons and placeables.

Ability

Straw Decoy has no ability, no mana bar and no effect of its own. It stands in a cell, blocks the way and soaks up enemy attacks until it is destroyed.

Where it comes from

Straw Decoy is never offered in the shop. It is planted on the board by an item. See Summons and placeables for how summoned and placed units behave.

  • Item Straw Decoy
    • The unit holding the item plants 1 at the start of combat, in the free cell nearest to it.
A placeable does not keep your side in the fight: when your last fighting unit falls the round is lost and Straw Decoy collapses. If you win, it adds nothing to the damage dealt to the loser.

Alliances

Straw Decoy carries no alliance. It neither counts towards an alliance step nor gains a members' bonus.
