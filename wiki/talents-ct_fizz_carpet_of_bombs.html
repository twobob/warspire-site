Carpet of Bombs is a talent of Fizzwick Emberhorn, offered at player level 10 to the Bombardier build.

Description

Bomb Brigade drops twelve bombs.

What it changes

Bomb Brigade becomes Carpet of Bombs.

  • The change takes effect from the next fight, and the upgraded form keeps the original's place in the casting order.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Carpet of Bombs means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
