Gnawing Wight is a tier 3 (Rare) assassin unit. It costs 3 gold in the shop and carries the Wight, Ravager and Swarmkin alliances.

Ability

Gnaw

Gorges on its foes, gaining lifesteal and attack speed for 6 seconds.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
None: the effect comes at once.
Targets
Itself.
  • Applies Gorging (+40 / 60 / 80% lifesteal on attack damage, +30% attack speed) to itself for 6 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Gnaw ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Ravager class

Brutes who break their foes’ will. A Ravager’s attack maims the target, dealing physical damage and reducing the target’s damage for 4 seconds.

Units neededEffect
2When a Ravager unit lands an attack (at most once every 4 seconds), it deals 80 physical damage to the unit it hit and applies Maimed (Ravager, 2 units) (-30% attack damage) to the same target for 4 seconds.
4When a Ravager unit lands an attack (at most once every 4 seconds), it deals 120 physical damage to the unit it hit and applies Maimed (Ravager, 4 units) (-50% attack damage) to the same target for 4 seconds.

Swarmkin race

Chitinous things from the spire's foundations. At the start of combat and every 10 seconds, a random Swarmkin releases untargetable skitterlings that harry the enemy for 10 seconds; their bites weaken the target's damage.

Units neededEffect
2One random Swarmkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat and then every 10 seconds, the chosen unit summons 3 Skitterling units at star 1 on the nearest free cells; each lasts 10 seconds. The repeats stop when the chosen unit dies.
4One random Swarmkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat and then every 10 seconds, the chosen unit summons 6 Skitterling units at star 2 on the nearest free cells; each lasts 10 seconds. The repeats stop when the chosen unit dies.
