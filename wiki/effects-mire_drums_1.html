How status effects work

What it does

A unit with this effect: +20% attack speed.

This counts as a buff.

Stacking and duration

  • It does not stack. A unit carries one copy at a time. Applying it again renews it: the longer of the time left and the new duration is kept.

See How status effects work for how effects stack, renew and expire, and Stat reference for what each stat means.

Applied by

SourceType of sourceDurationHow
Mirefolk at 2 unitsAlliance stepThe rest of combat
At the start of combat, each Mirefolk unit applies War Drums to all allies (itself included). This does not stack: however many units apply it, each target has it once.

Related effects
