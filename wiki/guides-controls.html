Controls

Touch, mouse and keyboard controls.

Warspire is built touch first. Every action in the game is a tap or a drag on something you can see, so the whole game can be played with one finger or with the left mouse button alone. Keys are shortcuts for those same buttons and are never the only way to do anything.

Gestures

  • Tap: touch and lift without moving. A finger may wander a little further than a mouse before the tap stops counting.
  • Drag: press, move and let go. A press has to travel a short way before it becomes a drag, so a tap never moves a unit by accident.
  • Double-tap: two quick taps, at most 0.4 seconds apart. A double-click with the mouse does the same, and so does a right click.
  • Press and hold: about 0.45 seconds without moving. It opens the tooltip and cancels the tap, so holding a shop card to read it never buys it.
  • Only the first finger down counts during a drag. A second finger cannot take over or drop what you are carrying.
  • If the device interrupts a touch (a system gesture or an alert), the drag is undone and the unit goes back where it was.

During a fight

  • Your board is locked while your own fight is running. The shop closes when combat starts, but the shop button reopens it. You can still buy units to your bench, sell bench units, reroll, buy experience and lock the shop with the buttons. The buy keys (1 to 5), the shop key (Space) and the sell key (X) work in a fight too. The reroll, experience and lock keys (D, F and L) work only in preparation. What each phase allows is in Rounds and phases.
  • When your own fight ends before the round does, your board unlocks and a message says so. Changes you make then apply from the next round.
  • An item can be dropped on one of your fighting units. It is equipped at once and takes effect from the next round.
  • Tapping a fighting unit, yours or the enemy's, opens its card with its live health and mana. A quick move on a fighting unit opens the card too, since a fight cannot be rearranged.
  • While you watch another player's board everything is read only: taps show details and nothing can be moved, sold or equipped.

The camera

There is no camera to steer. The view frames the whole board and bench by itself and reframes when the window changes size, so there is nothing to pan, zoom or rotate and no control for it.

Keyboard shortcuts and rebinding

On a keyboard, open the menu, choose CONTROLS and then KEY BINDINGS. Tap a key on the list, then press the new key. Esc cancels. RESET beside a key restores that one and RESET TO DEFAULTS restores them all. Your keys are saved on your device.

  • Two actions may share a key. The screen marks them, and both react when the key is pressed.
  • The quick move is a gesture and cannot be moved to a key.
  • Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in a text box, such as your name.
  • Where there is no keyboard the game hides key hints and shows the touch wording instead.
