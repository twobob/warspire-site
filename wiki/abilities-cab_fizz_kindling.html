Kindling is the passive of Fizzwick Emberhorn. The commander has it in every build, from the moment the commander joins your board.

Description

Every enemy Fizzwick attacks is set on fire for 3 seconds, taking magical damage every second. He also regenerates 10 health per second.

Standing bonus

A standing bonus on the commander alone. It is in place for the whole fight.

  • +10 health regeneration per second

Triggers

  • When the commander lands an attack, it applies Burning to the unit it hit for 3 seconds: 40 (levels 1 to 5), then 48 / 56 / 64 / 72 / 80 (levels 6 to 10) magical damage taken per second.

These values grow with your player level, and the game uses your level at the moment the fight starts. Levels that share every value are one row. Everything else about the ability is the same at every level.

When it fires

  • This is a passive. It is never cast, costs nothing and has no cooldown.
  • Its triggered effects fire on their own when their condition is met.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Upgraded form

The talent Wildfire swaps this ability for Wildfire. It is offered at player level 8 to the Bombardier and Firestarter builds.

Wildfire: Every enemy Fizzwick attacks is set on fire for 6 seconds, taking magical damage every second. He also regenerates 10 health per second.

Everything that differs between the two forms. Anything not listed is the same in both.

Applying it again

  • Burning: it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
