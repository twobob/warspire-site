Sundered

A class alliance of 4 units with a step at 3.

Sundered
Kind

Touched by the nothing at the spire’s heart. Every allied unit’s attacks have a 40% chance to deal 4% of the target’s maximum health as pure damage, at most once every 0.5 seconds.

Sundered is a class alliance carried by 4 units. It has one step, reached with 3 different Sundered units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
3When an ally lands an attack (40% chance; at most once every 0.5 seconds), it deals pure damage to the unit it hit equal to 4% of the target's maximum health.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
