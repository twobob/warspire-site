Hollow Strider is a tier 4 (Epic) assassin unit. It costs 4 gold in the shop and carries the Sundered, Shade and Riftborn alliances.

Ability

Null Step

Steps out of reality behind the farthest enemy, dealing magical damage around it and shredding armour.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.25 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
The farthest enemy.
  • Leaps behind the target.
  • Deals 200 / 300 / 400 magical damage to all enemies within 1 cell of itself (values per star).
  • Applies Unmade (-10 / -15 / -20 armour) to the same targets for 5 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Null Step ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Sundered class

Touched by the nothing at the spire’s heart. Every allied unit’s attacks have a 40% chance to deal 4% of the target’s maximum health as pure damage, at most once every 0.5 seconds.

Units neededEffect
3When an ally lands an attack (40% chance; at most once every 0.5 seconds), it deals pure damage to the unit it hit equal to 4% of the target's maximum health.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Shade class

Shadow-walkers who are never quite where you strike. Shades gain evasion, and gain attack damage each time they evade an attack.

Units neededEffect
2Shade units: +20% evasion.
When a Shade unit evades an attack, it applies a stack of Riposte (Shade, 2 units) (+10 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
4Shade units: +40% evasion.
When a Shade unit evades an attack, it applies a stack of Riposte (Shade, 4 units) (+20 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.

Riftborn race

Things that crawled out of the cracks in the spire. When a Riftborn unit dies it leaves behind a Rift Echo that fights on. Echoes are not Riftborn.

Units neededEffect
2When a Riftborn unit dies, it summons a Rift Echo in the nearest free cell.
4When a Riftborn unit dies, it summons a Greater Rift Echo in the nearest free cell.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
