Total Chaos is a talent of Fizzwick Emberhorn, offered at player level 10 to the Firestarter build.

Description

Wild Abandon grants 50% more attack speed.

What it changes

Wild Abandon becomes Total Chaos.

  • The change takes effect from the next fight, and the upgraded form keeps the original's place in the casting order.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

Ability

AbilityTimingDescription
Before the talentWild AbandonCooldown 10 secondsFizzwick whips the crew into a frenzy: every ally gains attack speed for the rest of the combat, and Fizzwick's attacks reach the whole board.
With the talentTotal ChaosCooldown 10 secondsFizzwick whips the crew into a wild frenzy: every ally gains even more attack speed for the rest of the combat, and Fizzwick's attacks reach the whole board.

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Total Chaos means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
