Fizzwick Emberhorn

A cackling pyromaniac with a horn full of black powder. Fizzwick sets everything he hits on fire and is happiest when the whole board is burning.

Fizzwick Emberhorn is one of the 4 commanders. A commander fights on your board beside your units, grows with your player level and is shaped by the build and talents you choose.

Stats by player level

Fizzwick has no star rank. Each fight uses the row for your current player level, so buying experience makes the commander stronger as well as widening your board. Commanders are offered at round 10, so the first row only matters if your level is still low by then. What each number means is on the stat reference.

A commander has no mana bar. Its active abilities run on cooldowns instead, as Mana and casting explains.

Passive ability

Fizzwick has this ability whichever build you take.

Kindling

In the game's words: "Every enemy Fizzwick attacks is set on fire for 3 seconds, taking magical damage every second. He also regenerates 10 health per second."

Cast
Passive. It is never cast and works for the whole combat.
  • The commander: +10 health regeneration per second.
  • When the commander lands an attack, it applies Burning to the unit it hit for 3 seconds: 40 (levels 1 to 5), then 48 / 56 / 64 / 72 / 80 (levels 6 to 10) magical damage taken per second.

Core ability

Whichever build you take, Fizzwick also has this ability.

Kaboom Bolt

In the game's words: "Fizzwick hurls a fireball at his target. It bursts on the target and every enemy within 1 cell, splitting its magical damage evenly among them and setting them all on fire for 5 seconds."

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 12 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 13 seconds apart. The first cast starts 6 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.

How Fizzwick plays in a match

These rules are the same for every commander, apart from where Fizzwick stands and which items Fizzwick may hold.

  • Choosing. In the preparation phase of round 10 you are shown all 4 commanders. You pick the commander and one of its builds together. That preparation phase lasts 10 seconds longer to give you time. If the fight starts before you have chosen, you are given the first commander shown, with its first build.
  • On the board. Fizzwick attacks from range, so the commander arrives in the back row, as near the centre as there is room. You may move it to any other cell of the board, but never to the bench, and it cannot be sold. It does not use up one of your unit places and it does not count toward any alliance. It can hold an item, with these exceptions. A commander has no mana bar, so it cannot hold the items that need a mana ability: Runescribed Quill, Cinder Core, Hourglass Prism, Renewal Orb, Archmage Codex. Fizzwick counts as ranged, so it cannot hold the melee only item Sweeping Warblade.
  • Levelling. The commander is always the same level as you, up to level 10. Its stats and the strength of its abilities are read from that level at the start of each fight. At player levels 4, 6, 8 and 10 you choose a talent during preparation. If several are waiting, they are offered lowest level first, and any you have not chosen when the fight starts is settled for you with the first option.
  • When it falls. If Fizzwick falls, the commander is out for the rest of that fight only and stands again, at full health, in the next round. Losing the commander costs you nothing beyond its help in the fight. A commander that is still standing when you win adds 1 to the damage your opponent takes, or 2 from round 31, whatever its level.

The full rules are on How commanders work.
