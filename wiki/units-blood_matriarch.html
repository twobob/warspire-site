Blood Matriarch is a tier 5 (Legendary) bruiser unit. It costs 5 gold in the shop and carries the Oathbound, Feral and Duelist alliances. It is an ace.

Ace effect

Blood Matriarch is the ace of the Oathbound alliance. Its ace effect is on while it stands on the board and at least one step of that alliance is active. See Aces.

In the game's own words: Ace: when the Blood Matriarch dies, a random other Oathbound unit gains +1000% attack damage.

In play, the effect applies to Blood Matriarch itself:

  • When this unit dies, it applies Matriarch’s Bequest (+1,000% attack damage) to one random other allied Oathbound unit for the rest of combat.

The damage bonus raises attack damage only. Damage from abilities is not changed. See Damage, armour and resistance.

When your shop rolls a tier 5 unit, each unit of that tier is weighted by the copies of it left in the pool. While that alliance is active for you, the weight of Blood Matriarch in that draw is 15% higher. See The shop.

Alliances

Oathbound race

Sworn in blood to one another. When an Oathbound unit dies, every other Oathbound unit gains +125 attack damage for the rest of the combat.

Feral race

Feral beasts of the ash wastes grow stronger with every blow. Affected units gain attack damage each time they land an attack, for the rest of the combat.

Duelist class

Blade-masters who thrive in the press. Each Duelist deals bonus damage for every enemy standing next to it.
