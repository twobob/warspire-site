The Crypt is a graveyard at night. The board is paved in slate that the moon turns blue, set in dark earth strewn with fallen leaves. Rows of headstones and crosses, each over a low mound, fill both sides and the ground beyond the far wall.

A dark wall with a gate crosses the far side, and bare twisted trees stand at the edges. Tall iron candelabra flank the board, fire bowls burn warm at its corners, and patches of cold green light lie among the graves.

Light and weather

Crypt arrives in the Moonlit mood. A bright cold moon over dark ground, so the board stays easy to read.

In the air: pale wisps rising among the graves.

The mood is only the starting light. The SCENERY row on the title screen relights the arena as Dusk, Day, Night, Lantern Festival, First Frost, Glass Storm, Aurora Night or Blood Moon instead.

From above

Rules

Arenas change the look of a match and never its rules. Every arena has the same board (8 by 4 cells for each side) and the same bench of 8 slots, and the match itself does not know which arena anyone is looking at. Nothing in the scenery blocks movement, gives cover or changes a number: combat, placement and gold work the same everywhere.

Playing here

You choose your own arena. On the title screen, before you press Find Match, the ARENA row has an arrow on each side: tap them to step through the 5 arenas and a last choice, RANDOM. The board behind the menu changes at once, so you see each one before you commit. A new player starts in Courtyard.

With RANDOM chosen, a different arena is rolled each time the title screen opens, which means once per match, and, while the game stays open, never the same one twice in a row. The row then shows which arena the roll picked.

The choice belongs to you alone. It is saved on your device and is never sent to the other players, so all 8 players in a match may each be looking at a different arena. Whichever you pick, you see every fight of the match there, your own and the ones you watch.

The title screen is described in The interface.
