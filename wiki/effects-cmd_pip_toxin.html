How status effects work

What it does

A unit with this effect: 10 to 18 physical damage taken per second. The values depend on player level. The table gives each one.

Values by player level
Levels 1 to 6Levels 7 and 8Levels 9 and 10
10 physical damage taken per second14 physical damage taken per second18 physical damage taken per second

This counts as a debuff, because it harms the unit that carries it.

Stacking and duration

  • Each new application adds a stack, up to 10 stacks. Every stack adds the full values again, and each application also renews the duration. The per stack values of the newest application are used for every stack, even when they are weaker than the old ones.

See How status effects work for how effects stack, renew and expire, and Stat reference for what each stat means.
