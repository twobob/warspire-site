Stoneborn

A race alliance of 5 units with steps at 2 and 4.

Stoneborn
Kind
Race
Units
5
Steps
2 / 4

Gargoyles and golems carved from the spire itself, hardened by every battle. Each Stoneborn unit permanently gains maximum health for every enemy it has killed this game, more at higher stars.

Stoneborn is a race alliance carried by 5 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 2 and 4 different Stoneborn units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2Stoneborn units: +10 / 20 / 30 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +600 / 1,200 / 1,800 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).
4Stoneborn units: +40 / 80 / 120 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +2,400 / 4,800 / 7,200 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
