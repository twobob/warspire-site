Jade Drakeling is a tier 2 (Uncommon) caster unit. It costs 2 gold in the shop and carries the Wyrmblood and Arcanist alliances.

Ability

Jade Breath

Breathes a cone of jade flame in a line, dealing magical damage to every enemy in it.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.4 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Cast range
Its target must be within 3 cells.
Targets
Every enemy in a line from it through its current target, 3 cells long.
  • Deals 200 / 300 / 450 magical damage to the targets (values per star).

Full details are on the Jade Breath ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Wyrmblood race

Heirs of the old wyrms. With two Wyrmblood, each of their spells also breathes fire on the target and every enemy within 1 cell, dealing magical damage and scorching them.

Units neededEffect
2When a Wyrmblood unit casts an ability, it deals 150 / 250 / 400 magical damage to the enemy its ability was cast on (its current target if there is none) and all enemies within 1 cell of it and applies Scorched (alliances) (30 / 50 / 80 magical damage taken per second) to the same targets for 4 seconds (values per star).

Arcanist class

Scholars of the broken wards. All enemies lose magic resistance, so they take more magical damage.
