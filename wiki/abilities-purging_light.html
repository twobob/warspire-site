Purging Light

The ability of Grey Confessor: heals and removes status effects. It targets the 2 / 2 / 3 allies with the lowest share of health left (itself included).

In game: Bathes the most wounded allies in purging light, healing them and removing their debuffs.

Purging Light is the ability of Grey Confessor.

What it does

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
The 2 / 2 / 3 allies with the lowest share of health left (itself included; values per star).
  • Heals the targets for 350 / 600 / 1,000 health (values per star).
  • Removes all timed debuffs from the targets.

How the cast works

  • Grey Confessor needs 60 mana to cast Purging Light. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It picks the 2 / 2 / 3 allies with the smallest share of their health left (by star level). The caster itself can be chosen. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
