Briar Shaper is a tier 3 (Rare) support unit. It costs 3 gold in the shop and carries the Grovekeeper and Watcher alliances.

Ability

Briar Blessing

Wraps the two most wounded allies in living bark, healing them and granting bonus armour for 4 seconds.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
The 2 allies with the lowest share of health left (itself included).
  • Heals the targets for 250 / 375 / 500 health (values per star).
  • Applies Living Bark (+5 / 8 / 12 armour) to the targets for 4 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Briar Blessing ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Grovekeeper class

Keepers of the hanging gardens who share the grove's strength. At the start of combat, one Grovekeeper (two with four) grows into a stronger form with double health and damage.

Watcher class

Lookouts who punish every spell they see. With two Watchers, Watchers deal 20% bonus damage; with four, every allied unit does, 25%.
