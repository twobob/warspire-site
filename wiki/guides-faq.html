Questions and answers

Short answers to common questions.

Economy

How much gold do I get each round?

After every fight you are paid, in this order: 1 gold if you won, interest, streak gold, and the round's base income of 7 gold, except 5 gold on rounds 1 and 2. Items such as Tithe Purse and Merchant's Compass add more. You also gain 1 experience. Details are on Gold and income.

Why did I not get interest?

Interest is 1 gold for every 10 gold, up to 3, and it is counted on the gold you hold at the moment the fight starts (plus the victory gold you have just won). Gold you spend or gain after that moment does not change it. You also get nothing if you are already at the gold cap of 100. See Gold and income.

Is there a limit to how much gold I can hold?

Yes: 100 gold. Any income or sale that would take you past it is lost, so spend before you reach it. See Gold and income.

How do win and lose streaks pay?

For wins, 3 or 4 in a row pays 1 gold, 5 or 6 pays 2, 7 pays 3 and 8 pays 4; once win 8 in a row has been paid the streak starts again from nothing. For losses, 3 or 4 in a row pays 1 gold and 5 or more pays 2. A win ends a lose streak and a loss ends a win streak. A draw changes neither, and a lost round in which you took no damage does not lengthen a lose streak. See Gold and income.

How do I level up, and what does a level give me?

You gain 1 experience after every round and can buy 5 more for 5 gold at any time. Each level raises your unit cap and shifts the shop toward higher tiers.

How much gold do I get back when I sell a unit?

The price of one copy, plus 2 gold if it is two-star or 4 gold if it is three-star. A two-star tier 1 unit sells for 3 gold, against the 3 gold its 3 copies cost; a two-star tier 5 unit sells for 7 gold, against the 15 gold its 3 copies cost. An upgraded unit does not return what was paid for it. Every copy inside the unit goes back to the shared pool, and an item it held returns to your inventory. See Star upgrades.

Shop

When does the shop change?

The shop rolls 5 new units after every round, unless you have locked it, and whenever you reroll for 2 gold. A slot you bought from stays empty until the next roll. See The shop.

How do I keep a shop for the next round?

Lock it. A locked shop is not replaced when the round ends, and the lock stays on until you turn it off, so remember to unlock it. A reroll still replaces a locked shop. See The shop.

When is a reroll free?

Losing a round gives you one free reroll. It waits until you use it, even over several rounds, but you never hold more than one. Recruiter's Ledger also changes the price of every reroll. See The shop.

Why do I never see tier 5 units?

The shop cannot roll tier 5 before level 8, or level 7 with Gilded Invitation, which makes the shop roll as if you were 1 level higher. The chance for each shop slot is 3% at level 8, 6% at level 9 and 10% at level 10. The full table is on The shop, and Shop odds calculator works out the chance of a single unit.

What happens when two players want the same unit?

All players buy from one shared pool that holds 30 copies of each tier 1 unit, 20 copies of each tier 2 unit, 18 copies of each tier 3 unit, 12 copies of each tier 4 unit and 10 copies of each tier 5 unit. Every copy another player holds is one fewer for you, and a unit with fewer copies left shows up less often. A unit in your shop is not reserved: if the last copy is bought elsewhere first, your purchase fails. Copies return when a unit is sold or its owner is eliminated. See The shared pool.

Why has a unit stopped appearing in my shop?

Either the pool has run out of copies, or you already own it at 3 stars. A unit you hold at the highest star is never offered to you again, and any copies of it in your shop disappear the moment you reach that star. See Star upgrades.

Can a reroll show me the same units again?

A paid or free reroll avoids the units that were in the shop it replaces, as long as other units of the same tier still have copies. The roll at the start of a round has no such rule. See The shop.

Can I buy a unit when my bench is full?

Only when the purchase completes a combine: if you already own 2 one-star copies, the new copy merges straight into them. Otherwise you need a free bench slot. See Board and bench.

How many copies do I need for two and three stars?

3 copies of a unit make a two-star unit, and 3 two-star units make a three-star unit, so 9 copies in all. Combining is automatic and uses copies on the bench and the board; while your own fight is running only bench copies combine, and board copies join in once it is over. See Star upgrades and compare the numbers in Unit stat table.

Combat

How is the damage to me worked out?

When you lose, you take the round number divided by 10, rounded down, plus damage for each enemy unit still standing: 1 plus its power level divided by 3 (the power level is its tier plus 2 per extra star), rounded down. A surviving commander adds 1, or 2 from round 31. Summons and placeables add nothing. Example: in round 25, losing to a surviving two-star tier 3 unit and a one-star tier 1 unit costs 2 + 2 + 1 = 5 health. See Player damage and elimination.

What does a ghost opponent mean?

When an odd number of players are alive, one of them cannot be paired, so that player fights a copy of another living player's board, chosen at random. You win gold or lose health as usual. The player who was copied is not involved: they neither gain nor lose anything from your fight. See Matchups.

Why did my unit not cast its ability?

Check these in turn. Its mana bar must be completely full. A silenced or stunned unit cannot cast, and a silence or stun that lands during the cast point cancels the cast. Some abilities need an enemy within their cast range, so the unit keeps attacking until one is close enough. And a unit that dies with a full bar was simply too late. See Mana and casting.

Can I move units during a fight?

Not on the board while your own fight is running. You can still buy, sell from the bench, reroll, buy experience and move items. As soon as your own fight ends your board unlocks, even if other fights are still going, and anything you change applies from the next round. See Board and bench.

What happens when a fight runs out of time?

From 35 seconds, overtime makes all damage stronger and all healing weaker every second, and burns every unit for a growing share of its health. If both sides still stand at 45 seconds, the fight stops: both players count as having lost, and each takes damage from the other's surviving units. See Time limit and overtime.

What if both sides die at the same moment?

That is a draw. Nobody takes damage and nobody earns victory gold. After one side is wiped out the fight runs on for 1 more second, so effects that are still burning can turn a win into a draw. See Player damage and elimination.

How does a unit choose whom to attack?

It takes the closest enemy it can reach, counting diagonal cells as one step. Between enemies at the same distance, a melee unit prefers one in a straight line, and then the one with less health. It stays on that target until the target dies, becomes untargetable or can no longer be reached. See Triggers and targeting.

How many units can I field?

As many as your level, and never fewer than 3. Veteran's Charter raises the cap. You may place more than that while preparing, but when the fight starts the units you touched most recently go back to the bench until you are at the cap; if the bench is full, the cheapest extra units are sold. Your commander does not use a slot. See Board and bench.

Do summons count for anything after the fight?

A summon keeps your side alive while it stands, so it can win you the round, but it deals no damage to the losing player and never counts toward an alliance. Placeables do not even keep a side alive. See Summons and placeables.

Alliances

Do bench units count for alliances?

No. Only units on the board count. The alliance panel shows bench units separately so you can see what placing them would add. See How alliances count.

Do two copies of the same unit count twice?

No. An alliance counts different units, so two copies of one unit on the board count once. See How alliances count.

Do alliance steps add together?

No. Each step lists the whole bonus for that step, and only the highest step you have reached applies. Alliance matrix shows the steps of every alliance.

Does my commander or a summon count toward an alliance?

No. Commanders, summons and placeables never count. See How alliances count.

Who receives an alliance bonus?

It depends on the alliance and the step. Most bonuses go to the members of the alliance on your board; some go to all your units, to one random member, or weaken every enemy. Each alliance page states it, starting from Alliances.

Can an item count as an alliance member?

Yes. 9 global items add to the count of one alliance, for example Oathbound Insignia, Venomist Sigil and Cutthroat Badge. The extra count only applies while at least one real member of that alliance is on your board. See Loot and items.

Items

When do I get items?

In loot rounds: rounds 1, 4, then round 8 and every 4 rounds after it. You choose 1 of 3 items and may reroll the offer once. The offer is tier 1 from round 1, tier 2 from round 8, tier 3 from round 16, tier 4 from round 24 and tier 5 from round 32. If you have not chosen when the fight starts, one of the offered items is taken for you at random. 27 items are never offered again once you own one. See Loot and items.

How many items can a unit hold?

One. Dropping a second item on a unit sends the first back to your inventory, and dragging an item from one unit onto another swaps them. See Loot and items.

Can I sell an item?

No. Items cannot be sold or thrown away. See Loot and items.

What happens to an item when its unit is sold or combined?

When you sell the unit, the item returns to your inventory. When units combine, the upgraded unit keeps one item and any others return to your inventory. See Loot and items.

What is a global item?

A global item works for your whole warband as soon as you take it. It is never equipped and does not use inventory space. Item table lists them all.

Why did I not get an item offer?

Your inventory holds 12 unequipped items. If it is full when a loot round starts, there is no offer that round, so equip items before then. See Loot and items.

Can I move items during a fight?

Yes. You can equip, swap and unequip at any time. The fight in progress was set up before your change, so the change takes effect next round. See Loot and items.

Why can I not equip an item on a unit?

A unit refuses an item for one of these reasons:

  • The item is for melee units only, such as Sweeping Warblade.
  • The item is for ranged units only, such as Hunter's Edge and Longshot Scope.
  • The item needs a holder that casts an ability with mana, such as Runescribed Quill and Cinder Core. A commander never has one, so it can never hold these.
  • The item is a global item. Those are never equipped: they already work for the whole warband.

Each item's rule is in the Effect column of the item table.

Commanders

When do I get a commander?

In the preparation of round 10. You are offered 4 commanders, and that preparation is 10 seconds longer. If you do not choose, the first commander offered is taken for you. See How commanders work.

Does my commander use a unit slot?

No. The commander stands on the board without counting toward your unit cap. It cannot be sold or moved to the bench, and it can hold an item like any unit. See How commanders work.

How does my commander get stronger?

Its health and abilities grow with your player level. At player levels 4, 6, 8 and 10 you also choose one of the talents your build offers; if you have not chosen by the time the fight starts, the first is taken for you. See Talents and Commanders.

Does a commander use mana?

No. Commander abilities are passive or run on cooldowns, and a cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned. See How commanders work.

Matches

How many players are in a match?

8. Seats that no player takes are filled by bots when the match starts. Everyone begins with 100 health and 5 gold at level 1. See Matchmaking and lobbies and Bots.

How does a match end?

When one player is left standing. A match that is still going after round 60 ends there: the player with the most health wins and the rest are ranked by health. See How a match works.

How long do I have to prepare?

30 seconds, except 20 seconds on round 1 and 15 seconds on rounds 2 and 3. A loot round adds 10 seconds. The fight starts sooner if every connected player presses ready. See Rounds and phases.

Who places higher when two players are knocked out in the same round?

The one with more health left after the round. Health can go below zero, so the player who was beaten harder places lower. See Player damage and elimination.

Will I fight the same player twice in a row?

It is unlikely. Pairs are drawn at random, but an opponent from your last round is drawn with only 5% of the usual weight, and one from the round before with 25%. When few players remain, repeats cannot be avoided. See Matchups.

What happens to my units when I am eliminated?

Every copy you held goes back to the shared pool, so the players still in the match can find those units again. Your final board is kept for the standings. See After the match.
