Skitterling is a summoned unit. It is never sold in the shop: it appears during combat and is gone when the fight ends. See Summons and placeables. It carries the Conjurer alliance.

Ability

Skitterling has no ability to cast and no mana bar. What it does is listed under its unit effect.

Unit effect

Skitterling carries an effect of its own, apart from any ability. It is always on. See Triggers and targeting for how such effects fire.

  • At the start of combat, this unit applies Skittering (cannot be targeted by enemies) to itself for the rest of combat.
  • When this unit lands an attack, it applies Bitten (-20 / -40 / -40% attack damage) to the unit it hit for 1 second (values per star).

Seconds per attack is the time from the start of one attack to the start of the next, before any attack speed bonus. Average damage per second is the middle of the damage range divided by the seconds per attack, before armour, bonuses and critical strikes.

Nothing in the game creates Skitterling at any other star level, so only 1 and 2 stars are shown.

Its health and damage are the same at every star level.

Where it comes from

Skitterling is never offered in the shop. It is created during combat by an alliance. It appears in the free cell nearest to whatever created it. See Summons and placeables for how summoned and placed units behave.

  • Alliance Swarmkin
    • At the 2 unit step, one random Swarmkin unit calls 3 at 1 star at the start of combat and again every 10 seconds while it lives; each lasts 10 seconds.
    • At the 4 unit step, one random Swarmkin unit calls 3 at 2 stars at the start of combat and again every 10 seconds while it lives; each lasts 10 seconds.
    • Only the highest step you have reached is in force: a higher step replaces a lower one.

Alliances

Skitterling is a summoned unit. Summoned units never count towards an alliance step, but a summon that carries an alliance receives the bonuses that alliance gives to its members. See How alliances count.

Conjurer class

Callers of things from beyond the spire. Every summoned ally deals 20% more damage (40% with four Conjurers). Conjurers share the bonus.
