Damage calculator

Damage after armour and magic resistance.

Work out what a hit does after armour, magic resistance and damage reduction. Type your own numbers, or pick real units or commanders to fill the fields with their stats at a star or a player level. Picking one only fills the fields: you can still change any of them, for example to add the armour of an item.

How it is worked out

The calculator uses the same formulas as the game:

  • Physical: damage × (1 - (0.052 × armour) / (0.9 + 0.048 × |armour|))
  • Magical: damage × (1 - magic resistance / 100), with magic resistance counted up to 100
  • Both are then multiplied by (1 - damage reduction / 100)
  • Pure: the damage is not reduced at all
  • Overtime: from 35 seconds into a fight the damage is first multiplied by (1 + overtime bonus / 100). This applies to every type, pure damage included. The bonus is worked out from the seconds you enter: it starts at 15% and grows by 15% every second, up to 150%

Damage never goes below zero. Hits to kill is the target's health divided by the damage of one hit, rounded up. It assumes every hit is the same, with no healing, shields, dodges or critical hits, and that every hit lands at the time you entered: in a real fight the overtime bonus keeps growing between hits, and overtime also drains health on its own.

What to enter

  • Damage before defences. For a basic attack this is the roll plus bonus attack damage, raised by any bonus damage and by a critical hit. For an ability it is the amount on the ability's page. See the order of a hit.
  • Armour and magic resistance. The unit's own value plus everything it gains from items, alliances and effects, and minus anything enemies strip. The sources are listed on the damage rules page.
  • Damage reduction. Several sources multiply. Two sources of 20% are 36%, not 40%. See damage reduction.
  • Health. The target's maximum health, with bonus health if it has any.
  • Seconds into the fight. Leave it at 0 for a hit before overtime. From 35 seconds on, the calculator adds the overtime damage bonus of that moment. A fight ends at 45 seconds at the latest.
