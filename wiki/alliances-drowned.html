Folk of the flooded undercity, slick with black water. All allies gain magic resistance.

Drowned is a race alliance carried by 5 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 2 and 4 different Drowned units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2All allies: +30% magic resistance.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
4All allies: +55% magic resistance.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

Aces

This unit is an ace tied to Drowned. An ace's extra effect is switched on while the ace is on your board and Drowned has at least one step active. See Aces. While Drowned is active, each copy of this ace left in the pool counts 15% heavier when the shop rolls a tier 5 unit, so the ace turns up a little more often.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
