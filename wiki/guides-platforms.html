Platforms and settings

Browser, Windows, quality levels and options.

Warspire runs in a web browser and as a Windows program. Both are the same game on the same servers, so browser and Windows players meet in the same matches.

The controls are the same everywhere: see Controls.

How the browser version finds its server

You do not type a server address. The browser version reads it from the address of the page: a page link can carry the address of the game server to join, and a link without one uses the server built into that copy of the game. Use the link you were given and Find Match does the rest.

If the server cannot be reached, the title screen says why and offers Retry. If you drop out of a running match, it offers Reconnect and puts you back in your seat. The browser keeps its connection while the tab is in the background. See Matchmaking and lobbies.

Quality levels

Quality on the VIDEO tab has three levels. A change applies at once, in the middle of a match if you like. It changes only how the game is drawn. It never changes what happens in a fight.

If fights stutter, drop one level first. Low draws the softest picture with the fewest particles, which is the largest saving.

Video options

  • Display mode (Windows): FULLSCREEN is a borderless window covering the monitor, quick to switch away from. EXCLUSIVE lets the game take over the monitor. WINDOWED is a resizable window.
  • Resolution and Monitor (Windows): step through the sizes that fit and the monitors attached.
  • APPLY (Windows) tries the display mode, resolution and monitor together. A box then asks whether to keep them; if you do not answer within 15 seconds they revert by themselves, so a bad choice cannot leave you with a black screen.
  • Fullscreen (browser): one switch that fills the screen with the game. Esc leaves it.
  • VSync: matches frames to the monitor's refresh rate, which removes tearing.
  • Frame-rate cap: 30, 60, 120, 144, Unlimited. It can be chosen only while VSync is off; with VSync on, the monitor sets the pace.

The interface scales with the window, so every panel stays on screen at any size. On Windows a window that is too tall for the desktop is shrunk to fit shortly after the game starts.

Audio options

The AUDIO tab has three sliders, each shown as a percentage: Master scales everything, Music the menu, preparation and combat music, and Effects the sounds of the fight and the interface.

Game options

  • Damage numbers: the numbers that float off units as they are hit. See Damage, armour and resistance.
  • Damage meter: the DAMAGE list at the top of the right column (The interface).
  • Hide inactive alliances in combat: off at first. When on, the alliances list shows only active alliances during a fight; preparation always shows them all (How alliances count).

Accessibility

Colour-blind mode offers three palettes besides the standard colours: Deuteranopia (green-weak), Protanopia (red-weak) and Tritanopia (blue-yellow-weak). In each, allies are blue and enemies orange, and a preview under the switch shows the tier colours and both sides.

Colour is never the only cue in these modes: tiers carry their numbers, progress pips change shape, and the selected unit's outline pulses. Information that a mouse reaches by hovering is always reachable by press and hold or a tap, so nothing is hidden from touch players.

Phones, tablets and safe areas

The interface is laid out touch first, and it respects the safe area a device reports: buttons, lists and text stay clear of a notch, a camera cut-out and the home bar, while backgrounds still run to the edge of the glass. The camera frames the board inside the same area, so the bench is never under a rounded corner.

Small buttons get an invisible larger touch area on handheld screens, so they are comfortable to hit without changing how they look. On Windows touch screens and in the browser, touches are treated like the mouse and every gesture works the same.

Saved settings

Settings are saved on the device you play on, as soon as you change them. There is no save button, and they are not tied to an account:

  • Your player name, and your place in a running match so that Reconnect can find it
  • Your arena, scenery mood and the hero on the title screen (Arenas)
  • The three volumes
  • Damage numbers, damage meter and the inactive alliances switch
  • Colour-blind mode
  • Key bindings (Controls)
  • Quality level, VSync and frame-rate cap, and on Windows the display mode, resolution and monitor

In the browser they are kept by that browser for the game's site. Clearing the site's data, or using another browser or device, starts again from the defaults. Where a browser refuses to store anything (some private windows), the game still runs and your choices last until the page closes. On Windows they are kept for your Windows user.

Nothing about your match is saved this way. Gold, units and standings live in the match itself; see How a match works.
