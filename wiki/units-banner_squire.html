Banner Squire is a tier 2 (Uncommon) bruiser unit. It costs 2 gold in the shop and carries the Spireborn, Duelist and Watcher alliances.

Ability

Rallying Banner

Plants the spire’s banner, granting itself and adjacent allies bonus damage and armour for 8 seconds.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Itself and all allies within 1 cell.
  • Applies Rallied (units) (+20 / 30 / 40% attack damage, +5 / 8 / 12 armour) to the targets for 8 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Rallying Banner ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Spireborn race

The last citizens of the spire draw on its fading wards. All allies generate mana every second.

Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

6All allies: +15 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Duelist class

Blade-masters who thrive in the press. Each Duelist deals bonus damage for every enemy standing next to it.

Units neededEffect
3Duelist units: +10% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
6Duelist units: +25% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

Watcher class

Lookouts who punish every spell they see. With two Watchers, Watchers deal 20% bonus damage; with four, every allied unit does, 25%.

Units neededEffect

The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
