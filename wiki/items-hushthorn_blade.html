Hushthorn Blade

Legendary offensive item. The holder: +70 attack damage. When the holder lands an attack (at most once every 10 seconds), it applies Silenced (cannot cast abilities) to the unit it hit for 5 seconds.

That is the wording on the item's card. The sections below are worked out from the item itself and say what happens in play.

As a legendary item (tier 5) it is offered on round 32 and every 4 rounds after that. See Loot and items for how offers work.

What it does

The holder: +70 attack damage.
When the holder lands an attack (at most once every 10 seconds), it applies Silenced (items) (cannot cast abilities) to the unit it hit for 5 seconds.

A triggered effect needs its holder in the fight: nothing fires from the bench, and nothing fires after the holder has died. Triggers and targeting explains how triggers pick their targets.

Who can carry it

Any unit you own can hold Hushthorn Blade, on the board or on the bench. A unit holds one item at a time.

Equipping it from your inventory sends the unit's current item back to the inventory. Dragging it from one unit to another swaps the two items; the swap is refused if either item would end up on a unit that cannot hold it. See Loot and items.

Your commander can hold it too.

An item does nothing while its holder sits on the bench. Selling the holder returns the item to your inventory.

There is no limit on copies, though an offer shows items you do not own yet before it repeats one.
