Wild Abandon is the ultimate of Fizzwick Emberhorn in the Firestarter build. Choosing that build is the only way to get it.

Description

Fizzwick whips the crew into a frenzy: every ally gains attack speed for the rest of the combat, and Fizzwick's attacks reach the whole board.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 10 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 12.3 seconds apart. The first cast starts 10 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
2.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All allies (the commander included).
  • Applies Wild Abandon to the targets for the rest of combat: +35% (levels 1 to 5), then 45 / 55 / 70 / 80 / 90% (levels 6 to 10) attack speed.
  • Applies Long Fuse (+8 attack range in cells) to the commander for the rest of combat.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 12.3 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 12.3 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 3 casts at most.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Upgraded form

The talent Total Chaos swaps this ability for Total Chaos. It is offered at player level 10 to the Firestarter build.

Total Chaos: Fizzwick whips the crew into a wild frenzy: every ally gains even more attack speed for the rest of the combat, and Fizzwick's attacks reach the whole board.

Applying it again

  • Wild Abandon: it does not stack and it already lasts the rest of the fight, so a second application on the same unit changes nothing.
  • Long Fuse: it does not stack and it already lasts the rest of the fight, so a second application on the same unit changes nothing.
