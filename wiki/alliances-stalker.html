Stalker

A class alliance of 8 units with steps at 3 and 6.

Hunters of the ruins. Each landed attack has a chance to fire a quick volley of extra attacks at the same target.

Stalker is a class alliance carried by 8 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 3 and 6 different Stalker units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
3When a Stalker unit lands an attack (30% chance), it launches 2 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).
6When a Stalker unit lands an attack (38% chance), it launches 3 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

There are 8 different Stalker units and the top step needs 6, so you can reach it with 2 units to spare. Stalker Crest counts as 1 extra unit, so every step that needs more than one unit needs 1 unit fewer on your board. At least one real Stalker unit must be there for the item to count.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.
  • An item that counts as an extra Stalker unit only counts while at least one Stalker unit is on your board.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
