Artificer

A class alliance of 5 units with steps at 2 and 4.

Artificer
Kind
Class
Units
5
Steps
2 / 4

Their contraptions are rigged to blow. When an Artificer dies it explodes, dealing magical damage equal to a share of its maximum health to all enemies within 1 cell.

Artificer is a class alliance carried by 5 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 2 and 4 different Artificer units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2When an Artificer unit dies, it deals magical damage to all enemies within 1 cell of itself equal to 10% of its own maximum health.
4When an Artificer unit dies, it deals magical damage to all enemies within 1 cell of itself equal to 20% of its own maximum health.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
