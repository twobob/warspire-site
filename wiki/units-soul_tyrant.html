Soul Tyrant is a tier 5 (Legendary) caster unit. It costs 5 gold in the shop and carries the Wight and Soulbinder alliances. It is an ace.

Ability

Soul Harvest

Rips at the souls of all enemies within 3 cells, dealing magical damage and silencing them.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.

0.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies within 3 cells of itself.
  • Deals 200 / 325 / 450 magical damage to the targets (values per star).
  • Applies Silenced (units) (cannot cast abilities) to the targets for 2 / 3 / 4 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Soul Harvest ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Ace effect

Soul Tyrant is the ace of the Soulbinder alliance. Its ace effect is on while it stands on the board and at least one step of that alliance is active. See Aces.

In the game's own words: Ace: casting a spell links the Soul Tyrant and the most wounded ally for 6 seconds, both healing for 200% of the damage they deal.

In play, the effect applies to Soul Tyrant itself:

  • When this unit casts an ability, it applies Tyrant’s Link (+200% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself for 6 seconds.
  • When this unit casts an ability, it applies Tyrant’s Link (+200% lifesteal on attack damage) to the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 6 seconds.

The healing is lifesteal: it heals from the damage of attacks only, not from abilities. See lifesteal and healing.

When your shop rolls a tier 5 unit, each unit of that tier is weighted by the copies of it left in the pool. While that alliance is active for you, the weight of Soul Tyrant in that draw is 15% higher. See The shop.
