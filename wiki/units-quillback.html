Quillback is a tier 2 (Uncommon) tank unit. It costs 2 gold in the shop and carries the Stoneborn, Feral and Grovekeeper alliances.

Ability

Gravel Volley

Sprays sharp gravel at all enemies within 2 cells, dealing physical damage.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.1 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies within 2 cells of itself.
  • Deals 60 / 100 / 160 physical damage to the targets (values per star).

Full details are on the Gravel Volley ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Stoneborn race

Gargoyles and golems carved from the spire itself, hardened by every battle. Each Stoneborn unit permanently gains maximum health for every enemy it has killed this game, more at higher stars.

Units neededEffect
2Stoneborn units: +10 / 20 / 30 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +600 / 1,200 / 1,800 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).
4Stoneborn units: +40 / 80 / 120 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +2,400 / 4,800 / 7,200 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).

Feral race

Feral beasts of the ash wastes grow stronger with every blow. Affected units gain attack damage each time they land an attack, for the rest of the combat.

Units neededEffect
2When a Feral unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 2 units) (+5 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
4When a Feral unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 4 units) (+8 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
6When an ally lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 6 units) (+13 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Grovekeeper class

Keepers of the hanging gardens who share the grove's strength. At the start of combat, one Grovekeeper (two with four) grows into a stronger form with double health and damage.

Units neededEffect
2One random Grovekeeper unit is picked at the start of combat.
The chosen unit: +100% maximum health, +100% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
42 random Grovekeeper units are picked at the start of combat.
The chosen units: +100% maximum health, +100% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
