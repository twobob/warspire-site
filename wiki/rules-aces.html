Aces

Tier five aces and their alliance effects.

An ace is a tier 5 unit that is the champion of one alliance. Besides its stats and its ability, an ace carries an extra ace effect that switches on when that alliance is active on your board. 8 units are aces; the other tier 5 units (Cinder Wyrmrider, Spire Exemplar) are not.

When the ace effect is active

  • The ace must be on the board. An ace on the bench does nothing.
  • The ace's alliance must have reached at least its first step. Any step is enough: a higher step does not make the ace effect stronger.
  • A step counts different units on the board. Two copies of the same unit count once, and units on the bench do not count. Some items add to the count of one alliance, for example Oathbound Insignia. See How alliances count.
  • The ace is a member of its own alliance, so it counts toward that step itself.
  • The check is made when combat starts and the effect lasts the whole fight. It is added on top of the alliance's own bonus, not instead of it.
  • An effect carried by the ace alone is carried by each copy of that ace you field. An effect that applies to all allies is applied once, however many copies of the ace are on the board, and also reaches units summoned during the fight.

Aces in the shop

  • Aces are bought like any other tier 5 unit: they cost 5 gold, the pool holds 10 copies of each, and they normally show up only once your shop can roll tier 5.
  • When a shop slot draws a unit from tier 5, each unit's chance is in proportion to its copies left in the pool. An ace whose alliance is active for you is counted with 15% more weight: with all 10 copies left it weighs 11.5 instead of 10.
  • With the tier 5 pool untouched, that lifts the ace from 10% of tier 5 draws to 11.3%. The chance of rolling tier 5 in the first place is not changed.
  • Active means the same as for the ace effect: the ace's alliance has reached at least its first step on your board at the moment the shop is rolled. Bench units do not count. The ace does not need to be owned yet, so building its alliance first makes the ace slightly easier to find.
  • Several aces can be boosted at once if several of their alliances are active.
  • The boost does not apply to tier 5 units that are not aces, and it cannot offer a unit with no copies left or one you already own at 3 star.
