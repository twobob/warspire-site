Ashwalker is a tier 3 (Rare) bruiser unit. It costs 3 gold in the shop and carries the Ashen, Stalker and Wyrmblood alliances.

Ability

Cinder Transfiguration

Burns into a cinder form for the rest of the combat, gaining attack damage and attack speed.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Itself.
  • Applies Cinder Form (+50 / 100 / 200 attack damage, +40 / 60 / 80% attack speed) to itself for the rest of combat (values per star).

Full details are on the Cinder Transfiguration ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Ashen race

The burnt martyrs of the spire. When an Ashen unit first falls, allies within 2 cells are healed for 250 and gain 10% bonus damage. With six Ashen, each one rises again once per combat with 40% of its health.

Units neededEffect
3When an Ashen unit dies (once per combat), it heals all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.
6Ashen units: one revival per combat at 40% of maximum health.
When an Ashen unit revives (once per combat), it heals itself and all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.

Stalker class

Hunters of the ruins. Each landed attack has a chance to fire a quick volley of extra attacks at the same target.

Units neededEffect
3When a Stalker unit lands an attack (30% chance), it launches 2 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).
6When a Stalker unit lands an attack (38% chance), it launches 3 extra attacks at the unit it hit at once (these never set off more extra attacks).

Wyrmblood race

Heirs of the old wyrms. With two Wyrmblood, each of their spells also breathes fire on the target and every enemy within 1 cell, dealing magical damage and scorching them.

Units neededEffect
2When a Wyrmblood unit casts an ability, it deals 150 / 250 / 400 magical damage to the enemy its ability was cast on (its current target if there is none) and all enemies within 1 cell of it and applies Scorched (alliances) (30 / 50 / 80 magical damage taken per second) to the same targets for 4 seconds (values per star).

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
