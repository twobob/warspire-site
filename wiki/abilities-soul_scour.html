The ability of Pit Imp: deals magical damage and weakens enemies. It targets its current target.

In game: Scours the target’s soul, dealing magical damage and crippling its speed for 5 seconds.

Soul Scour is the ability of Pit Imp.

How the cast works

  • Pit Imp needs 100 mana to cast Soul Scour. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It hits the enemy the unit is attacking. If the unit has no target at that moment, it picks the closest enemy instead. Enemies that cannot be targeted are never picked. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
