Pit Colossus is the upgraded form of Pit Golem, an ability of Vey the Pactbinder. It takes the place of the original once the talent Pit Colossus is chosen.

Description

Vey calls a stronger Pit Golem to her side. Its arrival stuns every enemy within 2 cells of her target for 3 seconds.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 15 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 15.7 seconds apart. The first cast starts 15 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
0.7 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target and all enemies within 2 cells of it.
  • Applies Stunned (general) (cannot move, attack or cast) to the targets for 3 seconds.
  • Summons a Pit Golem at star 2 (levels 1 to 6), then 3 (levels 7 to 10) in the nearest free cell.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 15.7 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 15.7 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 2 casts at most.
  • It has no cast range. It is aimed at the enemy the commander is fighting, or the closest enemy when there is none, and reaches that enemy at any distance.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Applying it again

  • Stunned (general): it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
