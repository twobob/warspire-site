Open Bar is a talent of Brugg Ironjaw, offered at player level 10 to the Innkeeper build.

Description

Last Round lasts 7 seconds and blocks more damage.

What it changes

Last Round becomes Open Bar.

  • The change takes effect from the next fight, and the upgraded form keeps the original's place in the casting order.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

In play. The damage reduction applies to physical and magical damage. Pure damage is not reduced.
