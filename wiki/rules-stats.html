Stat reference

Every unit stat and every modifier a bonus can carry.

This page lists every number a unit has and every bonus that items, alliances, abilities, talents and status effects can give. Part one covers the stats printed on a unit. Part two covers the bonuses that change them in a fight. For how damage is worked out from these numbers, see Damage, armour and resistance. To compare units side by side, use the Unit stat table.

Unit stats: Health, Minimum attack damage, Maximum attack damage, Attack time, Attack point, Attack range, Armour, Magic resistance, Maximum mana, Move speed, Projectile speed.

Bonuses: Attack speed, Bonus damage, Bonus attack damage, Bonus health, Damage over time, Damage reduction, Lifesteal, Mana regeneration, Evasion, Critical chance, Critical damage, Health regeneration, Move speed bonus, Silence, Disarm, Healing received, Stun, Untargetable, Attack range bonus, Bonus health percentage, Mana gain, Revive, Root, Survive a killing blow.

Part one: unit stats

Every unit has these 11 numbers. Health and attack damage grow with stars (see Star upgrades). The rest usually stay the same at every star. Commanders have the same stats, which grow with the player's level instead (see How commanders work).

Health

How much damage the unit can take before it dies. For 51 of the 65 units in the shop it doubles with each star (see Star upgrades). The other 14 double at two stars and then reach 2.4 times their two star health at three stars. Summons follow their own values, shown on their pages. Maximum health in a fight is (health + flat health bonuses) multiplied by (1 + percentage health bonuses / 100), and never less than 1. Measured in health points.

Range across all 88 units, summons included.
LowestHighest
One star50 (Skitterling)4,500 (Stormhide Beast)
Two stars50 (Skitterling)6,000 (Spire Colossus)
Three stars50 (Skitterling)12,000 (Spire Colossus)

Minimum attack damage

The lowest number a basic attack can roll before bonuses. Each attack rolls a whole number, evenly, between this and maximum attack damage. All basic attacks deal physical damage. See the order of a hit. Measured in damage per attack.

Two stars1 (Spiked Palisade and 4 more)360 (Mire King)
Three stars1 (Spiked Palisade and 4 more)720 (Mire King)

Maximum attack damage

The highest number a basic attack can roll before bonuses. Bonus attack damage is added after the roll, so it raises both ends. Measured in damage per attack.

Range across all 88 units, summons included.
LowestHighest
One star1 (Spiked Palisade and 4 more)280 (Pit Golem)
Two stars1 (Spiked Palisade and 4 more)400 (Mire King)
Three stars1 (Spiked Palisade and 4 more)800 (Mire King)

Attack time

The time from the start of one basic attack to the start of the next, before attack speed. Lower is faster. The real interval is attack time divided by (1 + attack speed / 100). Attacks per second is 1 divided by that. Measured in seconds between attacks.

Range across all 88 units, summons included.
LowestHighest
All stars0.5 (Skitterling)2 (Drowned Behemoth and 1 more)

Attack point

The wind-up of a basic attack: the delay between starting the swing and the hit landing (melee) or the projectile leaving (ranged). It is part of the attack time, not added to it, and it shortens with attack speed in the same way. A stun or disarm during the wind-up cancels the attack. Measured in seconds.

Range across all 88 units, summons included.
LowestHighest
All stars0.17 (Spire Marksman)0.7 (Grave Archer)

Attack range

How far the unit can attack, counted in board cells with diagonals counting as one. A range of 1 is melee: the unit must stand next to its target. See Combat and Board and bench. Measured in cells.

Range across all 88 units, summons included.
LowestHighest
All stars1 (Gutter Scavenger and 50 more)4 (Spire Acolyte and 12 more)

Armour

Reduces physical damage along a curve. See the armour formula and table. Armour can be pushed below zero, which makes the unit take more physical damage. Measured in points.

Range across all 88 units, summons included.
LowestHighest
All stars0 (Spire Acolyte and 12 more)20 (Spiked Palisade)

Armour bonus

Added to the unit's base armour. Negative values strip armour and can take it below zero. Measured in points.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 11 items, 3 alliances, 1 ability, 8 status effects, 3 talents, 1 ace. For example: Riveted Mail (7), Scrapkin (2 units, for one member picked at random: 7, and a further 7 while outnumbered; 4 units: 7, and a further 7 while outnumbered; 6 units, for every ally: 7, and a further 7 while outnumbered), Crowd Brawler (5 per adjacent ally, up to 4, and a further 5 per adjacent enemy, up to 4), Corroded (units) (-8 / -14 / -20), Thick Skin (8), Spire Colossus (30).

Magic resistance

The percentage of magical damage the unit ignores. See the magic resistance table. Values above 100 count as 100. Measured in percent.

Range across all 88 units, summons included.
LowestHighest
All stars0 (Gutter Scavenger and 81 more)100 (Spiked Palisade)

Magic resistance bonus

Added to the unit's base magic resistance. The total counts for at most 100. Measured in percentage points.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 8 items, 2 alliances, 2 status effects, 3 talents, 1 ace. For example: Warding Cowl (35), Drowned (2 units, for every ally: 30; 4 units, for every ally: 55), Cowl Ward (30), Pain Ward (20), Deep Basilisk (75), Spellward Amulet (35).

Maximum mana

The mana the unit must fill to cast its ability. Units start a fight empty and gain mana by attacking and by taking damage. A unit with 0 maximum mana never gains mana. See Mana and casting. Measured in mana points.

Range among the 65 units that use mana. The other 23 have 0.
LowestHighest
All stars30 (Quillback)100 (Gutter Scavenger and 44 more)

Move speed

How fast the unit walks. A board cell is 128 distance wide, so a step to the next cell takes 128 divided by move speed, in seconds. Move speed bonuses and slows multiply it, and it never drops below 50. Measured in distance per second.

Range among the 83 units that can move. The other 5 are placeables, which never move.
LowestHighest
All stars50 (Mire Ward)420 (Cinder Mite and 1 more)

Projectile speed

How fast a ranged unit's attack flies to its target, in the same distance as move speed (128 per cell). Melee units have 0: their attacks land at the end of the wind-up. A projectile follows its target. It fails only if the target dies or becomes untargetable before it arrives. Evasion is rolled when it arrives, using the target's evasion at that moment. Measured in distance per second.

Range among the 37 units that fire projectiles. The other 51 have 0.
LowestHighest
All stars900 (Spire Acolyte and 15 more)1,500 (Rift Mote and 3 more)

Part two: bonuses

A bonus is carried by an item, an alliance step, an ability, a talent or a status effect. Bonuses are listed here from the most used to the least used. Each entry says how several sources of the same bonus combine.

Reading the examples. The value in brackets after a name is what that source gives. Three numbers separated by slashes are the values at one, two and three stars. A commander's value that grows is written from its value at level 1 to its value at the highest level. An alliance value is labelled with the number of units its step needs. Words after a number say when it counts: a bonus given per adjacent unit or per kill is that much for each one, up to the limit shown, and "a further" marks a second bonus from the same source that only counts under the condition that follows it. A status effect is counted once, as the effect, and not again for the item or ability that applies it. On or off effects show how long they last instead of a value.

One rule sits above all of these: the same named status effect cannot be on a unit twice. Applying it again refreshes it, or adds a stack if the effect is stackable. Two different effects that carry the same bonus do combine as described below. See How status effects work.

Bonuses to armour and magic resistance are described with the stat itself in part one.

Attack speed

Makes basic attacks come faster. Both the time between attacks and the wind-up are divided by (1 + attack speed / 100), so +100 doubles the number of attacks. Negative values slow the unit. The divisor is kept between 0.2 and 7, so a unit can never attack more than 7 times as fast as its base, nor slower than 0.2 times. Measured in percent.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 15 items, 1 alliance, 27 status effects, 4 talents, 1 ace. For example: Hunter's Edge (10), Mirefolk (2 units: 20; 4 units: 30), War Drums (Mirefolk, 2 units) (20), Rounds for All (15 for every ally), Mire King (100), Quickstep Gauntlets (25).

Bonus damage

Multiplies the damage of basic attacks, after bonus attack damage is added and before a critical hit. It does not change the stated damage of abilities. Negative values weaken the unit's attacks. Measured in percent.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 4 items, 6 alliances, 17 status effects, 5 talents, 1 ace. For example: Runescribed Quill (12), Abyssal (1 unit: 50; 2 units: 100), Rekindled (10), Infernal Boost (8 for every ally), Abyssal Archon (100), Titanbane Spear (-50).

Bonus attack damage

Added to every basic attack's damage roll before percentage bonuses and critical hits. It is not added to ability damage. Measured in damage per attack.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 21 items, 11 status effects. For example: Hunter's Edge (20), Feral Fury (Feral, 2 units) (5), Ironwood Greatblade (33), Feral Fury (Feral, 4 units) (8), Brawler's Wraps (15), Feral Fury (Feral, 6 units) (13).

Bonus health

Added to base health before percentage bonuses. Gaining it in a fight also raises current health by the same amount. Measured in health points.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 18 items, 1 alliance, 1 status effect, 6 talents. For example: Heartwood Charm (375), Stoneborn (2 units: 10 / 20 / 30 per enemy it has killed this match, up to 60 kills; 4 units: 40 / 80 / 120 per enemy it has killed this match, up to 60 kills), Stalwart Vigour (250), Hardened Hide (300), Wellspring Brooch (150), Warded Crew (200 for every ally).

Damage over time

Deals its damage per second to the holder, in portions every 0.5 seconds. The damage has a type (magical when none is given) and is reduced like any other hit. See damage over time. Measured in damage per second.

Several sources: Every effect that carries it runs on its own, so two different effects both tick. A stackable effect multiplies its amount per second by its number of stacks.

Used by 1 item, 14 status effects. For example: Fevered Armlet (80 per second), Burning Pitch (20 / 35 / 55 per second), Shredded (70 / 105 / 140 per second), Unravelling (60 / 90 / 140 per second), Static Shock (35 / 50 / 80 per second), Sentenced (60 / 90 / 120 per second).

Damage reduction

Removes this share of physical and magical damage after armour or magic resistance. Pure damage ignores it. A negative value makes the unit take that much more. See damage reduction. Measured in percent.

Several sources: Sources multiply instead of adding: each is applied to what the others leave, so two sources of 20% give 36%, not 40%. One source counts for at most 100%.

Used by 2 items, 1 alliance, 9 status effects, 1 talent. For example: Rampart Targe (6), Bulwark (2 units: 15, and a further 15 while next to another Bulwark ally; 4 units: 20, and a further 20 while next to another Bulwark ally; 6 units: 25, and a further 25 while next to another Bulwark ally), Gashed (-10 / -20 / -30), Pain Ward (15), Nullstone Ward (10), Last Light (100).

Lifesteal

Heals the unit for this share of the damage its basic attacks deal after the target's defences. See lifesteal and healing. Measured in percent of damage dealt.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 2 items, 9 status effects. For example: Leechfang Mask (30), Soul Link (Soulbinder, 2 units) (50), Frenzy Visage (15), Soul Link (Soulbinder, 4 units) (60), Soul Link (Soulbinder, 6 units) (70), Blood Frenzy (25).

Mana regeneration

Mana gained steadily through the fight, on top of mana from attacking and taking damage. See Mana and casting. Measured in mana per second.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 10 items, 1 alliance. For example: Cinder Core (7), Spireborn (2 units, for every ally: 4; 4 units, for every ally: 8; 6 units, for every ally: 15), Wellspring Brooch (4), Wayfarer's Lantern (5), Squall Sceptre (10), Hourglass Prism (2).

Evasion

The chance to dodge a basic attack completely. Abilities and damage over time cannot be dodged. Measured in percent chance.

Several sources: Sources multiply instead of adding: each is applied to what the others leave, so two sources of 20% give 36%, not 40%. One source counts for at most 100%.

Used by 5 items, 1 alliance, 2 status effects, 1 talent. For example: Mistveil Talisman (25), Shade (2 units: 20; 4 units: 40), Umbral Dance (60 / 75 / 90), Slippery Crew (10 for every ally), Mirrorweave Cloak (20), Dusk Veil (100).

Critical chance

The chance for a basic attack to be a critical hit. The total counts for at most 100. Measured in percent chance.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 5 items, 1 alliance, 1 status effect, 1 ace. For example: Keen Whetstone (15), Cutthroat (3 units: 15; 6 units: 25), Deadly Aim (20), Hourblade (25), Stormglass Shard (15), Twin Fang Daggers (10).

Critical damage

How hard a critical hit lands, as a percentage of the normal hit. A unit with no source of its own uses 200%. Measured in percent of normal damage.

Several sources: Only the strongest source counts. Weaker ones are ignored.

Used by 5 items, 1 alliance, 1 status effect, 1 ace. For example: Keen Whetstone (175), Cutthroat (3 units: 325; 6 units: 400), Deadly Aim (200), Hourblade (400), Stormglass Shard (175), Twin Fang Daggers (175).

Health regeneration

Heals the holder steadily, in portions every 0.5 seconds. It is changed by healing received like any other heal. Measured in health per second.

Several sources: Every effect that carries it runs on its own, so two different effects both tick. A stackable effect multiplies its amount per second by its number of stacks.

Used by 2 items, 1 alliance, 4 abilities, 1 status effect. For example: Warding Cowl (10 per second), Scrapkin (2 units, for one member picked at random: 8 per second, and a further 8 per second while outnumbered; 4 units: 8 per second, and a further 8 per second while outnumbered; 6 units, for every ally: 8 per second, and a further 8 per second while outnumbered), Pact Retinue (20 per second), Blood Offering (8 at level 1 to 30 at level 10 per second), Seer's Lens (10 per second), Crowd Brawler (20 at level 1 to 40 at level 10 per second).

Move speed bonus

Multiplies move speed. Negative values are slows. Move speed never drops below 50. Measured in percent.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 1 item, 7 status effects. For example: Wayfarer's Lantern (15), Scoured (-50), War Chant (10), Unravelling (-50), Embers (-40), Rime Chill (-35).

Silence

The unit cannot cast abilities. A cast in progress is cancelled. It can still move and attack.

Several sources: On or off. One source is enough, and more sources change nothing while it lasts.

Used by 1 item, 6 status effects. For example: Frenzy Visage, Silenced (units) (2 / 3 / 4 seconds), Pact Silence (4 seconds), Static Shock (3 / 4 / 5 seconds), Sentenced (10 seconds), Silenced (items) (5 seconds).

Disarm

The unit cannot make basic attacks. An attack wind-up in progress is cancelled. It can still move and cast.

Several sources: On or off. One source is enough, and more sources change nothing while it lasts.

Used by 1 ability, 4 status effects. For example: Pact Retinue, Disarmed (units) (2 / 3 / 4 seconds), Disarmed (items) (3 seconds), Last Light (2 seconds), Hexed (3.5 seconds).

Healing received

Raises or lowers every heal the unit receives, lifesteal and regeneration included. The total cannot go below -100, where the unit cannot be healed. Measured in percent.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 1 alliance, 3 status effects. For example: Mender (2 units, for every ally: 25; 4 units, for every ally: 45), Wounded (Cutthroat, 3 units) (-75), Wounded (Cutthroat, 6 units) (-100), Blessed Healing (20).

Stun

The unit cannot move, attack or cast. A stun cancels an attack wind-up or a cast in progress. See How status effects work.

Several sources: On or off. One source is enough, and more sources change nothing while it lasts.

Used by 4 status effects. For example: Stunned (general) (0.5 to 4 seconds), Slammed (1.5 / 2.25 / 3 seconds), Stunned (items) (1.5 seconds), Swept Aloft (2.5 seconds).

Untargetable

Enemies cannot pick the unit as a target, and attacks already on their way to it miss.

Several sources: On or off. One source is enough, and more sources change nothing while it lasts.

Used by 4 status effects. For example: Skittering (the rest of the fight), Last Light (2 seconds), Swept Aloft (2.5 seconds), Smoke Cloud (1.5 or 1 seconds).

Attack range bonus

Added to attack range. Range never drops below 1. A melee unit given extra range can attack from further away, and its attacks still land without a projectile. Measured in cells.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 2 items, 1 status effect. For example: Hunter's Edge (1), Long Fuse (8), Longshot Scope (2).

Bonus health percentage

Multiplies health after flat bonuses: (health + flat bonuses) times (1 + this / 100). Measured in percent.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 2 alliances. For example: Grovekeeper (100 for one member picked at random at every step), Paragon (1 unit: 40).

Mana gain

Raises the mana gained from landing attacks and from taking damage. It does not change mana regeneration or mana restored by effects. See Mana and casting. Measured in percent.

Several sources: Every source adds together.

Used by 2 items. For example: Oxhide Helm (-50), Hourglass Prism (43).

Revive

The first time the unit would die in a fight it stays on the board instead, with this share of its maximum health and its timed debuffs removed. It works once per fight, gives the enemy no kill, and never applies to a summon whose time has run out. See How status effects work. Measured in percent of maximum health.

Several sources: Only the strongest source counts. Weaker ones are ignored.

Used by 1 item, 1 alliance. For example: Rebirth Sigil (50), Ashen (6 units: 40).

Root

The unit cannot move. It can still attack enemies in range and cast, and it only picks targets it can reach from where it stands.

Several sources: On or off. One source is enough, and more sources change nothing while it lasts.

Used by 2 status effects. For example: Rooted (units) (2 / 2.5 / 3 or 3 seconds), Rooted (items) (2 seconds).

Survive a killing blow

Once per fight, a hit that would kill the unit leaves it on 1 health instead. It does nothing if the unit is already on 1 health.

Several sources: On or off. One source is enough, and more sources change nothing while it lasts.

Used by 1 status effect. For example: Grave-Cloth (the rest of the fight).
