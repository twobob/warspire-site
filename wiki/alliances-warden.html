Wardens of the spire gates. Wardens gain bonus armour.

Warden is a class alliance carried by 7 units. It has 2 steps, reached with 3 and 6 different Warden units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
3Warden units: +10 armour.
6Warden units: +25 armour.
  • Only the highest step you have reached applies. Each step states its full effect; steps do not add together.

There are 7 different Warden units and the top step needs 6, so you can reach it with 1 unit to spare.

Aces

This unit is an ace tied to Warden. An ace's extra effect is switched on while the ace is on your board and Warden has at least one step active. See Aces. While Warden is active, each copy of this ace left in the pool counts 15% heavier when the shop rolls a tier 5 unit, so the ace turns up a little more often.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
