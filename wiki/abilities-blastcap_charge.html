Blastcap Charge

The ability of Blastcap Sapper: deals magical damage. It targets its current target and all enemies within 1 cell of it.

In game: Lobs a long-fused charge at the target that explodes for heavy magical damage to it and enemies next to it.

Blastcap Charge is the ability of Blastcap Sapper.

How the cast works

  • Blastcap Sapper needs 100 mana to cast Blastcap Charge. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It affects every enemy within 1 cell of the enemy the unit is attacking (the closest enemy if it has no target). That enemy is hit too. A diagonal step counts as one cell. Enemies that cannot be targeted are never picked. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
