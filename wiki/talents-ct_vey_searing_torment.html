Searing Torment

Searing Torment is a talent of Vey the Pactbinder, offered at player level 8 to the Thrall Binder and Golem Caller builds.

Description

Vey also casts Searing Torment.

What it changes

The commander also casts Searing Torment.

  • The new ability runs on its own cooldown from the next fight on, and is cast after the build's own abilities when several are ready together.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

Ability

AbilityTimingDescription
Added by the talentSearing TormentCooldown 8 secondsVey curses a random enemy, dealing pure damage every second for 7 seconds.

The choice

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Searing Torment means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
