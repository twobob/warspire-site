Thrall Mark is the ultimate of Vey the Pactbinder in the Thrall Binder build. Choosing that build is the only way to get it.

Description

Vey marks the weakest enemy for 6 seconds. The marked enemy loses 10 armour and takes 20% more damage from every source.

In play. The extra damage taken applies to physical and magical damage. Pure damage is not increased, and that includes Searing Torment.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 10 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 10.4 seconds apart. The first cast starts 10 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
0.4 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
The enemy with the lowest share of health left.
  • Applies Thrall Mark (-10 armour, 20% more physical and magical damage taken) to the target for 6 seconds.

By player level

Nothing about this ability changes with player level: it is the same from the day the commander arrives.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 10.4 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 10.4 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 4 casts at most.
  • It has no cast range: any enemy on the board can be picked, however far away.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Upgraded form

The talent Binding Mark swaps this ability for Binding Mark. It is offered at player level 10 to the Thrall Binder build.

Binding Mark: Vey marks the weakest enemy for 9 seconds. The marked enemy loses 15 armour and takes 30% more damage from every source.

Everything that differs between the two forms. Anything not listed is the same in both.

Applying it again

  • Thrall Mark: it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
