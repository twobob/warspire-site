Warded Crew is a talent of Vey the Pactbinder, offered at player level 10 to the Thrall Binder and Golem Caller builds.

Description

Every ally gains 200 maximum health.

What it changes

Every ally in the fight when combat begins, including the commander and the units the commander summons at the start of combat: +200 maximum health. Units summoned later in the fight do not get it.

  • The bonus is handed out at the start of each fight and lasts the whole fight, so the allies that received it keep it even if the commander falls.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

The choice

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Warded Crew means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
