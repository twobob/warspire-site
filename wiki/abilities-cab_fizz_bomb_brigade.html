Bomb Brigade is the ultimate of Fizzwick Emberhorn in the Bombardier build. Choosing that build is the only way to get it.

Description

Fizzwick's goblin pals lob eight bombs onto random cells of the board. Each bomb deals magical damage to every enemy within 1 cell of where it lands and sets them on fire for 5 seconds.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 13 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 13.25 seconds apart. The first cast starts 13 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
0.25 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
8 random cells of the board, hitting every enemy within 1 cell of each (an enemy caught by several is hit once for each).
  • Deals 210 (levels 1 to 5), then 250 / 300 / 360 / 430 / 500 (levels 6 to 10) magical damage to the targets.
  • Applies Burning to the targets for 5 seconds: 40 (levels 1 to 5), then 48 / 56 / 64 / 72 / 80 (levels 6 to 10) magical damage taken per second.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 13.25 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 13.25 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 3 casts at most.
  • The commander does not wait for anything to be in reach. The cast goes off as soon as it is ready and affects whatever is in its area at that moment, which can be nothing. The cooldown is spent either way.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Upgraded form

The talent Carpet of Bombs swaps this ability for Carpet of Bombs. It is offered at player level 10 to the Bombardier build.

Carpet of Bombs: Fizzwick's goblin pals lob twelve bombs onto random cells of the board. Each bomb deals magical damage to every enemy within 1 cell of where it lands and sets them on fire for 5 seconds.

Everything that differs between the two forms. Anything not listed is the same in both.

Applying it again

  • Burning: it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
