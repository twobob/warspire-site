Thornback Boar is a tier 1 (Common) bruiser unit. It costs 1 gold in the shop and carries the Feral, Warden and Grovekeeper alliances.

Ability

Goring Charge

Gores the target, dealing heavy physical damage and stunning it.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.1 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target.
  • Deals 175 / 275 / 400 physical damage to the target (values per star).
  • Applies Stunned (general) (cannot move, attack or cast) to the target for 1 / 1.25 / 1.5 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Goring Charge ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Feral race

Feral beasts of the ash wastes grow stronger with every blow. Affected units gain attack damage each time they land an attack, for the rest of the combat.

Units neededEffect
2When a Feral unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 2 units) (+5 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
4When a Feral unit lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 4 units) (+8 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
6When an ally lands an attack, it applies a stack of Feral Fury (Feral, 6 units) (+13 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Warden class

Wardens of the spire gates. Wardens gain bonus armour.

Grovekeeper class

Keepers of the hanging gardens who share the grove's strength. At the start of combat, one Grovekeeper (two with four) grows into a stronger form with double health and damage.
