Mega Kaboom is a talent of Fizzwick Emberhorn, offered at player level 6 to the Bombardier and Firestarter builds.

Description

Kaboom Bolt deals much more damage, still split among its targets.

What it changes

Kaboom Bolt becomes Mega Kaboom.

  • The change takes effect from the next fight, and the upgraded form keeps the original's place in the casting order.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

Ability

AbilityTimingDescription
Before the talentKaboom BoltCooldown 12 secondsFizzwick hurls a fireball at his target. It bursts on the target and every enemy within 1 cell, splitting its magical damage evenly among them and setting them all on fire for 5 seconds.
With the talentMega KaboomCooldown 12 secondsFizzwick hurls a much bigger fireball at his target. It bursts on the target and every enemy within 1 cell, splitting its greater magical damage evenly among them and setting them all on fire for 5 seconds.
