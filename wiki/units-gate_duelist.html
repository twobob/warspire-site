Gate Duelist is a tier 2 (Uncommon) bruiser unit. It costs 2 gold in the shop and carries the Stoneborn, Duelist and Warden alliances.

Ability

Blade Cyclone

Spins into a cyclone of steel, dealing magical damage to adjacent enemies.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All enemies within 1 cell of itself.
  • Deals 250 / 450 / 650 magical damage to the targets (values per star).

Full details are on the Blade Cyclone ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Stoneborn race

Gargoyles and golems carved from the spire itself, hardened by every battle. Each Stoneborn unit permanently gains maximum health for every enemy it has killed this game, more at higher stars.

Units neededEffect
2Stoneborn units: +10 / 20 / 30 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +600 / 1,200 / 1,800 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).
4Stoneborn units: +40 / 80 / 120 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +2,400 / 4,800 / 7,200 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).

Duelist class

Blade-masters who thrive in the press. Each Duelist deals bonus damage for every enemy standing next to it.

Units neededEffect
3Duelist units: +10% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
6Duelist units: +25% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

Warden class

Wardens of the spire gates. Wardens gain bonus armour.

Units neededEffect
3Warden units: +10 armour.
6Warden units: +25 armour.
