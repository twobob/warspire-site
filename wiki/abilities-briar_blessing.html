Briar Blessing

The ability of Briar Shaper: heals and strengthens allies. It targets the 2 allies with the lowest share of health left (itself included).

In game: Wraps the two most wounded allies in living bark, healing them and granting bonus armour for 4 seconds.

Briar Blessing is the ability of Briar Shaper.

What it does

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.

  • Applies Living Bark (+5 / 8 / 12 armour) to the targets for 4 seconds (values per star).

How the cast works

  • Briar Shaper needs 100 mana to cast Briar Blessing. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It picks the 2 allies with the smallest share of their health left. The caster itself can be chosen. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
