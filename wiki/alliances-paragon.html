A living legend of the spire. The Paragon gains 15% bonus damage and 40% bonus maximum health.

Paragon is a class alliance carried by a single unit. It has one step, active as soon as one Paragon unit is on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
1Paragon units: +15% attack damage, +40% maximum health.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
