Barrel Avalanche is a talent of Brugg Ironjaw, offered at player level 10 to the Barrel Roller build.

Description

Barrel Barrage hits five enemies and five allies.

What it changes

Barrel Barrage becomes Barrel Avalanche.

  • The change takes effect from the next fight, and the upgraded form keeps the original's place in the casting order.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

Ability

AbilityTimingDescription
Before the talentBarrel BarrageCooldown 12 secondsBrugg rolls barrels across the board. Three enemies take physical damage and take 20% more damage for the rest of the combat; three allies gain 50 attack speed for 5 seconds.
With the talentBarrel AvalancheCooldown 12 secondsBrugg rolls more barrels across the board. Five enemies take physical damage and take 20% more damage for the rest of the combat; five allies gain 50 attack speed for 5 seconds.

In play. The extra damage taken applies to physical and magical damage. Pure damage is not increased.

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Barrel Avalanche means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
