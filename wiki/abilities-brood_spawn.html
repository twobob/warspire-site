Brood Spawn

The ability of Brood Queen: summons Brood Hatchling units.

In game: Lays a clutch of eggs that hatch into hatchlings to fight for you, up to 3 alive at once.

Brood Spawn is the ability of Brood Queen.

What it does

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.4 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
  • Summons 2 / 3 / 4 Brood Hatchling units at its own star level on the nearest free cells (values per star).

Values by star

The numbers that change with the star level of the caster. See Star upgrades for how units gain stars.

Summons

  • Brood Hatchling: 2 appear at star 1, 3 at star 2 and 4 at star 3. Each one has as many stars as the caster. They stay until they die or the fight ends.

Summoned units appear on the free cells nearest to the caster and fight on its side. If the board has no free cell, nothing more appears. See Summons and placeables.

How the cast works

  • Brood Queen needs 100 mana to cast Brood Spawn. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It affects the caster itself. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
