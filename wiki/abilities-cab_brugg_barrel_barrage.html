Barrel Barrage is the ultimate of Brugg Ironjaw in the Barrel Roller build. Choosing that build is the only way to get it.

Description

Brugg rolls barrels across the board. Three enemies take physical damage and take 20% more damage for the rest of the combat; three allies gain 50 attack speed for 5 seconds.

In play. The extra damage taken applies to physical and magical damage. Pure damage is not increased.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 12 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 12.5 seconds apart. The first cast starts 12 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
0.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
3 random enemies.
  • Deals 300 (levels 1 to 5), then 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 / 800 (levels 6 to 10) physical damage to the targets.
  • Applies Soaked (20% more physical and magical damage taken) to the targets for the rest of combat.
  • Applies On Brugg's Tab (+50% attack speed) to 3 random allies (the commander included) for 5 seconds.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 12.5 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 12.5 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 3 casts at most.
  • It has no cast range: any enemy on the board can be picked, however far away.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Upgraded form

The talent Barrel Avalanche swaps this ability for Barrel Avalanche. It is offered at player level 10 to the Barrel Roller build.

Barrel Avalanche: Brugg rolls more barrels across the board. Five enemies take physical damage and take 20% more damage for the rest of the combat; five allies gain 50 attack speed for 5 seconds.

Everything that differs between the two forms. Anything not listed is the same in both.

Applying it again

  • Soaked: it does not stack and it already lasts the rest of the fight, so a second application on the same unit changes nothing.
  • On Brugg's Tab: it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
