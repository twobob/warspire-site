Brood Queen is a tier 4 (Epic) caster unit. It costs 4 gold in the shop and carries the Swarmkin and Conjurer alliances.

Ability

Brood Spawn

Lays a clutch of eggs that hatch into hatchlings to fight for you.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.4 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
  • Summons 2 / 3 / 4 Brood Hatchling units at its own star level on the nearest free cells (values per star).

Full details are on the Brood Spawn ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Swarmkin race

Chitinous things from the spire's foundations. At the start of combat and every 10 seconds, a random Swarmkin releases untargetable skitterlings that harry the enemy for 10 seconds; their bites weaken the target's damage.

Units neededEffect
2One random Swarmkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat and then every 10 seconds, the chosen unit summons 3 Skitterling units at star 1 on the nearest free cells; each lasts 10 seconds. The repeats stop when the chosen unit dies.
4One random Swarmkin unit is picked at the start of combat.
At the start of combat and then every 10 seconds, the chosen unit summons 6 Skitterling units at star 2 on the nearest free cells; each lasts 10 seconds. The repeats stop when the chosen unit dies.

Conjurer class

Callers of things from beyond the spire. Every summoned ally deals 20% more damage (40% with four Conjurers). Conjurers share the bonus.

Units neededEffect
2Conjurer units and the summoned units that carry the Conjurer mark: +20% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
4Conjurer units and the summoned units that carry the Conjurer mark: +40% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
