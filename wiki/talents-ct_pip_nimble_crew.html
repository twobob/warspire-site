Nimble Crew is a talent of Pip Quickfingers, offered at player level 10 to the Pickpocket and Brawler builds.

Description

Every ally deals 8% more damage and attacks 8 faster.

What it changes

Every ally in the fight when combat begins, including the commander and the units the commander summons at the start of combat: +8% attack damage, +8% attack speed. Units summoned later in the fight do not get it.

  • This raises attack damage only. Damage from abilities and damage over time are not increased.
  • The bonus is handed out at the start of each fight and lasts the whole fight, so the allies that received it keep it even if the commander falls.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

The choice

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Nimble Crew means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
