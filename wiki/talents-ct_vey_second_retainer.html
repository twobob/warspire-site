Second Retainer is a talent of Vey the Pactbinder, offered at player level 4 to the Thrall Binder and Golem Caller builds.

Description

A second Pact Archer joins Vey at the start of combat.

What it changes

At the start of combat, the commander summons a Pact Archer at star 1 (levels 1 to 6), then 2 (levels 7 and 8), then 3 (levels 9 and 10) in the nearest free cell.

  • Its triggered effects fire on their own, like those of a passive ability.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

The choice

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Second Retainer means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
