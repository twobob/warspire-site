Unbreakable is the upgraded form of Shrug It Off, an ability of Brugg Ironjaw. It takes the place of the original once the talent Unbreakable is chosen.

Description

Brugg wades in: for 7 seconds he takes 50% less damage, attacks much faster and heals for half the damage he deals.

In play. The damage reduction applies to physical and magical damage. Pure damage is not reduced.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 10 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 11.5 seconds apart. The first cast starts 5 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
1.5 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
The commander.
  • Applies Shrug It Off (50% less physical and magical damage taken, +200% attack speed, +50% lifesteal on attack damage) to the commander for 7 seconds.

By player level

Nothing about this ability changes with player level: it is the same from the day the commander arrives.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 11.5 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 6.5 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 4 casts at most.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

What the upgrade changes

This is what Shrug It Off becomes.

Shrug It Off: Brugg cracks his knuckles and wades in: for 5 seconds he takes 50% less damage, attacks much faster and heals for half the damage he deals.

Everything that differs between the two forms. Anything not listed is the same in both.

Applying it again

  • Shrug It Off: it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
