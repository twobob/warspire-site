Storm Augur is a tier 4 (Epic) caster unit. It costs 4 gold in the shop and carries the Stoneborn, Soulbinder and Triad alliances.

Ability

Thunderhead Squall

Calls a thunderhead over the target that silences and shocks it and enemies within 2 cells.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target and all enemies within 2 cells of it.
  • Applies Static Shock (35 / 50 / 80 magical damage taken per second, cannot cast abilities) to the targets for 3 / 4 / 5 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Thunderhead Squall ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Stoneborn race

Gargoyles and golems carved from the spire itself, hardened by every battle. Each Stoneborn unit permanently gains maximum health for every enemy it has killed this game, more at higher stars.

Units neededEffect
2Stoneborn units: +10 / 20 / 30 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +600 / 1,200 / 1,800 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).
4Stoneborn units: +40 / 80 / 120 maximum health for each enemy the unit has killed this match, up to +2,400 / 4,800 / 7,200 maximum health at 60 kills; kills are counted by the unit and carry over from round to round (values per star).

Soulbinder class

Casting a spell binds the Soulbinder to the most wounded ally. For a short time both heal for part of the damage they deal.

Units neededEffect
2When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 2 units) (+50% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 2 seconds.
4When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 4 units) (+60% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 2.5 seconds.
6When a Soulbinder unit casts an ability, it applies Soul Link (Soulbinder, 6 units) (+70% lifesteal on attack damage) to itself and the ally with the lowest share of health left (itself included) for 3 seconds.

Triad race

Three elemental siblings who fight as one. With three Triad units, a spell charges its caster, and a charged Triad slams the ground around its target, dealing magical damage and stunning (at most once every 6 seconds each).

Units neededEffect
3When a Triad unit casts an ability (at most once every 6 seconds), it deals 130 / 195 / 260 magical damage to the enemy its ability was cast on (its current target if there is none) and all enemies within 1 cell of it and applies Slammed (cannot move, attack or cast) to the same targets for 1.5 / 2.25 / 3 seconds (values per star).

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
