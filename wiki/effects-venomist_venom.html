How status effects work

What it does

A unit with this effect: -7% attack speed.

This counts as a debuff, because it harms the unit that carries it.

Stacking and duration

  • Each new application adds a stack, up to 99 stacks. Every stack adds the full values again, and each application also renews the duration.
  • Attack speed cannot go below 20% or above 700% of normal, so stacks beyond that have no further effect (see Stat reference). On a unit with no other attack speed changes, stack 12 is the last one that makes a difference.

See How status effects work for how effects stack, renew and expire, and Stat reference for what each stat means.
