Blood Offering is the core ability of Vey the Pactbinder. Every build of the commander has it.

Description

Vey spends 5% of her maximum health to heal every ally at once and grant them health regeneration and attack damage for 6 seconds.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 8 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 8.4 seconds apart. The first cast starts 4 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
0.4 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
All allies (the commander included).
  • Deals pure damage to the commander equal to 5% of the commander's maximum health.
  • Heals the targets for 61 (levels 1 to 5), then 75 / 90 / 135 / 150 / 180 (levels 6 to 10) health.
  • Applies Blood Offering to the targets for 6 seconds: +8 (levels 1 to 5), then 12 / 15 / 22 / 25 / 30 (levels 6 to 10) health regeneration per second; +25 (levels 1 to 5), then 27 / 29 / 31 / 33 / 35 (levels 6 to 10) attack damage.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 8.4 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 4.4 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 5 casts at most.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Upgraded form

The talent Deep Offering swaps this ability for Deep Offering. It is offered at player level 6 to the Thrall Binder and Golem Caller builds.

Deep Offering: Vey spends 5% of her maximum health to heal every ally for 50% more and grant them health regeneration and attack damage for 8 seconds.

Applying it again

  • Blood Offering: it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
