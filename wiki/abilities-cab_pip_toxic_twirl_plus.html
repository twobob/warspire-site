Venom Cyclone is the upgraded form of Toxic Twirl, an ability of Pip Quickfingers. It takes the place of the original once the talent Venom Cyclone is chosen.

Description

Pip spins and flicks potent darts at several enemies within 3 cells, dealing 50% more physical damage and adding two stacks of toxin to each. Pip heals for each dart.

In play. Pip heals once per cast, however many targets are hit, not once for each target: 30 health at player levels 1 to 6, 45 at 7 to 8 and 60 at 9 to 10.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 3 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 3.1 seconds apart. The first cast starts 1.5 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
0.1 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
3 (levels 1 to 5), then 4 (levels 6 and 7), then 5 (levels 8 to 10) random enemies within 3 cells.
  • Deals 37.5 (levels 1 to 6), then 52.5 (levels 7 and 8), then 67.5 (levels 9 and 10) physical damage to the targets.
  • Applies 2 stacks of Pip's Toxin to the targets for the rest of combat: 10 (levels 1 to 6), then 14 (levels 7 and 8), then 18 (levels 9 and 10) physical damage taken per second; stacks up to 10 times.
  • Heals the commander for 30 (levels 1 to 6), then 45 (levels 7 and 8), then 60 (levels 9 and 10) health.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 3.1 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 1.6 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 14 casts at most.
  • The commander does not wait for anything to be in reach. The cast goes off as soon as it is ready and affects whatever is in its area at that moment, which can be nothing. The part that affects the commander still happens, and the cooldown is spent either way.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.
