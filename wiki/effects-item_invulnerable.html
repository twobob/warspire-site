Last Light

Last Light is a disable applied by items. It is mixed: it also helps the unit that carries it.

How status effects work

What it does

A unit with this effect: 100% less physical and magical damage taken, cannot attack, cannot be targeted by enemies.

This is a disable: a unit with it cannot attack. Disables also count as debuffs. It is mixed, though: it also protects or strengthens the unit that carries it. The game counts the whole effect as a debuff, so while it is timed an ally's cleanse removes it, the helpful part included, and an enemy's dispel, which strips buffs, leaves it alone.

Stacking and duration

  • It does not stack. A unit carries one copy at a time. Applying it again renews it: the longer of the time left and the new duration is kept.

See How status effects work for how effects stack, renew and expire, and Stat reference for what each stat means.
