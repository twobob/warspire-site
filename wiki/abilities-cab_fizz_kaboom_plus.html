Mega Kaboom is the upgraded form of Kaboom Bolt, an ability of Fizzwick Emberhorn. It takes the place of the original once the talent Mega Kaboom is chosen.

Description

Fizzwick hurls a much bigger fireball at his target. It bursts on the target and every enemy within 1 cell, splitting its greater magical damage evenly among them and setting them all on fire for 5 seconds.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 12 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 13 seconds apart. The first cast starts 6 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
1 second from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target and all enemies within 1 cell of it.
  • Deals 700 (levels 1 to 5), then 900 / 1,100 / 1,300 / 1,500 / 1,700 (levels 6 to 10) magical damage to the targets, split evenly among the units hit.
  • Applies Burning to the targets for 5 seconds: 40 (levels 1 to 5), then 48 / 56 / 64 / 72 / 80 (levels 6 to 10) magical damage taken per second.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 13 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 7 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 3 casts at most.
  • It has no cast range. It is aimed at the enemy the commander is fighting, or the closest enemy when there is none, and reaches that enemy at any distance.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Applying it again

  • Burning: it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
