Oathkeeper Thane is a tier 2 (Uncommon) support unit. It costs 2 gold in the shop and carries the Oathbound, Arcanist and Ravager alliances.

Ability

War Chant

Bellows a blood oath, granting itself and adjacent allies attack speed for 10 seconds.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.45 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Itself and all allies within 1 cell.
  • Applies War Chant (+40 / 70 / 100% attack speed, +10% movement speed) to the targets for 10 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the War Chant ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Oathbound race

Sworn in blood to one another. When an Oathbound unit dies, every other Oathbound unit gains +125 attack damage for the rest of the combat.

Units neededEffect
2When an Oathbound unit dies, it applies a stack of Oath of Vengeance (+125 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to every other allied Oathbound unit for the rest of combat.

Arcanist class

Scholars of the broken wards. All enemies lose magic resistance, so they take more magical damage.

Units neededEffect
3All enemies: -35% magic resistance.
Enemies summoned later in the fight get this too.
6All enemies: -70% magic resistance.
Enemies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Ravager class

Brutes who break their foes’ will. A Ravager’s attack maims the target, dealing physical damage and reducing the target’s damage for 4 seconds.

Units neededEffect
2When a Ravager unit lands an attack (at most once every 4 seconds), it deals 80 physical damage to the unit it hit and applies Maimed (Ravager, 2 units) (-30% attack damage) to the same target for 4 seconds.
4When a Ravager unit lands an attack (at most once every 4 seconds), it deals 120 physical damage to the unit it hit and applies Maimed (Ravager, 4 units) (-50% attack damage) to the same target for 4 seconds.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
