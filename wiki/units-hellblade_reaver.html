Hellblade Reaver is a tier 2 (Uncommon) bruiser unit. It costs 2 gold in the shop and carries the Abyssal, Bulwark and Duelist alliances.

Ability

Hellfire Brand

Brands the target with hellfire, dealing magical damage and stunning it.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Its current target.
  • Deals 150 / 225 / 300 magical damage to the target (values per star).
  • Applies Stunned (general) (cannot move, attack or cast) to the target for 1.5 / 2.5 / 3.5 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Hellfire Brand ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Abyssal race

Demons of the pit beneath the spire share one hunger. A lone Abyssal deals 50% more damage. With two or more, each gains 100% more damage, but casting a spell silences the other Abyssals for 4 seconds.

Units neededEffect
1Abyssal units: +50% attack damage.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
2Abyssal units: +100% attack damage.
When an Abyssal unit casts an ability, it applies Pact Silence (cannot cast abilities) to every other allied Abyssal unit for 4 seconds.
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.

Bulwark class

Shield-bearers sworn to hold the line. Bulwark units take less physical and magical damage, and take even less while standing within 1 cell of another Bulwark.

Units neededEffect
2Bulwark units: 15% less physical and magical damage taken.
In addition, Bulwark units: 15% less physical and magical damage taken while there is at least one other adjacent allied Bulwark unit. The reductions multiply instead of adding up: with both, 27.75% less damage in all.
4Bulwark units: 20% less physical and magical damage taken.
In addition, Bulwark units: 20% less physical and magical damage taken while there is at least one other adjacent allied Bulwark unit. The reductions multiply instead of adding up: with both, 36% less damage in all.
6Bulwark units: 25% less physical and magical damage taken.
In addition, Bulwark units: 25% less physical and magical damage taken while there is at least one other adjacent allied Bulwark unit. The reductions multiply instead of adding up: with both, 43.75% less damage in all.

Duelist class

Blade-masters who thrive in the press. Each Duelist deals bonus damage for every enemy standing next to it.

Units neededEffect
3Duelist units: +10% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
6Duelist units: +25% attack damage for each adjacent enemy unit (up to 8).
The attack damage bonus is for attacks only: ability damage is not raised.
