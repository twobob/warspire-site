Cinder Wyrmrider is a tier 5 (Legendary) tank unit. It costs 5 gold in the shop and carries the Spireborn, Ashen and Bulwark alliances.

Ability

Wyrmfire Gout

Spews wyrmfire in a line, dealing magical damage and weakening the damage of everything it burns.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.27 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Cast range
Its target must be within 3 cells.
Targets
Every enemy in a line from it through its current target, 3 cells long.
  • Deals 200 / 300 / 450 magical damage to the targets (values per star).
  • Applies Scorched (units) (-25 / -35 / -45% attack damage) to the targets for 4 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Wyrmfire Gout ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Alliances

Spireborn race

The last citizens of the spire draw on its fading wards. All allies generate mana every second.

Units neededEffect
2All allies: +4 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
4All allies: +8 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.
6All allies: +15 mana per second.
Allies summoned later in the fight get this too.

Ashen race

The burnt martyrs of the spire. When an Ashen unit first falls, allies within 2 cells are healed for 250 and gain 10% bonus damage. With six Ashen, each one rises again once per combat with 40% of its health.

Units neededEffect
3When an Ashen unit dies (once per combat), it heals all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.
6Ashen units: one revival per combat at 40% of maximum health.
When an Ashen unit revives (once per combat), it heals itself and all allies within 2 cells for 250 health and applies Rekindled (+10% attack damage) to the same targets for the rest of combat.

Bulwark class

Shield-bearers sworn to hold the line. Bulwark units take less physical and magical damage, and take even less while standing within 1 cell of another Bulwark.

Units neededEffect
2Bulwark units: 15% less physical and magical damage taken.
In addition, Bulwark units: 15% less physical and magical damage taken while there is at least one other adjacent allied Bulwark unit. The reductions multiply instead of adding up: with both, 27.75% less damage in all.
4Bulwark units: 20% less physical and magical damage taken.
In addition, Bulwark units: 20% less physical and magical damage taken while there is at least one other adjacent allied Bulwark unit. The reductions multiply instead of adding up: with both, 36% less damage in all.
6Bulwark units: 25% less physical and magical damage taken.
In addition, Bulwark units: 25% less physical and magical damage taken while there is at least one other adjacent allied Bulwark unit. The reductions multiply instead of adding up: with both, 43.75% less damage in all.
