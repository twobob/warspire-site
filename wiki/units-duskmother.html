Duskmother is a tier 5 (Legendary) assassin unit. It costs 5 gold in the shop and carries the Shade and Cutthroat alliances. It is an ace.

Ability

Umbral Dance

Dances through the shadows, becoming almost impossible to hit and striking faster for 5 seconds.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
None: the effect comes at once.
Targets
Itself.
  • Applies Umbral Dance (+60 / 75 / 90% evasion, +60 / 90 / 120% attack speed) to itself for 5 seconds (values per star).

Full details are on the Umbral Dance ability page. See Mana and casting for how mana is gained.

Ace effect

Duskmother is the ace of the Shade alliance. Its ace effect is on while it stands on the board and at least one step of that alliance is active. See Aces.

In the game's own words: Ace: when damaged, the Duskmother has a 15% chance to gain 100% evasion and +35 attack speed for 5 seconds.

In play, the effect applies to Duskmother itself:

  • When this unit takes damage from an attack or an ability (15% chance), it applies Dusk Veil (+100% evasion, +35% attack speed) to itself for 5 seconds.

When your shop rolls a tier 5 unit, each unit of that tier is weighted by the copies of it left in the pool. While that alliance is active for you, the weight of Duskmother in that draw is 15% higher. See The shop.

Alliances

Shade class

Shadow-walkers who are never quite where you strike. Shades gain evasion, and gain attack damage each time they evade an attack.

Units neededEffect
2Shade units: +20% evasion.
When a Shade unit evades an attack, it applies a stack of Riposte (Shade, 2 units) (+10 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.
4Shade units: +40% evasion.
When a Shade unit evades an attack, it applies a stack of Riposte (Shade, 4 units) (+20 attack damage, stacks up to 99 times) to itself for the rest of combat.

Cutthroat class

Knife-work in the dark. Cutthroats gain a chance to critically strike, and a critical strike wounds the target, reducing its healing received for 3 seconds.

Units neededEffect
3Cutthroat units: +15% critical strike chance, critical strikes deal 325% damage instead of the usual 200%.
When a Cutthroat unit lands a critical strike, it applies Wounded (Cutthroat, 3 units) (-75% healing received) to the unit it hit for 3 seconds.
6Cutthroat units: +25% critical strike chance, critical strikes deal 400% damage instead of the usual 200%.
When a Cutthroat unit lands a critical strike, it applies Wounded (Cutthroat, 6 units) (-100% healing received) to the unit it hit for 3 seconds.

How units are counted towards a step is explained under How alliances count.
