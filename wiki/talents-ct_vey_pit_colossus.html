Pit Colossus is a talent of Vey the Pactbinder, offered at player level 10 to the Golem Caller build.

Description

Pit Golem calls a stronger golem and stuns for 3 seconds.

What it changes

Pit Golem becomes Pit Colossus.

  • The change takes effect from the next fight, and the upgraded form keeps the original's place in the casting order.
  • The ability stops with the commander: if the commander falls, it is not cast again in that fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Pit Colossus means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
