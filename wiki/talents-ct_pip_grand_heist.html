Grand Heist is a talent of Pip Quickfingers, offered at player level 10 to the Pickpocket build.

Description

Pickpocket robs two enemies and steals more.

What it changes

Pickpocket becomes Grand Heist.

  • The change takes effect from the next fight.
  • A talent is kept for the rest of the match.

Ability

AbilityTimingDescription
Before the talentPickpocketPassiveAt the start of combat Pip robs a random enemy, which deals 20% less damage and attacks 20 slower, while Pip gains the same. Every enemy Pip kills drops more loot: +15 attack damage and +10 attack speed.
With the talentGrand HeistPassiveAt the start of combat Pip robs two random enemies, which deal 25% less damage and attack 25 slower, while Pip gains 40% damage and 40 attack speed. Kills drop even more loot.

In play. The percentage change to damage applies to attack damage only. Damage from abilities and damage over time are unchanged.

The choice

At each talent level you are shown the options for your build and keep one. Taking Grand Heist means giving up the other option of that level for the rest of the match.
