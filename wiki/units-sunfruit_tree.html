Sunfruit Tree

Sunfruit Tree is a placeable planted on the board by the item Sunfruit Sapling. It is not sold in the shop.

Sunfruit Tree is a placeable: an object that stands in a cell of the board. The unit holding Sunfruit Sapling plants 1 at the start of combat, in the free cell nearest to it. It has 1,000 health, never moves and never attacks. See Summons and placeables.

Ability

Sunfruit Tree has no ability to cast and no mana bar. What it does is listed under its unit effect.

Unit effect

Sunfruit Tree carries an effect of its own, apart from any ability. It is always on. See Triggers and targeting for how such effects fire.

  • Every 2 seconds, this unit restores 30 mana to itself and all allies within 2 cells and heals the same targets for 60 health.

Sunfruit Tree is a placeable. It never moves and never attacks. It acts only through its unit effect, listed above.

Where it comes from

Sunfruit Tree is never offered in the shop. It is planted on the board by an item. See Summons and placeables for how summoned and placed units behave.

  • Item Sunfruit Sapling
    • The unit holding the item plants 1 at the start of combat, in the free cell nearest to it.
A placeable does not keep your side in the fight: when your last fighting unit falls the round is lost and Sunfruit Tree collapses. If you win, it adds nothing to the damage dealt to the loser.

Alliances

Sunfruit Tree carries no alliance. It neither counts towards an alliance step nor gains a members' bonus.
