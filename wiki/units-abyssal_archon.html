Abyssal Archon is a tier 5 (Legendary) caster unit. It costs 5 gold in the shop and carries the Abyssal and Arcanist alliances. It is an ace.

Ability

Hellrain

Rains hellfire on the target, dealing magical damage to it and all enemies within 2 cells, then again as the embers fall.

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.

  • Deals 250 / 375 / 500 magical damage to the targets (values per star).
  • Applies Embers (50 / 75 / 100 magical damage taken per second, -40% movement speed) to the targets for 4 seconds (values per star).

Ace effect

Abyssal Archon is the ace of the Abyssal alliance. Its ace effect is on while it stands on the board and at least one step of that alliance is active. See Aces.

In the game's own words: Ace: the Abyssal Archon deals 100% more damage.

In play, the effect applies to Abyssal Archon itself:

  • +100% attack damage

The damage bonus raises attack damage only. Damage from abilities is not changed. See Damage, armour and resistance.

When your shop rolls a tier 5 unit, each unit of that tier is weighted by the copies of it left in the pool. While that alliance is active for you, the weight of Abyssal Archon in that draw is 15% higher. See The shop.
