Wyrmblood

A race alliance of 3 units with a step at 2.

Heirs of the old wyrms. With two Wyrmblood, each of their spells also breathes fire on the target and every enemy within 1 cell, dealing magical damage and scorching them.

Wyrmblood is a race alliance carried by 3 units. It has one step, reached with 2 different Wyrmblood units on your board. See How alliances count for how units are counted.

Steps

Units neededWhat it grants
2When a Wyrmblood unit casts an ability, it deals 150 / 250 / 400 magical damage to the enemy its ability was cast on (its current target if there is none) and all enemies within 1 cell of it and applies Scorched (alliances) (30 / 50 / 80 magical damage taken per second) to the same targets for 4 seconds (values per star).

How it counts

  • Only units on your board count. Units on the bench do not.
  • Each different unit counts once. Two copies of the same unit count as one, and star level makes no difference.
  • Summoned units never count.

The full rules are on How alliances count.
