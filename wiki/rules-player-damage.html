Player damage and elimination

Losing a round, health damage and final placement.

Every player starts with 100 health. Health never comes back. You lose health only by losing a fight (or by a time out), the amount depends on the round and on what is left standing on the winner's side, and you are out of the match when your health reaches 0.

The formula

When you lose a fight, the damage to your health is the sum of two parts:

Damage = round damage + the damage of every surviving enemy unit

  • Round damage is the round number divided by 10, rounded down.
  • Each surviving unit adds 1 plus its power level divided by 3, rounded down. A unit's power level is its tier, plus 2 for every star above the first (Star upgrades).
  • A surviving commander adds a flat amount instead, whatever its level (table below).
  • Summoned units add nothing, and neither do placed objects such as wards and totems (Summons and placeables).

Only units that are alive when the fight ends count. How much health they have left does not matter, and neither does how many of your own units died. The winner takes no damage.

A board holds at most 11 units besides the commander: one for each of the 10 player levels, and 1 more from Veteran's Charter (Board and bench). So the most that units alone can deal is 11 x 4 = 44.

Draws and time outs

  • Draw. If both sides have no units left, the fight is a draw. That happens when the last units of both sides die at the same moment, and also when the last units of the winning side die within the 1 second that follows the wipe, for example to damage over time, to an attack already in flight or to overtime. So wiping out the enemy is not yet a win: something of yours must still be standing when that delay is over (how a fight ends). Nobody takes damage in a draw. A draw neither extends nor breaks a win or loss streak, pays no victory gold and grants no free reroll.
  • Time out. If both sides still have units when the time limit is reached, both players lose. Each takes the damage that the other side's survivors would deal by the formula above, round damage included. Both win streaks end, both loss streaks grow (if damage was taken), and nobody is paid victory gold. See Time limit and overtime for how the game pushes fights to finish first.
  • A loss with no damage. If the only things left standing on the winner's side are summons, and the round damage is still 0 (before round 10), you lose the fight but no health. Your win streak still ends, but a loss without damage does not add to your loss streak (Gold and income).

Every loss and every time out grants a free reroll of the shop. It is kept until you use it, however many rounds that takes, and you never hold more than one. See The shop.

Elimination

Health is checked once per round, when the round is settled after all fights (see rounds). Every player at 0 health or less is knocked out at that moment. Until then a player on low health plays the whole round normally.

  • All of a knocked out player's units, on the board and on the bench, go back to the shared pool.
  • Their open item, commander and talent offers are dropped, and they can no longer act.
  • They are paid no income for the round they fell in.
  • Their board as it stood is kept for the results screen.
  • They may leave and look for a new match straight away (Matchmaking and lobbies).
  • While at least one person is still alive, the match carries on at its normal speed and a knocked out player may stay and watch.
  • As soon as no person is left alive, so that only bots remain, there is nothing left to watch: the remaining rounds are played out at once, with no timers and no fights shown, and the final results arrive within moments (After the match). In a match of one person and bots, this happens as soon as that person is knocked out.

Final placement

Your placement is fixed when you are knocked out: it is the worst placement still open, which is the number of players who were still alive before you fell. The first player out of 8 finishes 8th, and the last player standing finishes first.

Several players fall in the same round

  • They are ranked by health after the round's damage. Health can go below zero, and the player who went furthest below takes the worst placement.
  • Players who fell exactly as far as each other are separated by seat order, which is fixed when the match starts. Nothing in play can change it.
  • If every remaining player falls in the same round, the match ends there, and the one who finished with the most health is the winner.

The round limit

If more than one player survives round 60, the match ends (the last round). Players who fell in that round are placed first, as above. The survivors are then ranked by health: the most health wins, and equal health is separated by the same seat order.

Forfeits and disconnects

Quit Match

  • Quitting a running match is a forfeit. Your health is set to zero.
  • During preparation you are knocked out at once, with the worst placement still open. If that leaves one player, the match ends on the spot and they win.
  • During combat your board still fights this round's fight, so your opponent gets a real result. You are knocked out with the round's other casualties when the round is settled. If your board lost that fight, the damage is taken from zero, so you place below a player who merely reached zero in the same round.
  • Quitting in the lobby, before the match starts, just gives up your seat (Matchmaking and lobbies).

Disconnecting

  • A disconnect is not a forfeit. Your seat, health, gold and warband are kept, and you can come back (Matchmaking and lobbies).
  • While you are away your board keeps fighting each round with the units on it. Offers you cannot answer are settled for you when combat starts.
  • You do not hold up the other players: an absent player is not waited for when everyone else is ready (readying up).
  • You still take damage and can be knocked out while away.
  • If every person who is still alive has been disconnected for 15 seconds, nobody is left to play, and the rest of the match is played out at once between the bots and the abandoned boards.
  • That wait applies only while a person is still alive. Once every person has been knocked out, the rest of the match is played out at once with no wait, even if knocked out players are still connected (elimination).

Victory

The match ends the moment a round's settlement, or a forfeit, leaves one player alive. That player wins and is placed first. No income is paid for the final round. The results then open for everyone still connected (After the match).
