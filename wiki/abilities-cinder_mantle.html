Cinder Mantle

The ability of Ashen Abbess: grants a shield. It targets itself and all allies within 2 cells.

In game: Shields every ally within 2 cells with a mantle of cinders.

Cinder Mantle is the ability of Ashen Abbess.

What it does

Cast
When mana is full. Casting spends all of it.
Cast point
0.3 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
Itself and all allies within 2 cells.
  • Gives the targets a shield that absorbs 150 / 300 / 600 damage for 10 seconds (values per star).

How the cast works

  • Ashen Abbess needs 100 mana to cast Cinder Mantle. Units gain mana by landing attacks and by taking damage: see Mana and casting.
  • Casting comes first. Whenever the unit is free to act and its mana is full, it casts before it attacks or moves. It cannot start a cast while stunned or silenced, and it does not cast when there is no enemy it can target.
  • The unit holds still for the cast point, then the ability takes effect and its mana drops to zero. Mana is spent only when the cast point ends, so a stun or silence that lands during it cancels the cast without costing mana, and the unit casts again as soon as it is free.
  • Targets are chosen when the ability takes effect, not when the cast starts. It affects every ally within 2 cells of the caster. The caster counts as one of those allies. A diagonal step counts as one cell. See Triggers and targeting.
  • After the ability resolves, anything that fires when the unit casts, such as an item or alliance effect, triggers. See Triggers and targeting.
