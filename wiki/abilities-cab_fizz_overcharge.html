Overcharge is an extra ability of Fizzwick Emberhorn. No build starts with it: it is added by the talent Overcharge.

Description

Fizzwick overcharges a random ally, which gains a large burst of attack speed for 5 seconds.

What it does

Cast
Not cast with mana. It has a cooldown of 8 seconds, counted from the end of each cast, so with the cast point two casts are at least 9.35 seconds apart. The first cast starts 4 seconds into combat. The cooldown keeps running while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot start a cast.
Cast point
1.35 seconds from the start of the cast to its effect.
Targets
A random ally (the commander included).
  • Applies Overcharged to the target for 5 seconds: +40% (levels 1 to 5), then 50 / 65 / 80 / 90 / 105% (levels 6 to 10) attack speed.

When it fires

  • The cooldown restarts when the cast point completes, so casts come 9.35 seconds apart (the cooldown plus the cast point) and the first one lands 5.35 seconds into the fight at the earliest. A fight that runs to the 45 second time limit has room for 5 casts at most.
  • The only thing a cast waits for is an enemy that can be targeted: none begins while no such enemy is left.
  • The cooldown keeps counting down while the commander is stunned, but a stunned or silenced commander cannot begin a cast, and a stun or silence that lands during the cast point cancels it. A cancelled cast does not spend the cooldown, so the commander tries again when able.
  • When several abilities are ready together the commander casts them in a fixed order: build abilities first, in the order the build lists them, then abilities granted by talents.

The rules every commander ability shares are on How commanders work and Mana and casting.

Applying it again

  • Overcharged: it does not stack. A second application on the same unit renews it, and it then lasts for the longer of the time left and the new duration.
