Status effects

Every buff, debuff and disable.

A status effect is a named change that sits on a unit for a while. Warspire has 100 of them: 51 buffs, 34 debuffs and 15 disables. A buff helps the unit that carries it. A debuff harms it. A disable is a debuff that stops the unit from doing something: acting, casting, moving or attacking. An effect marked mixed does some of both, and the game still counts it as the kind shown.

Effects are applied by units, alliances, items, commanders and aces; the Applied by column lists every kind of source an effect has. How status effects work explains durations and stacking, and Stat reference explains each stat an effect can change.
