Ironsight Monocle

Uncommon global item. Counts as one extra unit toward Ironsight, as long as at least one Ironsight unit of yours is on the board.

That is the wording on the item's card. The sections below are worked out from the item itself and say what happens in play.

As an uncommon item (tier 2) it is offered on rounds 8 and 12. See Loot and items for how offers work.

What it does

Global item: it is never given to a unit and works from the moment you own it.
Counts as one extra unit toward Ironsight, as long as at least one Ironsight unit of yours is on the board (how alliances count).
It is no longer offered as loot once you own one copy.

Alliances

While you own it you count as having 1 more unique Ironsight unit. Ironsight has one step, at 1 unit.

Every Ironsight step is already reached by the first Ironsight unit on your board, and the extra count is ignored until that unit is there. This item currently changes nothing.

Who can carry it

No unit carries Ironsight Monocle. It goes to you the moment you pick it and works from then on, whichever units you field. It cannot be equipped on a unit and it does not take a slot in the item inventory.

You can own 1 copy at most.
