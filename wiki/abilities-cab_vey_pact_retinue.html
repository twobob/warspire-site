Pact Retinue is the passive of Vey the Pactbinder. The commander has it in every build, from the moment the commander joins your board.

Description

Vey never fights with her own hands. At the start of combat she calls her retinue, a Pact Archer and a Pact Bruiser, to fight beside her, and she regenerates 20 health per second.

Standing bonus

A standing bonus on the commander alone. It is in place for the whole fight.

  • cannot attack, +20 health regeneration per second

Triggers

  • At the start of combat, the commander summons a Pact Archer at star 1 (levels 1 to 6), then 2 (levels 7 and 8), then 3 (levels 9 and 10) in the nearest free cell and summons a Pact Bruiser at star 1 (levels 1 to 6), then 2 (levels 7 and 8), then 3 (levels 9 and 10) in the nearest free cell.

These values grow with your player level, and the game uses your level at the moment the fight starts. Levels that share every value are one row. Everything else about the ability is the same at every level.

When it fires

  • This is a passive. It is never cast, costs nothing and has no cooldown.
  • Its triggered effects fire on their own when their condition is met.
