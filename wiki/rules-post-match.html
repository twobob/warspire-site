After the match

Standings and post-match statistics.

When the last fight is settled and one player is left, the match is over. Every player still connected is sent the final standings, followed by a full set of statistics for every seat, bots included. How placements are decided is on Player damage and elimination.

Standings

The standings list every seat in order of placement, with the winner first. For each seat they give the player's name, the placement and whether the seat was a bot. The match as a whole records who won and how many rounds were played.

For every player

Placement
Final position, with the winner first.
Knocked out
The round the player fell in. The winner has none.
Final health
Health at the end. It can be below zero for a player who was knocked out; the results screen shows that as zero.
Final level
Player level at the end (Levels and experience).
Final gold
Gold in hand at the end.
Starting gold
The 5 gold everyone begins with, kept so the gold totals add up.
Commander
The commander, the build and every talent taken, if the player had a commander.
Best units
Up to 5 units the player raised to the highest star at any point in the match, highest star first, then highest tier (Star upgrades).
Final board
Every unit on the board at the end or at the moment of elimination: its cell, its star, the item it holds, and which one is the commander.

Match totals

Wins, losses, draws
Fights against other players and ghosts. A time out counts as a loss.
Neutral wins and losses
Fights against neutral waves, counted apart.
Player damage dealt
Health taken from other players by winning (Player damage and elimination). Beating a ghost deals none.
Player damage taken
Health lost over the match.
Combat damage dealt and taken
All damage the player's units dealt and received in fights, summons included (Damage, armour and resistance).
Units bought and sold
Purchases from the shop and sales.
Rerolls
Shop rerolls, free ones included.
Experience bought
Experience points bought with gold (Levels and experience).
Items picked
Items taken from offers (Loot and items).
Gold spent on units, rerolls and experience
Three separate totals.
Gold from income
Round gold, interest, streak gold, victory gold and gold from items (Gold and income).
Gold from sales
Gold received for selling units.
Gold earned
Income plus sales.
Gold spent
Units plus rerolls plus experience.
Rounds played
Rounds the player took part in.
The gold figures reconcile exactly: starting gold + gold from income + gold from sales, less the gold spent on units, rerolls and experience, equals final gold.

Round by round

One entry is kept for every round a player took part in.

Round and kind
The round number and whether it was a fight against a player or a neutral wave.
Opponent
Who the player fought, and whether it was a ghost.
Result
Win, loss, draw or time out.
Player damage
Health the player lost that round and health they took from the opponent.
Combat damage
Damage dealt and received by units in that fight.
Health after
Health once the round was settled.
Gold
Gold when combat began, the income paid afterwards split into victory gold, interest, streak gold, round gold and item gold, and gold after income.
Level and streaks
Player level, win streak and loss streak after the round.
Activity
Units bought and sold, rerolls, experience bought, and gold spent and earned from sales during that round.
Board
The board as it went into the fight: units, stars, items and the commander.
Alliances
Every active alliance, its active step and how many different units counted toward it.

The results screen

The results screen opens when you are knocked out or when the match ends, whichever comes first for you.

  • Title. Victory for the winner, otherwise your placement.
  • Headline. Who won, after how many rounds, with which commander and which leading alliance. While the match is still running it says how many players are still fighting.
  • Standings table. One row per player: placement, portrait, name and commander, health, level, player damage dealt, gold earned, rerolls, the round they went out, and their best units. You and the bots are marked.
  • Detail pane. Tap a player to see their final board with stars and items, their totals (record, combat damage, player damage, gold earned and spent, income and sales, units bought and sold, rerolls, experience bought, items picked, and the round they were knocked out), and a chart of health by round. Each bar is one round, coloured by result, and shows the opponent, the damage both ways, health, level, gold and activity for that round.
  • Share row. Buttons that open a share page with your result filled in, plus Save image and Copy text. Nothing is posted for you.

If you were knocked out while another person is still alive, the totals are marked as so far, players still alive are listed first, and a Keep Watching button returns you to the match as a spectator. The match carries on at its normal speed, and the full statistics arrive when it ends.

If you were the last person alive, so that only bots are left, there is nothing to watch. The remaining rounds are played out at once, with no timers and no fights shown, and the final standings and full statistics arrive within moments (elimination).

Playing again

Continue leaves the results and returns you to the holding screen, where you choose when to look for the next match. You get a seat in a fresh lobby, not a rematch against the same table (Matchmaking and lobbies). You do not have to wait for the match to end: a knocked out player can leave for a new match at once, and their old match carries on without them.

A finished match is kept open for 45 seconds so that everyone can read the results, or until the last player has left, whichever comes first. After that the room is cleared for new players.

Nothing carries over into the next match. Everyone starts again with 5 gold, 100 health and level 1 (How a match works).
